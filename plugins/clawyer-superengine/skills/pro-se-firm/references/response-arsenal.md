# Response Arsenal — every way to fight what's coming at you (multi-front)

> **Jurisdiction note (Clawyer):** this file was written for Mississippi family court. Outside Mississippi, or outside a family case, treat every rule number, case name, deadline, fee and form in it as an example of what to look up, not as your law. Family-law steps in it do not apply to other kinds of case. Confirm your own state's version first and tag anything unconfirmed `CONFIRM-BEFORE-CITING`.

> When something lands — a motion, a proposed order, discovery, a demand, an emergency/ex parte order, a subpoena — the
> firm does **not** pick one response. It lays out **every legitimate way to fight it across all fronts**, marks which
> ones fit, and sequences them into a **coordinated multi-front plan.** This is also the firm's catalog of *what a person
> can file.* Pairs with `responsive-filing.md` (how to answer one motion), `adversarial-combat.md` (posture),
> `problem-to-proposal.md` (offer the court an answer), `proactive-offense.md` (offense), and `courtroom-bulldog.md`
> (hold the line in the room). Research/drafting support — **not legal advice.**
>
> **The one-issue rule still holds:** each *filing* stays a single issue. "Multi-front" means running several single-issue
> moves in parallel and never losing track of one — not blending them into one document.
>
> **This applies to the WHOLE divorce, not just custody.** The eight fronts are issue-agnostic — use the same arsenal
> whether the attack is on **custody/parenting time**, **child support** (incl. a contempt/arrears motion), **alimony**,
> **property / the marital home / equitable distribution / retirement**, or **discovery**. Only the *substantive standard*
> on Front B changes by track (Albright for custody, the § 43-19-101 guidelines for support, Armstrong for alimony,
> Ferguson for property) — every other front works the same. If an incoming motion blends issues, split your responses
> issue-by-issue (one-issue rule) and run them as parallel fronts.

## Step 1 — classify what's incoming (the type sets the available weapons)
Motion to **modify** custody/support · motion for **contempt** · motion for **temporary relief** · a **proposed order /
redline** · **discovery** served on you · a **demand letter** · an **emergency / ex parte** order · a **subpoena**. Name
it first; the fronts below light up differently for each.

> **Mississippi timing trap (verified):** custody/support **modification, enforcement, and contempt** are **Rule 81(d)(2)**
> matters — **triable 7 days after service** by a *special Rule 81 summons* that names a court date; custody/support
> **actions** are **81(d)(1)** — 30 days. `[VERIFIED — MRCP 81(d), official text, 2026-05-23]` A Rule 81 matter usually
> needs **no written answer**, but you **must appear** on the date — and a **defective/late special summons is itself a
> front** (Front A). Find that date first.

## Step 2 — the fronts (the full menu)

### Front A — Procedural / threshold (attack the vehicle, before the merits)
- **Defective service / wrong summons** — e.g., a Rule 81 matter served on an ordinary Rule 4 summons, or wrong/short
  notice. `[VERIFIED — MRCP 81(d)]`
- **Untimely / missed their own deadline.**
- **No jurisdiction / wrong venue / no standing.**
- **Failure to state a claim** — they allege no real material change / no grounds → motion to dismiss. `[VERIFIED — MRCP 12(b)(6)]`
- **Too vague to answer** → motion for a more definite statement. `[VERIFIED — MRCP 12(e)]`
- **Scandalous / improper matter in their filing** → motion to strike. `[VERIFIED — MRCP 12(f)]`
- **Already decided / not ripe** (res judicata / prematurity).

### Front B — Substantive opposition (beat it on the merits)
- **Written response/objection:** they carry the burden and haven't met it; rebut each allegation with proof.
- **Declaration + exhibits that disprove the claim** (declarations beat arguments; the document beats the testimony).
- **The legal standard isn't satisfied — by track:**
  - *Custody:* modification needs a material change adversely affecting the child; supervised visitation needs real
    endangerment (Albright). `[CONFIRM — verify the MS modification + restricted-visitation standards]`
  - *Child support:* a modification needs a **material change in circumstances**; the number itself runs off the
    § 43-19-101 guidelines (14/20/22/24/26%). `[VERIFIED — § 43-19-101]`
  - *Alimony:* rehabilitative/standard-of-living factors (Armstrong) — attack the factors they haven't proven.
  - *Property / marital home / retirement:* equitable distribution under Ferguson — challenge their valuation, their
    separate-vs-marital characterization, and any non-court-standard math (a Zillow estimate, a "net-difference" split).

### Front C — Affirmative counter-moves (go on offense in the same fight)
- **Your own cross-motion / counter-petition** (turn defense into offense; Problem-to-Proposal) — examples by track:
  *custody:* they move to restrict your time → you move to **clarify/expand** it with a specific schedule; *support:* they
  move to raise it → you cross-move for the **guideline-correct figure or a justified deviation**; *property:* they move
  on the house → you counter with your **Ferguson position + a valuation method**; *any:* their overreach is your opening.
- **Motion to strike** improper content. `[VERIFIED — MRCP 12(f)]`
- **Motion in limine** to exclude their weak/inadmissible evidence at the hearing. `[CONFIRM — MS Rules of Evidence basis]`
- **Motion for sanctions / fees** for a frivolous or bad-faith filing — **high bar, never premature, last-resort tone.**
  `[CONFIRM — MRCP 11 signing/sanctions; verify before filing]`
- **Amend your own pleadings** to add a claim/defense. `[VERIFIED — MRCP 15]`

### Front D — Evidence / discovery (expose and lock down what they must prove)
- **Serve discovery** to force them to prove their allegations — interrogatories (≤30, MRCP 33(a)), requests for
  production, **requests for admission (deemed admitted in 30 days, MRCP 36)**, third-party subpoenas. `[VERIFIED]`
- **Motion to compel** if they stonewall (good-faith-conferral certification required). `[VERIFIED — MRCP 37(a)]`
- **Build your own proof:** the daily log, OFW/message pulls, witness declarations, records.

### Front E — Order-integrity (if it's a proposed order or a redline)
- Run the **Order Integrity Reviewer** (does it match the ruling?) **and** the **Gray-Area Auditor** (any exploitable
  vagueness?); **don't sign**; redline to the ruling. (`order-integrity.md`, `ambiguity-auditor.md`)

### Front F — Negotiation (resolve or narrow it on your terms)
- A **counter-proposal / agreed order** that hands the court a reasonable answer and narrows the dispute — run the
  redline-integrity protocol so nothing gets slipped in. (`negotiation-counsel.md`)

### Front G — Record & appeal (protect the long game)
- **Request specific findings** (e.g., on each Albright factor) to box them in; **preserve every objection** for appeal;
  build the record now so a later modification/appeal stays open.

### Front H — Restraint (sometimes the strongest front is not firing)
- **Don't take the bait; don't over-file.** A clean response + calm, documented conduct can beat a pile of motions.
  Scattershot filing reads as harassing and costs credibility — *coordinated* beats *more*. (Bompiani: calm parent wins.)

## Step 3 — build the multi-front battle map
Pick the combination that fits, **sequence it** (what to file first, what to hold in reserve, what depends on a hearing
date), keep each filing single-issue, **calendar every deadline**, and assign who/what produces each. Deliverable:

| Front | The move | What it does for you | Bar / risk | Vehicle (filing) | Timing / sequence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Then hand each chosen move to the right specialist (Drafting Counsel, Discovery, Negotiation Counsel, Order Integrity)
and surface the **single most important first step**.

## Guardrails (every front obeys these)
- **One issue per filing**; multi-front = parallel single-issue moves, never a blended document.
- **No premature contempt, no revenge framing, no crime allegations, no gender arguments** (Pisarra/Bompiani; forbidden
  outputs).
- **Client-protection floor:** never file something that exposes you, concedes a point, or invites sanctions/fees against
  you; every custody move must protect the child and preserve/expand your time.
- **Citation discipline:** verify each rule/vehicle/deadline before filing (use `scripts/fetch_law.py` if a source is
  blocked); tag every cite.
- **Coordinated, not scattershot** — more motions is not more winning. Pick the fronts that actually move the goal.
