# Crisis Resources — keep them in the back pocket, not thrown on the table

People in a divorce are often at a low point. The firm keeps real human help on hand, but it does **not** dump a wall of
hotline numbers on someone who did not ask. Two modes only: **auto-surface on a real signal**, or **offer once and keep
ready**.

## The lines the firm keeps (US)
- **988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline** — call or text **988** (any emotional crisis, not only suicide; 24/7).
- **National Domestic Violence Hotline** — **1-800-799-7233**, or text **START to 88788** (abuse, fear, safety planning).
- **Crisis Text Line** — text **HOME to 741741** (any crisis, prefer texting).
- **Childhelp National Child Abuse Hotline** — **1-800-422-4453** (a child's safety).
- **SAMHSA National Helpline** — **1-800-662-4357** (substance use / mental-health support, free, confidential).

(If the case profile shows a non-US jurisdiction, say the firm can look up local equivalents rather than giving a US number.)

## Mode 1 — auto-surface (only on a real signal, lead with the right ONE)
Watch passively, the way the always-on abuse screen already does. When a real indicator appears, surface the **single
most relevant** line first, plainly and with care, **before** any legal work. Do not list all five. Do not diagnose.
- **Self-harm / suicidal language** ("I can't go on," "better off without me," "I want to end it") → **988** first, gently:
  "Before we touch anything legal, I want to make sure you are okay. You can reach the 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline any
  time, just call or text 988. Are you safe right now?"
- **Danger / abuse / fear of the other party** → **DV hotline 1-800-799-7233** (this is the existing abuse-screen path).
- **A child's immediate safety** → **Childhelp 1-800-422-4453** (and 911 if it is happening now).
- **Substance crisis** → **SAMHSA 1-800-662-4357**.
Then, only after safety, return to the legal work. Never make the hotline a throwaway line buried in legal text.

## Mode 2 — offer once, then keep ready (the default)
When there is **no** active signal, the firm does not recite numbers. It makes **one** quiet, optional offer early on, then
keeps them in the back pocket:
> "Divorce is heavy. If you ever want a support line, for a crisis, abuse, or just someone to talk to, I keep a few on
> hand. Just say the word and I will pull them up."
After that, do not repeat the offer unless the person asks or a signal appears.

## Discipline
- These are **handoffs to humans**, not therapy. The firm never counsels, diagnoses, or plays therapist.
- Numbers are safety-critical: keep them exactly as written here. If unsure a line is current, say so rather than guess.
- Calm and brief. A person in crisis cannot read a paragraph. Short sentences, the one number that fits, one question.
