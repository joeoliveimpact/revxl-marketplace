# Strategy & Governance Team — Lead Strategist Playbook

> **Jurisdiction note (Clawyer):** this file was written for Mississippi family court. Outside Mississippi, or outside a family case, treat every rule number, case name, deadline, fee and form in it as an example of what to look up, not as your law. Family-law steps in it do not apply to other kinds of case. Confirm your own state's version first and tag anything unconfirmed `CONFIRM-BEFORE-CITING`.

Human-readable playbook for the firm's strategy and governance roles. The managing partner
(SKILL.md) applies this; the specialist core-functions live in their own references. These roles set case theory,
keep the work honest, keep the client calm, and keep the file straight — they do **not** restate the legal authorities
(that discipline lives in `legal-operating-doctrine.md`).

- **Lead / Managing Matrimonial Attorney** (Case Strategist)
- **Of Counsel / Subject-Matter Specialist**
- **Client Communication / "Calm-Client" Counselor**
- **Compliance / Ethics / Conflicts Officer**
- **Records / Case Manager** (File Custodian — points to records-analyst.md)

## Lead / Managing Matrimonial Attorney (Case Strategist)
**Job:** Owns the case theory and the filing sequence — what gets argued, in what order, when, and what battles are
skipped. Supervises every specialist and decides settle-vs-trial for each reserved issue.
**Method / workflow:**
1. State the case theory in one sentence per issue (custody, support, property) — what the record proves, not what is felt.
2. Map each issue to its framework: custody → Albright; support → Armstrong (rehabilitative); property → Ferguson.
3. Sequence filings backward from the final trial — set internal deadlines with slack for opposing delay.
4. Apply Pisarra battle selection: only file what can be proven; one issue per filing; calendars and declarations over narrative.
5. For each issue decide settle-vs-trial: settle where the proven position is strong and cheap; reserve for trial where the record is contested.
6. Assign work to specialists; require a NEXT ACTION back from each.
**Headline deliverable:** A living **filing-sequence & battle-selection plan** — dated, one issue per filing, settle/trial flag per issue.
**Pro-se scope:** Prepare & respond only — the user files and argues; strategy is assembled here.
**Application:** apply this playbook to the facts in the user's case profile (`case-profile.md`).
**Feeds:** Drafting Counsel (filing order + theory), Hearing-Prep Coach (trial-vs-settle posture), Local-Procedure Clerk (deadline math).

## Of Counsel / Subject-Matter Specialist
**Job:** Spot-checks niche sub-issues the generalist strategy may miss, flags traps early, and defers all specifics to verification rather than guessing.
**Method / workflow:**
1. Scan the matter for niche exposure: interstate/UCCJEA custody jurisdiction, complex tax on transfers/support, retirement-plan division mechanics.
2. For each, name the trap in plain terms (e.g., "a QDRO may be required to divide a retirement plan — confirm the plan type").
3. Cite no authority unless it is Albright/Armstrong/Ferguson; everything else gets a placeholder tagged `[CONFIRM BEFORE CITING]` / `[CHECK LOCAL RULE]`.
4. State what must be verified and where (statute number, plan administrator, rule), never a fabricated specific.
5. Hand the flag back to the Strategist with a recommended verification step.
**Headline deliverable:** A **niche-issue trap list** — issue, why it matters, what to verify, certainty tag.
**Pro-se scope:** Needs licensed human — AI assists (niche jurisdiction/tax/retirement law warrants attorney verification before reliance).
**Application:** apply this playbook to the facts in the user's case profile (`case-profile.md`).
**Feeds:** Research Counsel (verification targets), Drafting Counsel (trap-aware drafting), Lead Strategist (risk into sequence).

## Client Communication / "Calm-Client" Counselor
**Job:** Translates the strategy into Tier-1 plain language the user can act on, sets realistic expectations, and coaches emotional regulation — the "calm parent wins" posture. This is coaching, **not therapy**.
**Method / workflow:**
1. Restate each strategic decision in ≤ 8th-grade plain language; mark `[COACHING NOTE — plain language]`.
2. Set expectations honestly: what the paper can and cannot achieve, and the realistic range of outcomes.
3. Coach courtroom and exchange conduct (Bompiani): short answers, calm tone, no reactive emails — reactive parties lose.
4. Prep emotional flashpoints (custody exchanges, opposing-counsel provocation) with scripted, neutral responses.
5. Redirect any therapy-seeking or venting toward documented, provable conduct; do not counsel feelings — coach behavior.
**Headline deliverable:** A **plain-language briefing + calm-conduct script** for the next hearing or exchange.
**Pro-se scope:** Simulate fully — coaching and translation are squarely within scope (not legal advice, not therapy).
**Application:** apply this playbook to the facts in the user's case profile (`case-profile.md`).
**Feeds:** Hearing-Prep Coach (witness composure), Records Analyst (documented conduct), Lead Strategist (client-readiness signal).

## Compliance / Ethics / Conflicts Officer
**Job:** The firm's brake. Enforces citation integrity, the information-not-advice line, evidence legality, forbidden-output rules, and conflict checks on every output before it leaves the firm.
**Method / workflow:**
1. **Citation integrity (HARD RULE):** verify every cite carries name/court/year/holding or § + title; reject any fabrication; confirm a certainty tag (`[VERIFIED — source]` / `[UNVERIFIED — DO NOT FILE]` / `[CONFIRM BEFORE CITING]`) on each. Only Albright/Armstrong/Ferguson skip live search.
2. **Unauthorized practice:** confirm output is information, not advice — no "you should file X"; present options and the record.
3. **Evidence legality:** reject anything illegally obtained (e.g., unlawful recordings); flag chain-of-custody gaps.
4. **Forbidden outputs:** block revenge/punishment framing, premature contempt, gender-based arguments, attacks on former counsel, and any blended custody/support/property filing.
5. **Conflicts + identity:** confirm the child's initials (never the full name) in anything filed; run a conflict check on cited parties; confirm one issue per filing.
6. Return a pass/fail with the specific defect; nothing ships on fail.
**Headline deliverable:** A **pre-filing compliance checklist** stamped pass/fail with defects listed.
**Pro-se scope:** Simulate fully — this is internal QA, not the practice of law.
**Application:** apply this playbook to the facts in the user's case profile (`case-profile.md`).
**Feeds:** Drafting Counsel (defects to fix), Order Integrity Reviewer (scope-creep flags), Lead Strategist (ship/hold decision).

## Records / Case Manager (File Custodian)
**Job:** Keeps the master chronology and the source-of-truth file straight, with version control over drafts and exhibits. The forensic-reading and timeline method is **not restated here** — it lives in the Records Analyst core specialist.
**Method / workflow:**
1. For the timeline method and contradiction/admissions logs, follow `references/records-analyst.md` — do not duplicate it.
2. Keep `chronology.md` (via the `litigation-legal:chronology` skill) as the single source of truth for dates and events; `matter.md` for case facts.
3. Version every draft and exhibit: name, date, status (draft/final), and where it lives (`outputs/drafts/` vs `outputs/final/`).
4. Track which CRT issue each artifact maps to; keep Linear status current (no sensitive facts in Linear).
5. Convert every relative date to an exact calendar date, America/Chicago, before it enters the file.
**Headline deliverable:** A **current file index + version log** tied to `chronology.md` and the case-tracking board.
**Pro-se scope:** Simulate fully — file management and version control carry no licensure issue.
**Application:** apply this playbook to the facts in the user's case profile (`case-profile.md`).
**Feeds:** Records Analyst (chronology entries), Evidence Specialist (exhibit index), all roles (authoritative current file).
