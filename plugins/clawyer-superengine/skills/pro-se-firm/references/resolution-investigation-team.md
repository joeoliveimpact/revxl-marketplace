# Resolution, Orders & Investigation Team — Role Playbooks

> **Jurisdiction note (Clawyer):** this file was written for Mississippi family court. Outside Mississippi, or outside a family case, treat every rule number, case name, deadline, fee and form in it as an example of what to look up, not as your law. Family-law steps in it do not apply to other kinds of case. Confirm your own state's version first and tag anything unconfirmed `CONFIRM-BEFORE-CITING`.

The firm's deal-and-decree desk plus its lawful-investigation coordinators. These are **method/workflow playbooks** — how each
role works, what it produces, and which core specialist consumes the output. They are **not** legal-authority docs: only
Albright, Armstrong, and Ferguson may be named outright. Every other case, statute, rule, deadline, fee, or local
practice is a placeholder tagged `[CONFIRM BEFORE CITING]` or `[CHECK LOCAL RULE]` until verified per the citation
doctrine. Doctrine binds everything here: the child by initials only in anything filed; one issue per filing; no gender-based arguments;
support framed rehabilitatively (Armstrong); anti-AI tone in any drafted prose; contempt is a last resort, never premature.

## Settlement / Mediation / Negotiation Specialist
> **Front-line negotiation now has its own specialist:** for running a live deal and the back-and-forth redline defense
> (scope-lock, full-document diff every round, change ledger to catch sneak-edits), use **`references/negotiation-counsel.md`**.
> This bench entry is the underlying playbook it draws on.

**Job:** Build and run the user's negotiating position — BATNA, opening/target/floor, term sheets — and make sure every
proposal exchanged is documented and tied to the child's benefit, not to grievance.
**Method / workflow:**
1. Define the BATNA: what happens at the final trial if no deal — realistic range on each reserved issue, not a hope.
2. Set three numbers per issue (custody time, support, property): **opening**, **target**, **floor** — floor justified by the BATNA.
3. Keep **one issue per term sheet line**; never trade custody against property in a single move that blends them.
4. Attach a **child-benefit statement** to every proposal: how this term serves the child's stability and reduces future conflict.
5. Frame any support ask as temporary, structured, outcome-based (Armstrong) — never need-based or unfairness-by-sex.
6. Log every offer made and rejected — date (America/Chicago), exact terms, who proposed, who declined, on what stated basis.
7. Pressure-test each incoming term for hidden cost (e.g., a "waiver" that concedes nothing).
**Headline deliverable:** A negotiation packet — BATNA memo, opening/target/floor grid, drafted term sheet, and an offer/rejection log.
**Pro-se scope:** Simulate fully.
**Application:** apply this playbook to the facts in the user's case profile (`case-profile.md`).
**Feeds:** Drafting Counsel (term sheet → conforming order); Records Analyst (offer log → chronology).

## Mediator Interaction
**Job:** Prepare the user to use court-ordered mediation effectively. The mediator is a **neutral** — this role prepares
for and responds to that process; it never simulates being the mediator or predicts the neutral's call as fact.
**Method / workflow:**
1. Confirm whether mediation is ordered or expected and on what terms `[CHECK LOCAL RULE]`; do not assume a requirement.
2. Build a **one-page issue map** for the session — reserved issues, the user's target/floor per issue, and the child-benefit line.
3. Scenario-model likely moves: what opposing may open with, where they'll push, what a neutral may suggest as compromise.
4. Pre-draft the user's calm, short responses (Bompiani: short answers win; reactive parties lose) to each scenario.
5. Set walk-away triggers tied to the BATNA so a "split the difference" suggestion doesn't drift below the floor.
6. Prepare to convert any agreement reached into a written memorandum the same day — hand off to the Order Drafter.
**Headline deliverable:** A mediation prep brief — issue map, scenario tree, response scripts, and walk-away triggers.
**Pro-se scope:** Prepare & respond only (mediator is a court-appointed neutral — never simulate the neutral).
**Application:** apply this playbook to the facts in the user's case profile (`case-profile.md`).
**Feeds:** Hearing-Prep Coach (response scripts, calm-delivery rehearsal); Drafting Counsel (any mediated memo → decree).

## Order & Judgment Drafter / Conforming-Order Specialist
**Job:** Reduce a ruling or settlement to a precise, enforceable final order/decree — close every ambiguity so the
written order cannot later be read against the user.
**Method / workflow:**
1. Start from the exact source of authority — the oral ruling transcript or the signed term sheet — and quote it.
2. Draft each operative term as a **specific, enforceable command**: who does what, by when (exact calendar dates, Central), how measured.
3. Replace every undefined term with a defined one — e.g., "net difference" gets a written formula; a value gets a named valuation method and date. *(To audit an **existing** order for vague terms and tighten the unfavorable ones, use `references/ambiguity-auditor.md`.)*
4. Use a clean MS chancery decree structure `[CHECK LOCAL RULE for required recitals/form]`; child by initials only; one subject matter, not a blended order.
5. Build a **ruling-to-order map**: each clause traced to the line of the ruling/settlement it implements — nothing added.
6. Run anti-AI scrubber on prose; precise Tier-2 legal language, no filler.
7. Hand the finished draft to the order-integrity reference for the audit step — this role drafts the conforming order; the **Order Integrity Reviewer** audits a proposed order against the ruling (do not duplicate that audit here).
**Headline deliverable:** A drafted conforming final order/decree with a clause-by-clause ruling-to-order map.
**Pro-se scope:** Simulate fully.
**Application:** apply this playbook to the facts in the user's case profile (`case-profile.md`).
**Feeds:** Order Integrity Reviewer (drafted order → audit vs ruling); Drafting Counsel (decree language for filing).

## Appellate / Post-Judgment Specialist
**Job:** Assess whether an adverse ruling is worth appealing or modifying, and protect the record so the option stays open.
**Method / workflow:**
1. Identify the precise ruling and its entry date; calculate the appeal/post-trial-motion window `[CHECK LOCAL RULE]` `[CONFIRM BEFORE CITING the governing rule]` — do not state a deadline you have not verified.
2. Triage viability: was there a clear legal/factual error preserved on the record, or only an unfavorable discretionary call? Be honest — most discretionary calls don't reverse.
3. Confirm **preservation**: was the issue raised below and is it in the record? An unpreserved issue usually can't be appealed.
4. For changed-circumstances modification, frame the petition around a **material change affecting the child** — one issue, evidence-backed, not re-litigation of the divorce.
5. Keep support-modification asks within the Armstrong rehabilitative frame (milestones, defined end).
6. Output a go/no-go memo with the verified deadline, the preserved issues, and the realistic odds.
**Headline deliverable:** An appeal-viability / modification memo — deadlines (verified), preserved issues, go/no-go, draft petition skeleton.
**Pro-se scope:** Prepare & respond only — appellate practice has hard, unforgiving deadlines; Needs licensed human for the actual appeal — AI assists with assessment and record preservation.
**Application:** apply this playbook to the facts in the user's case profile (`case-profile.md`).
**Feeds:** Research Counsel (verify the controlling appeal/modification rule + deadline); Records Analyst (preservation gaps → chronology).

## Enforcement / Contempt Specialist
**Job:** Enforce an **existing** order when the other side violates it — building the documented record of the violation first.
Contempt is a **last resort, never premature**; it is on the never-override list and is pursued only after a clear,
documented violation of an order already in effect.
**Method / workflow:**
1. **Gate check (mandatory):** Is there an existing, entered order with a specific, enforceable term that was violated? If no clear order or no clear violation — **stop**; no contempt. Premature contempt is a forbidden output.
2. Document the violation precisely: the order's exact term, what happened, dates (Central), and proof for each instance.
3. Try the lesser remedy first where appropriate — written notice / meet-and-confer / request to comply — and log it.
4. Maintain running logs: **arrears log** (amount owed, due date, paid/unpaid, running balance) and **interference log** (each missed/blocked exchange, exact term breached, evidence).
5. Keep it one issue — a support-arrears matter and a parenting-time-interference matter are separate filings.
6. Strip emotion and any punishment/revenge framing; the filing shows the order, the breach, the proof — nothing more.
7. Only after the record is clean does enforcement escalate; the decision to file is flagged for the user, not auto-taken.
**Headline deliverable:** A violation-of-order package — order term quoted, dated proof, arrears/interference logs, escalation ladder (last step = contempt).
**Pro-se scope:** Simulate fully (documentation + drafting); escalation to a contempt filing is a deliberate, gated decision.
**Application:** apply this playbook to the facts in the user's case profile (`case-profile.md`).
**Feeds:** Drafting Counsel (violation facts → motion, if and only if gated); Records Analyst (logs → chronology); Order Integrity Reviewer (confirm the order term being enforced matches what was actually entered).

## Private Investigator Coordination
**Job:** Plan a **lawful** investigation and organize its intake — define questions, scope a licensed PI's tasks, and keep
everything admissible. This role coordinates a licensed human; it never performs surveillance or investigation itself.
**Method / workflow:**
1. Define the **specific factual question** tied to a contested issue (e.g., a residence or employment fact relevant to a reserved issue) — no fishing.
2. Scope the licensed PI's task in writing and confirm licensure `[CHECK LOCAL RULE on PI licensing/admissibility]`.
3. **Legality guardrails (hard):** lawful observation of public conduct only — **no** illegal interception of communications, **no** trespass, **no** unauthorized account access, **no** GPS/tracking that the law forbids. If a tactic might be illegal, it does not get tasked.
4. Require a documented chain of custody and a dated, factual report from the PI so the product can be authenticated later.
5. Run anything obtained through the Bompiani 5-step Evaluator before treating it as usable.
6. Organize intake into the evidence index; flag what still needs a sponsoring witness or foundation.
**Headline deliverable:** A PI scope-and-intake plan — the factual question, lawful task list, legality guardrails, and an admissibility/chain-of-custody checklist.
**Pro-se scope:** Needs licensed human — AI assists (plan, scope, and keep admissible; never investigate).
**Application:** apply this playbook to the facts in the user's case profile (`case-profile.md`).
**Feeds:** Evidence Specialist (admissible product → exhibit index); Records Analyst (new dated facts → chronology).

## Digital Forensics Coordination
**Job:** Identify and **preserve** digital evidence the user already has lawful access to, and prepare it for a licensed
examiner — without spoliation and without any illegal access. This role coordinates; it does not perform forensic acquisition.
**Method / workflow:**
1. Inventory the digital sources the user **lawfully** controls or is a party to (their own devices, co-parenting app logs, texts/emails they sent or received).
2. **Preserve first, no spoliation:** do not delete, edit, or "clean up" anything potentially relevant; capture originals with metadata intact; keep a copy untouched.
3. **Legality guardrails (hard):** **no** accessing the other party's accounts, devices, or passwords; **no** intercepting communications the user is not a party to; **no** circumventing security. Illegal collection is inadmissible and dangerous — it does not get done.
4. For anything beyond simple preservation (imaging a device, authenticating metadata), scope a **licensed digital-forensics examiner** `[CHECK LOCAL RULE / [CONFIRM BEFORE CITING] any controlling authentication rule]`.
5. Maintain chain of custody and a preservation log (what, when, how captured, by whom).
6. Run preserved items through the Bompiani 5-step Evaluator; flag foundation/authentication needs.
**Headline deliverable:** A digital-evidence preservation plan — lawful-source inventory, preservation/chain-of-custody log, examiner-handoff scope, and legality guardrails.
**Pro-se scope:** Needs licensed human — AI assists (identify, preserve, keep admissible; examiner does acquisition/authentication).
**Application:** apply this playbook to the facts in the user's case profile (`case-profile.md`).
**Feeds:** Evidence Specialist (preserved, authenticated items → exhibit index); Records Analyst (dated digital facts → chronology).
