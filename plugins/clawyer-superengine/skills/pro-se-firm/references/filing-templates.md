1.  RESIDENCE. At least one party has been a bona fide resident of Mississippi for more than six (6)

    child(ren) and all property rights, attached, and ask the Court to find it adequate and sufficient

incorporating the attached agreement.

[DATES + BOTH SIGNATURES + ADDRESSES — MRCP 11]

## 8. UCCR 8.05 — Financial Statement  *(mandatory where money/children at issue)*
```
[CAPTION — Title: "RULE 8.05 FINANCIAL DECLARATION OF {{PARTY}}"]

I.  GENERAL: name, address, DOB, occupation, employer; name(s) + DOB of minor child(ren) [INITIALS only
    in the public filing — confirm the court's redaction practice].
II. GROSS MONTHLY INCOME: wages/salary (incl. commissions, bonuses, overtime), self-employment,
    pension/retirement, Social Security, investment, other — itemized.
III. DEDUCTIONS: taxes, FICA, mandatory retirement, insurance, pre-existing support — itemized.
    → NET MONTHLY INCOME.
IV. MONTHLY EXPENSES: housing, utilities, food, transportation, child-related, insurance, debt, other.
V.  ASSETS: real property, vehicles, bank/investment, retirement, personal property — with values.
VI. LIABILITIES: each debt, creditor, balance, monthly payment.
ATTACH: most recent income tax return [+ recent pay stubs if required locally].

[DATE + SIGNATURE]   {{Sworn/notarized — confirm.}}
```
*Required by **Uniform Chancery Court Rule 8.05.** `[VERIFIED — official Uniform Chancery Court Rules PDF, Rule 8.05, rev. Apr 18 2024, read 2026-05-23].` A substantially false 8.05 is

## 9. Child-Support Guideline Worksheet  *(Miss. Code § 43-19-101)*
```
A. Paying parent's gross income from all sources (monthly):            $ ______
B. Statutory deductions (taxes, FICA, mandatory retirement,
   pre-existing court-ordered support):                                $ ______
C. ADJUSTED GROSS INCOME (A − B):                                      $ ______
D. Number of children due support: ____   →   Guideline %: ____
      1=14%   2=20%   3=22%   4=24%   5+=26%
E. PRESUMPTIVE MONTHLY SUPPORT (C × %):                                $ ______

If AGI < $10,000 or > $100,000/yr → court must make a WRITTEN FINDING on reasonableness.
Guideline = REBUTTABLE PRESUMPTION; list any factor to rebut: {{...}}
```
*Percentages `[VERIFIED — FindLaw published MS Code § 43-19-101, read verbatim 2026-05-23: table exact].` Guidelines apply
unless the court makes a written finding they'd be unjust/inappropriate (§43-19-101(2)); the >$100k / <$10k written-finding
rule is §43-19-101(4) (verbatim).*

## 14. Rule 81(d) Special Summons  *(modification, contempt, paternity, grandparent, guardianship, protective order — NOT the Rule 4 summons)*
> **Why this is its own form.** Post-judgment and many separate actions are **Rule 81(d)** matters. They do **not** use
> the 30-day Rule 4 answer summons (#6); they use a **special summons that sets a specific date, time, and place to
> appear.** Modification & contempt are triable on **7 days'** notice (81(d)(2)); custody/support/legitimation/paternity
> *actions* use **30 days** (81(d)(1)). A defective/omitted special summons is a real defect.
> `[VERIFIED — MRCP 81(d) read s4; re-confirm the exact day-counts + the official Rule 81 summons form before filing]`
```
[CAPTION — Title: "RULE 81 SUMMONS"]

THE STATE OF MISSISSIPPI
TO: {{RESPONDENT NAME}}, {{address}}

You have been made a Respondent in the {{Petition to Modify / Petition for Citation of Contempt / Petition to
Establish Paternity / etc.}} filed by {{PETITIONER}} seeking {{one-line statement of the relief sought}}.

You are summoned to appear and defend at a hearing on this matter at {{TIME}} on the ___ day of ______, ____,
in the Chancery Courtroom of {{COUNTY}} County, {{address of courthouse}}, and in case of your failure to appear
and defend, a judgment may be entered against you for the relief demanded.

You are NOT required to file an answer or other pleading, but you may do so if you desire.
ISSUED this ___ day of ______, ____.
                                   {{CLERK}}, Chancery Clerk   By: ____________________ D.C.
```
*The appearance date must satisfy the 81(d) notice period for the action type. **Confirm the official Rule 81 summons
language with the clerk** — many districts have a required form. `[CONFIRM — official MRCP 81 form + day-count]`*

## 15. Rule 81 Verified Petition (shell)  *(the workhorse for paternity, grandparent visitation, name change, guardianship — fill the action-specific allegations)*
```
[CAPTION — Title: e.g., "PETITION TO ESTABLISH PATERNITY" / "PETITION FOR GRANDPARENT VISITATION" /
 "PETITION FOR CHANGE OF NAME" / "PETITION FOR APPOINTMENT OF GUARDIAN"]

COMES NOW the Petitioner, {{NAME}}, and respectfully petitions the Court, and would show:

1.  PARTIES & STANDING. {{Who the petitioner is and the legal relationship giving the right to file.}}
2.  JURISDICTION & VENUE. This Court has jurisdiction; venue is proper in {{COUNTY}} County because {{basis}}.
3.  THE CHILD (if any). {{[child INITIALS], a minor, DOB __, residing with __}}.  [initials only]
4.  FACTS / GROUNDS. {{plain, dated, provable facts that satisfy the statute's elements — see insert below}}.
5.  STATUTORY BASIS. {{cite the verified statute for this action}}.
6.  RELIEF REQUESTED. Petitioner asks the Court to {{the single, specific relief}}.  [ONE issue]

                                         Respectfully submitted, ____________________ {{NAME}}, Pro Se
VERIFICATION: I, {{NAME}}, declare under penalty of perjury that the foregoing is true and correct. ____  Date ____
```
**Action-specific allegation inserts (paragraph 4 + 5):**
- **Paternity** → allege the relationship of the parties, the child's birth out of wedlock, and request adjudication
  (and genetic testing if contested). **§ 93-9-9** (mother/father/child/public authority may petition).
  `[VERIFIED — §93-9-9]`
- **Grandparent visitation** → allege the qualifying trigger (custody decree to one parent / TPR / death of a parent →
  §93-16-3(1)) **or** a **viable relationship + unreasonable denial + best interest** (§93-16-3(2)). `[VERIFIED — §93-16-3]`
- **Name change** → allege residence in the county and the requested name; for a **minor**, allege best interest **and
  that the other parent is given notice**. **§ 93-17-1**. `[VERIFIED — §93-17-1]`
- **Guardianship of a minor** → allege the minor has no guardian, appointment is in the best interest, and the route:
  **parental consent / prior TPR / clear-and-convincing** inability. **§ 93-20-201**. `[VERIFIED — §93-20-201]`
> Serve with the **Rule 81 Special Summons (#14)**, not the Rule 4 summons.

## 17. Petition for Citation of Contempt  *(enforce an existing order)*
```
[CAPTION — Title: "PETITION FOR CITATION OF CONTEMPT"]

COMES NOW the Petitioner, {{NAME}}, and would show:
1.  THE ORDER. On {{DATE}} this Court ordered {{the clear, specific provision}} (attached as Exhibit A).
2.  WILLFUL NON-COMPLIANCE. Respondent has willfully failed to comply, as follows: {{dated specifics —
    e.g., missed each support payment since __, total arrears $__ / denied visitation on these dates: __}}.
3.  RELIEF. Petitioner asks the Court to hold Respondent in civil contempt, order compliance, reduce arrears
    to a judgment, and award {{attorney's fees / costs}}.
```
*Civil contempt = coercive (compliance). Movant shows a clear order + non-compliance; burden then shifts to Respondent
to prove **inability** to comply. `[CONFIRM BEFORE CITING — MS civil-contempt burden-shifting case]` ONE issue — don't
bundle with a modification. Serve with Rule 81 Special Summons (#14).*

## 18. Domestic-Abuse Protection Order petition  *(§ 93-21 — often a court-provided form)*
> **Most courts provide a fill-in petition for a domestic-abuse protection order, and there is no filing cost to the
> petitioner (§ 93-21-7(3)).** Use the court's official form if one exists; if drafting, include: the parties; the
> relationship; the **abuse as defined by § 93-21-3** (physical harm / attempt or threat of harm / sexual offense /
> stalking) with **dated, specific incidents**; whether **emergency/ex parte** relief is needed (immediate danger); and
> the **relief requested** from the § 93-21-15 menu (no-contact, stay-away, exclusive possession, temporary custody/
> support, etc.). `[VERIFIED — §93-21-7, §93-21-15; confirm the court's official petition form first]`
> **Safety first:** 911 / DV hotline 1-800-799-7233 if there is danger now. Financial control **alone** does not qualify
> (§ 93-21-3) — route that to `references/abuse-coercive-control.md`.
