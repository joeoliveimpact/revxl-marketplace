## Testimony & courtroom conduct  → Hearing-Prep Coach / `courtroom-scripts.md`
- **Self-introduction script.** Open testimony with a fixed sequence: name; where you live, how long, with whom; work + schedule; then the child(ren) — ages, school, grade, activities, your participation. Credibility before substance.
- **Issue-grouped, positive-first direct.** Give direct in self-contained blocks per issue (e.g., home environment: bedrooms, yard, neighborhood). Keep your direct positive about you; save criticism of the other side for *their* cross.
- **Take the sting out.** Volunteer your own specific, dated bad fact on direct — with what you did to remediate it — before the other side springs it on cross, so the cross-question lands flat.
- **Cross = controlled admissions + silence.** Build cross as short leading statements ending in one "kill" question; never ask "why"; after a damaging admission, stop talking. If the witness dodges: "I'm asking yes or no… Your Honor, I'd ask the Court to direct the witness to answer," then move on.
- **Counsel-table rebuttal log.** During the hearing keep a 3-column notepad: (a) statements you can rebut, (b) statements that are false, (c) follow-up points. Otherwise stay quiet and listen.
- **Improper-scope / due-process objection.** If a hearing expands past your notice: "I was noticed for X; I have not received notice that Y is set today; I respectfully ask the Court to confirm today's scope." `[CHECK LOCAL RULE on notice]`

## Communication / messages  → Communication Command / `communication-command.md`
- **Don't litigate in messages.** Answer a neutral logistical question minimally; don't preemptively attach the order + calendar. Cite the order only once a real dispute exists.
- **Bait recognition + don't-admit-a-gap.** Spot embedded bait (finance jabs, leading "what are your plans?"); answer only the child-centered question. Never put a parenting/planning gap in writing ("I have no plans") — it becomes custody/GAL material.
- **Observed-not-inferred.** When documenting interference you can't fully prove, state only what was directly observed (who did what, when), cite the order provision, make one ask. No speculation about motive.
- **Opposing-counsel correspondence triage.** Reply only to neutralize an adverse paper-trail framing; one line; never preview exhibits/strategy; route substantive demands through the court mechanism, not email.
- **Social-media blackout (4 don'ts) while the case is open.** Don't respond to online negativity; don't trash the other side/family; don't air the case; don't post anything inconsistent with your claims. Respond to attacks by delete/block or a neutral positive post.

## Evidence & forensic  → Evidence Specialist / Records Analyst / Forensic Accountant
- **Sustainability test.** Before any exhibit or cross point goes in: "what's the worst single follow-up, and does it still stand?" Drop anything that backfires.
- **Multi-version sworn-document comparison.** Lay the same disclosure across every filing year side by side; two consistent figures + one outlier is a cleaner credibility story than a two-point jump. Flag values that legally can't move (pension accrues, federal pay doesn't drop, mortgage balance only falls) as the strongest contradictions.
- **Sworn-form vs property-form asymmetry.** Compare the oath-signed financial statement against the asset/distribution worksheet; an asset on one but blank on the sworn form is a high-value finding.
- **Value, don't claim.** Inventory and value EVERY marital asset, even ones you don't want (you're entitled to half the *value*); flag concealed/under-disclosed assets. (Ferguson frame.)
- **Provisional-finding rule.** Tag any finding drawn from partial/garbled OCR as provisional; re-run on clean documents before it enters a filing. Process large doc sets in small batches; for "PDFs" that are really zipped scans, extract embedded text/images first; flag image-only files.
- **Contemporaneous capture ("recording artist").** Preserve evidence as events happen (logs, saved messages). ⚠️ **Audio recording: `[CHECK LOCAL RULE — one-party vs all-party consent]` before capturing or offering any recording — getting this wrong can be a crime.**
- **Child's own writings.** A child's texts/emails to the parent may be admissible with proper foundation and can show favorable contact or coaching — a way to surface the child's voice without putting the child on the stand. `[VERIFY MS foundation/authentication + any child-preference rule]`

## Custody strategy / alienation-as-evidence  → Custody team / `custody-team.md`
- **Information-flow / gatekeeping inventory.** Document specific, dated gatekeeping conduct (screening calls, withholding school/medical info, then blaming you for "not knowing") as a **pattern of fact**. Never label the person — conduct and dates only.
- **Respond to an unsubstantiated safety allegation.** Counter a "child is unsafe with you" claim with an affirmative documentary record (medical, school, routine, witnesses), not emotional denial. Frame as evidence + the child's needs — **never "by sex."**
- **Extended-family contact.** Grandparents/extended family can request and document one-on-one time to counter one-sided information; if refused, obstruction becomes visible. `[VERIFY MS third-party/grandparent visitation standing before filing]`
- **GAL report rebuttal.** Audit the report's source-balance (whose input, who got record access, was the subject substantively interviewed); separate documented findings (address head-on) from characterizations (rebut); reframe a real adverse finding (acknowledge + responsive steps already taken) rather than deny; raise a procedural defect as a request to see the full file, not an accusation.
- **Read the bench.** Before a hearing, learn the assigned judge's docket tendencies/disposition and tailor demeanor and framing (calm, child-focused, settlement-ready if the judge pushes settlement). Conduct/disposition only — never bias/political/gender speculation.

## Procedure & drafting  → Local-Procedure Clerk / Drafting Counsel
- **Confirm the docket yourself.** Independently call the court administrator/clerk to confirm what's set; don't rely on opposing counsel for scope; don't reply-all to court staff (don't telegraph your read). `[CHECK LOCAL RULE]`
- **Dual-track hearing prep.** When scope is unconfirmed, prepare both the narrow and full versions, with a tabbed "use-only-if" section so the wrong material can't be deployed in the wrong proceeding.
- **Digital parenting-plan clauses.** Pre-specify screen-time limits in both homes, who may post the child's photos + takedown rules, parental-controls/allowed-apps responsibility, and device-free times — to pre-empt repeat litigation. `[VERIFY MS parenting-plan content requirements]`
- **Self-created-emergency / unclean-hands timeline.** When a party creates a condition then seeks emergency relief premised on it, lay the timeline as paper (stopped paying → filed re: nonpayment; changed the schedule → asked the court to ratify it). Strictly an **equitable defense + timeline-of-record — never revenge or a contempt push.**
- **Citation triage for passed-along "internet law."** Vet every shared cite for existence, correct holding, and jurisdiction; out-of-jurisdiction/federal-circuit cites are not controlling in state chancery; reject misattributed cases. State controlling authority beats out-of-jurisdiction constitutional cites.

## Case management & deadlines  → Lead Strategist / Docketing / Docket Watcher
- **Track all fronts at once.** Keep one live board of every open front (proposed-order fight, financial relief, post-ruling motions, discovery, trial), each with its next deadline and dependencies. (See doctrine "Multi-front case management.")
- **Trigger-clock deadlines.** When a deadline depends on an event (order entry, service, ruling), compute and monitor it *from the event date*, and file early — missing a triggered deadline can downgrade the remedy. `[CHECK LOCAL RULE on day-counting — calendar vs business days]`
- **Living financial-claim ledger.** Maintain a running, updated financial audit: solo-payment running totals (e.g., mortgage paid alone), separate-property/inheritance tracing, dissipation inventory, and the net-claim calculation — updated as documents arrive, ready to drop into a filing or the trial packet.
- **Proposed Findings of Fact & Conclusions of Law + Proposed Final Order.** For a final trial or contested evidentiary hearing, draft proposed *findings of fact*, *conclusions of law*, and a *proposed final order* the judge can adopt — make it easy for the court to rule your way on paper. Bundle with the exhibit index + witness list = the trial packet.
