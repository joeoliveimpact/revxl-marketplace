# Responsive Filing (answering the other side) — Drafting Counsel mode

> **Jurisdiction note (Clawyer):** this file was written for Mississippi family court. Outside Mississippi, or outside a family case, treat every rule number, case name, deadline, fee and form in it as an example of what to look up, not as your law. Family-law steps in it do not apply to other kinds of case. Confirm your own state's version first and tag anything unconfirmed `CONFIRM-BEFORE-CITING`.

> For the **full menu of every way to fight an incoming filing across multiple fronts** (procedural defenses, merits,
> counter-motions, discovery, order-integrity, negotiation, record, restraint), use `references/response-arsenal.md`
> first to pick the coordinated plan. **This file** is the focused method for drafting the **single substantive
> response** once that's chosen.

When the opposing party files a motion or response, work it in **five steps**. Goal: the user understands exactly what
was filed and answers it cleanly, one issue, on time.

## The 5 steps
1. **Plain-language summary.** In simple words (Tier 1): what are they actually asking the court to do, and **what must
   they prove** to get it? Strip the legalese.
2. **Response-deadline alert.** State the deadline to respond. Verify it against the rule; if unconfirmed, mark
   `[CHECK LOCAL RULE]` and tell the user to confirm with the clerk **now** — a missed response can lose by default.
3. **Counter-argument map.** Map each thing they must prove to the governing factors (Albright / Armstrong / Ferguson /
   the controlling rule) and to the **evidence the user has** that defeats or weakens it. Note gaps.
4. **Draft the response/objection.** One issue, proper chancery format, plain-language, numbered facts, specific relief,
   **proposed order attached**, signature + certificate of service. Run the one-issue and citation-discipline checks.
5. **Strategic note.** Tell the user plainly: is this filing **substantive** (a real legal threat to meet head-on),
   **tactical** (delay/leverage/noise to stay calm about), or **dangerous** (a trap or a deadline that could cost the
   case)? Recommend the posture.

## Rules
- Verify every cited authority and every deadline (citation discipline; verify-first procedure).
- Do not blend issues — if their motion mixes custody/support/property, your response still answers **one issue per
  filing** and you flag the blend.
- End with the single most important **NEXT ACTION** (usually: confirm the deadline, then file by [date]).
