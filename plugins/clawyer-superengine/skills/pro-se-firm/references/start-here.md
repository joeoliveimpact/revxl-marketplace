# Start Here — for the person using this firm

> You do **not** need to know anything about law or court to use this. You just talk in your own words.
> This page is the firm's reminder to keep everything this simple.

## How to talk to the firm
Tell it what's going on in plain words. No legal terms needed. If you don't know where to begin, pick one:
- "I'm worried about my kids / custody"
- "I got court papers / something in the mail"
- "I have a court date coming up"
- "I need money help / I can't afford something"
- "My ex sent a message and I don't know how to answer"
- "I don't understand this document"
- "I don't know where to start"

*(There's a fuller list of plain things you can say in `references/starter-questions.md` — but you don't need it; just talk.)*

Whatever you say, the firm takes it from there and puts the right people on it — and it tells you who's working so you
can see the firm actually doing the job. **You never have to know the names of any specialists or how this is organized.**

**Got a paper, order, message, or file?** Don't type it out — **upload it, paste the text, snap a photo, or tell the
firm where the file is**, and it reads the document itself. Then it looks up the rules that apply and organizes
everything for you.

## What the firm will always do for you
- **Explain in plain words first.** Want the deeper legal version? Just ask.
- **Tell you exactly what to do next** — step by step, including what to print, what to bring, who to hand it to.
- **Never use a court word without explaining it.**
- **Tell you the truth about your case** — even the hard parts.
- **Point you to free human help** (real lawyers and clinics). The firm is not a lawyer.

## What the firm needs from you (only when you're ready)
Just the basics about your case. It asks a few easy questions at a time and writes them down for you. If you don't know
an answer, "I don't know" is a fine answer.

## If you ever feel lost
Say **"I'm lost"** or **"explain that simpler"** and the firm slows down and breaks it into the smallest steps.
