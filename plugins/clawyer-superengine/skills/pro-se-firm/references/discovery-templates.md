# Discovery Templates — Mississippi Chancery (the firm's offense kit)

> **Jurisdiction note (Clawyer):** this file was written for Mississippi family court. Outside Mississippi, or outside a family case, treat every rule number, case name, deadline, fee and form in it as an example of what to look up, not as your law. Family-law steps in it do not apply to other kinds of case. Confirm your own state's version first and tag anything unconfirmed `CONFIRM-BEFORE-CITING`.

> **Discovery = the lawful way to make the other side hand over proof and lock in their answers** *before* trial. It is
> the core of going on offense (see `references/proactive-offense.md`): serve it early. Governed by **MRCP 26–37.**
> Tier 2 documents; Drafting Counsel fills them from `case-profile.md` and the Marital Estate Inventory.
>
> **Verified this build (2026-05-23, official MRCP text):**
> - **Scope (MRCP 26(b)(1))** — "Parties may obtain discovery regarding any matter, **not privileged**," relevant to the
>   case. Frequency of methods is **not limited** unless the court orders otherwise. `[VERIFIED — official MRCP]`
> - **Requests for Admission (MRCP 36)** — each matter "**is admitted unless, within thirty days after service of the
>   request**," the party serves a written answer or objection. *Miss the 30 days → it's deemed admitted.* This is the
>   sharpest tool here — use it, and never blow the 30-day clock on incoming ones. `[VERIFIED — official MRCP]`
> - **Interrogatories (MRCP 33(a))** — limited to **thirty (30)** as a matter of right ("written interrogatories not to
>   exceed thirty in number"; leave of court, on a showing of necessity, is required to serve more); each interrogatory
>   "shall consist of a single question." `[VERIFIED — MRCP 33(a), official text via MC Law mirror, 2026-05-23]`
> - **Production response time** — 30 days is the standard response period; **confirm the exact MRCP 34 period** before
>   serving. `[CONFIRM — MRCP 34]`
>
> **Client-protection floor cuts both ways.** *Serving* discovery = offense. *Answering* the other side's discovery runs
> the protection floor hard: answer truthfully (a false 8.05 or discovery answer is fraud on the court) **but**
> **object properly** to privileged / overbroad / harassing requests, never over-disclose, and never volunteer a
> self-defeating admission. Route incoming discovery through Drafting Counsel + the protection floor, on the 30-day clock.

> **Word it so it doesn't telegraph your case.** Before drafting any request that targets a sensitive fact (abuse,
> hidden income, dissipation), read `references/discovery-strategy.md`: ask for **small, neutral, documentable facts —
> never the conclusion** — so a witness willing to lie can't see what you're building and "answer it wrong" to warn
> themselves. Box them with facts your documents already prove; save the inference for argument; prove each pivotal fact
> 2–3 independent ways.

Templates below: 1. Interrogatories · 2. Requests for Production · 3. Requests for Admission · 4. Notice of Deposition /
Subpoena Duces Tecum · 5. Discovery responses (answering theirs). All carry a caption (`filing-templates.md` #1) and a
certificate of service (#13). Organize by issue, but discovery itself is **not** a one-issue "filing" — you may seek
custody, support, and property facts in one set, grouped by topic.

## 1. Interrogatories  *(written questions they must answer under oath)*
```
[CAPTION — Title: "PLAINTIFF'S FIRST SET OF INTERROGATORIES TO DEFENDANT"]

Pursuant to M.R.C.P. 33, Plaintiff propounds the following interrogatories, to be answered separately, fully, in
writing and under oath, within the time allowed by the rule.

DEFINITIONS: "You/your" = the responding party; "document" = any record in any form; "identify" (person) = name,
address, phone, relationship; (document) = type, date, author, custodian.

INCOME / EMPLOYMENT
1. State your employer(s), position, and gross monthly income for the last 36 months, and identify each source.
2. Identify all other income (self-employment, commissions, bonuses, rental, investment, benefits, gifts).

ASSETS / PROPERTY
3. Identify every financial account (bank, brokerage, crypto, app) you hold or control, with institution and balance.
4. Identify all real property you own or have an interest in, and all retirement/pension/IRA accounts.
5. Identify any asset transferred, sold, or gifted in the last 3 years (date, item, value, recipient). [tracing]

DEBTS
6. Identify each debt in your name or jointly, with creditor, balance, and monthly payment.

CHILDREN / CUSTODY  *(when custody is at issue)*
7. State your work schedule and who cares for the child during your work hours.
8. Identify every person who has lived in your household in the last 2 years.
9. Identify each witness you may call regarding the child's best interest.

GENERAL
10. Identify every person with knowledge of the facts in this case and the subject of their knowledge.
11. Identify every document you may introduce at trial.

[DATE + SIGNATURE + ADDRESS]   [CERTIFICATE OF SERVICE]
```
*Limited to **30** as of right (MRCP 33(a)); more requires leave of court on a showing of necessity; one question each.
`[VERIFIED — MRCP 33(a), 2026-05-23]`*

## 2. Requests for Production of Documents  *(M.R.C.P. 34)*
```
[CAPTION — Title: "PLAINTIFF'S FIRST REQUESTS FOR PRODUCTION TO DEFENDANT"]

Pursuant to M.R.C.P. 34, produce the following within the time allowed by the rule (legible copies; native files where
requested):

1. Federal & state tax returns (with W-2s/1099s) for the last 3 years.
2. Pay stubs / earnings statements for the last 12 months.
3. Statements for every bank, brokerage, and retirement account for the last 24 months.
4. Documents showing any asset transfer, sale, or gift in the last 3 years.
5. Deeds, mortgages, and appraisals for any real property.
6. Loan/credit-card statements for every debt claimed.
7. The complete UCCR 8.05 financial statement and all supporting documents.
8. All text messages, emails, and co-parenting-app records concerning the child or the parties [date range].
   *[scope it tightly — see protection note; demand the OTHER side's, don't over-produce your own]*
9. Any document you may introduce at trial; any expert report.

[DATE + SIGNATURE + ADDRESS]   [CERTIFICATE OF SERVICE]
```
*Response period: 30 days is standard — `[CONFIRM — MRCP 34(b)]`.*

## 3. Requests for Admission  *(M.R.C.P. 36 — the sharp tool: silence = admission in 30 days)*
```
[CAPTION — Title: "PLAINTIFF'S FIRST REQUESTS FOR ADMISSION TO DEFENDANT"]

Pursuant to M.R.C.P. 36, admit or deny each of the following. Each matter is ADMITTED unless you serve a written answer
or objection within THIRTY (30) DAYS of service.

1. Admit that [a specific, provable fact — e.g., "on [date] you received the school's email regarding the child"].
2. Admit that [Exhibit __] is a true and correct copy of [document].
3. Admit that [a fact that pins down their position or contradicts a prior statement].
   *(Each request = ONE specific fact, separately set forth — MRCP 36.)*

[DATE + SIGNATURE + ADDRESS]   [CERTIFICATE OF SERVICE]
```
*Use these to authenticate exhibits and to lock the other side into facts the Records Analyst found in the contradiction
log. `[VERIFIED — MRCP 36, 30-day deemed-admitted rule]`*

## 5. Answering THEIR discovery  *(protection-floor controlled)*
```
[CAPTION — Title: "DEFENDANT'S/PLAINTIFF'S RESPONSES TO {{party}}'S {{interrogatories/RFP/RFA}}"]

For each item: (a) a complete, truthful answer; OR (b) a specific objection (privileged / overbroad / not relevant /
unduly burdensome / harassing), stating the ground — then answer the unobjectionable part.

RESPONSE 1: {{answer or objection}}
...
[DATE + SIGNATURE]   {{Interrogatory answers are under oath — notarized.}}   [CERTIFICATE OF SERVICE]
```
*Run every incoming answer through the **client-protection floor**: truthful, but no over-disclosure, no self-defeating
volunteering, proper objections preserved. **Calendar the 30-day deadline the day you're served** — for Requests for
Admission, missing it admits everything (MRCP 36).*
