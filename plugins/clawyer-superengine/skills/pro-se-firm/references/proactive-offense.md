# Case Campaign — go on offense, get ahead

> **Jurisdiction note (Clawyer):** this file was written for Mississippi family court. Outside Mississippi, or outside a family case, treat every rule number, case name, deadline, fee and form in it as an example of what to look up, not as your law. Family-law steps in it do not apply to other kinds of case. Confirm your own state's version first and tag anything unconfirmed `CONFIRM-BEFORE-CITING`.

Reacting keeps a person alive; **offense wins.** The firm doesn't wait to be attacked — it **drives the case toward the
goal** and keeps the person a step ahead, so the other side has to respond to *them*. Set the agenda. Make your case the
one the court works from.

## 1. Define the goal, then work backward
- Pull the **goal** from the case profile (e.g., expand parenting time toward equal, establish/modify custody, fair
  property division) and the **anchor date** (trial/hearing).
- Build a **backward plan**: from the goal and date, list what must be **filed, proven, and gathered — and by when.**
  That's the campaign roadmap. Everything else serves it.

## 2. Go on offense — file YOUR motions, don't wait to be sued
Common proactive moves (always verify deadlines/local rules first):
- **Serve discovery first** — interrogatories + requests for production to force disclosure (income, assets, accounts,
  records). Every month without it is a month the other side can hide things.
- **File your own motion** — to establish/modify custody or **set a specific parenting schedule**; for a **scheduling
  conference**; to **compel** disclosure if they stall.
- **Propose the parenting plan** — put YOUR specific, dated schedule on the table so the court starts from your draft.
- **Lock the evidence early** — preserve records; request the child's school/medical records directly.
- **Pre-empt** — if a hearing or proposed order is coming, file *before* it to set the frame.

## 3. Build the case before you need it (proactive documentation)
- Keep the **Daily Parenting Log** going and the **Child Snapshot** updated (`references/child-snapshot.md`) —
  contemporaneous proof beats a last-minute scramble.
- Assemble the exhibit set early; map each item to its **Albright / Armstrong / Ferguson** factor; **fill the gaps now**,
  while there's time.

## 4. Anticipate the opponent and pre-empt
- Predict their likely moves (from their patterns) and **pre-build the rebuttal**.
- **Harden your own weaknesses before they're attacked** — get ahead of the bad facts (defensive prep; see operating-
  doctrine "Accountability").

## 5. Proactive cadence — don't wait to be asked
- After **every** task, surface the **next offensive move** — not just "respond to X," but **"here's what to FILE next,
  and why."**
- Offer a **weekly case brief**: where you are, what's coming, and the single highest-value thing to do this week to get
  ahead. (This can be **scheduled** to run on its own, like the docket watcher.)

## Feeds
Discovery, Drafting Counsel (your motions), Parenting-Plan Drafter, Evidence Specialist, Local-Procedure Clerk
(deadlines), and the Docket Watcher. **One issue per filing; verify-first; end every output with the next offensive
action.**
