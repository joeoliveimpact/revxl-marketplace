# Starter Questions — what to say to launch the firm

> **Jurisdiction note (Clawyer):** this file was written for Mississippi family court. Outside Mississippi, or outside a family case, treat every rule number, case name, deadline, fee and form in it as an example of what to look up, not as your law. Family-law steps in it do not apply to other kinds of case. Confirm your own state's version first and tag anything unconfirmed `CONFIRM-BEFORE-CITING`.

The person doesn't need the "right words." They can say **any of these — or anything like them** — and the firm fires
the matching work **on its own.** This is both the **menu the firm offers** a new/overwhelmed person and the **patterns
it listens for.** Match the *intent*, not the exact words; recognize slang and messy phrasing; if a request fits two
paths, do **both**; never make the person pick a category.

## Just getting started / overwhelmed → Intake + plain front door
"I don't know where to start." · "I'm just starting this." · "I don't know anything about court." · "Help, I'm lost."

## There's a problem / "what do I do about this?" → Problem-to-Proposal
"She's doing X and it's not right." · "This isn't working." · "What can I do about ___?" · "She keeps ___." · "The order
isn't being followed." · "I'm so frustrated about ___." → load `references/problem-to-proposal.md`: define the problem,
build concrete solutions, and turn them into a fix that can be offered to the court (proposed order / motion /
parenting-plan term / declaration / settlement term). We never just raise a problem — we bring the proposed answer.

## I want a divorce / where does this go from here → Divorce Roadmap (start to finish)
"I want to get divorced." · "How do I start a divorce?" · "What do I file first?" · "Walk me through the whole thing." ·
"What happens next in my case?" · "What's left to file before trial?" → load `references/divorce-roadmap.md`, place the
case at its current stage, and give the **single next filing** (drafted from `references/filing-templates.md`).

## Something changed / the order isn't being followed (AFTER the divorce) → Post-Judgment Roadmap
"We're already divorced but…" · "He stopped paying support." · "She won't follow the custody order / won't let me see my
kid." · "I lost my job — can I lower support?" · "My ex wants to move away with the kids." · "When does child support
end?" · "He's behind on support — how do I collect?" · "Can I change custody now that things are different?" · "I want to
take her back to court." · "Do I have to pay for college?" → load `references/post-judgment-roadmap.md`, match the
proceeding (modify / contempt / enforce / relocation / emancipation / college / interstate / appeal), confirm that
track's legal test, and give the **single next petition** with a **Rule 81 special summons**.

## This order is too vague / they twist it → Gray-Area / Ambiguity Auditor
"The order is too vague." · "It doesn't say a time/place." · "She interprets it however she wants." · "It says 'as
agreed' and we never agree." · "There's a loophole." · "Make this order more specific." → load
`references/ambiguity-auditor.md`: flag every gray area, sort by who it favors, and draft specific replacement language
for the ones that hurt you.

## My kids / custody → deep custody intake + Child Snapshot + Albright
"I'm scared about my kids." · "She won't let me see him." · "I want more time with my son." · "Help me with custody." ·
"What are my chances of getting custody?" · "She took the kids."

## Money / property / support / alimony / retirement → deep financial intake + Marital Estate Inventory
"What happens with the house?" · "She's hiding money." · "Do I have to pay her?" · "What do I get?" · "How do we split
the retirement?" · "I can't afford this." · "Is she going to get alimony?" · "What about our debt?"
**Receiving side (the broke/lower-earner spouse — pursue, don't just defend):** "Can I get alimony / spousal support?" ·
"I can't survive on my own." · "I gave up my career/job for the family." · "I supported him through school." · "Do I get
part of his retirement / the house?" · "I want my fair share." · "Am I owed anything?" → claim it: the right alimony
type + a larger equitable property share (`armstrong-ferguson.md`, property-first strategy).
**Career-sacrifice / "I put you through school" (its own playbook):** "I stayed home with the kids and gave up my
career." · "I put her/him through college and now they're leaving." · "They have a degree and a big income because of my
sacrifice." · "Do I get anything for giving up my career?" · "Can I get money for putting them through school?" → load
`references/career-sacrifice-claim.md` (reimbursement alimony + periodic/rehabilitative + larger Ferguson share, stacked).

## I got a document → read + analyze (Markdown Maker + Records Analyst)
"I got papers in the mail." · "She sent me this." · "What does this mean?" · "Can you read this order?" · *(or they
upload / paste / photograph it)*

## I was abused (any kind — incl. financial) → Abuse & Coercive-Control Specialist
"She controlled all the money for years." · "I wasn't allowed to work." · "I had to ask for an allowance." · "It wasn't
physical but it was abuse." · "He cut me off from money / friends / family." · "She racked up debt in my name." · "How do
I prove the financial abuse?" · "Can I make her admit it?" → load `references/abuse-coercive-control.md` (safety-screen
first; then prove the pattern + lock in admissions via Requests for Admission). *If there's physical/sexual/stalking
danger, protection comes first — §93-21 + National DV Hotline 1-800-799-7233.*

## The other side is coming after me → Adversarial Combat
"Her lawyer filed something." · "They're saying I'm a bad parent." · "She's lying about me." · "They're attacking me." ·
"How do I fight this?"

## Every way to fight it / hit it on multiple fronts → Response Arsenal
"What are ALL my options?" · "How do I fight this from every angle?" · "I want to hit this on multiple fronts." · "What
can I file back?" · "Give me everything I can do against this." → load `references/response-arsenal.md`: it maps every
front (procedural, merits, counter-motions, discovery, order-integrity, negotiation, record, restraint) into a
coordinated battle map. (Pairs with Adversarial Combat.)

## They're walking all over me / stand up for me in court → Courtroom Bulldog
"They're walking all over me." · "She gets whatever she wants." · "The judge isn't hearing me." · "Her lawyer
steamrolls me." · "How do I stand up to them in court?" · "They keep pushing me around." · "I don't want to get bullied
at the hearing." → load `references/courtroom-bulldog.md` (hold-the-line reflexes; calm voice, relentless on substance).

## Negotiating / a draft keeps changing → Negotiation Counsel
"We're trying to settle." · "They sent a counter-offer." · "Every time the draft comes back something's different." ·
"They keep sneaking changes into the agreement." · "Is this settlement fair?" · "Help me negotiate the parenting plan." ·
"They changed a part we didn't even discuss." → load `references/negotiation-counsel.md` (run the position + the
redline-integrity protocol: scope-lock, full-document diff every round, change ledger).

## I need to send or answer a message → Communication Command
"How do I respond to her?" · "What do I say to the GAL?" · "The clerk emailed me." · "Her attorney emailed me." ·
"I want to tell the judge…" → the firm converts it to the **proper, copied** form — never improper ex parte contact.

## Deadlines / how-to / the law → Research + Local-Procedure (verify-first)
"When is this due?" · "How do I file?" · "Where do I file?" · "Is there a law about…?" · "What's the rule on…?" ·
"How much does it cost?"

## Make them hand over proof / lock in their answers → Discovery
"How do I get her bank records?" · "Can I make him prove his income?" · "She's hiding money — how do I find it?" ·
"I got interrogatories / requests for admission." · "What is discovery?" → load `references/discovery-templates.md`
(serve early; on incoming discovery, calendar the 30-day clock and run the client-protection floor).

## Ask without tipping them off / they'll lie if they see it coming → Discovery Strategist
"How do I word this so she doesn't see what I'm getting at?" · "He'll lie if he knows what I'm after." · "How do I get
her to admit it without warning her?" · "I need to prove this more than one way." → load `references/discovery-strategy.md`
(ask for small neutral documentable facts, box them with what you can already prove, save the conclusion for argument).

## What should I be doing? → Case Campaign (proactive offense)
"What should I be doing?" · "Am I doing enough?" · "What's next?" · "I'm just waiting." · "What should I file next?"

## It's urgent → Emergency Mode
"URGENT" · "My hearing is tomorrow." · "This is due tomorrow." · "I just got served." → skip planning: filing → hearing
script → exhibit list → deadline.

## Show me the work → behind-the-scenes trace
"What are you doing?" · "Show me." → reveal which specialists fired and what each did.
