# Deep Financial Intake — the whole money side (property · support · alimony · retirement · debt)

> **Jurisdiction note (Clawyer):** this file was written for Mississippi family court. Outside Mississippi, or outside a family case, treat every rule number, case name, deadline, fee and form in it as an example of what to look up, not as your law. Family-law steps in it do not apply to other kinds of case. Confirm your own state's version first and tag anything unconfirmed `CONFIRM-BEFORE-CITING`.

The financial half of the divorce, at the same depth as the custody intake. **Ask everything, extract all** — the person
usually doesn't know what's "marital vs separate," what's traceable, or what's relevant, so the firm gathers it and
decides. Maps to the resolved state's property, spousal-support, and child-support law (MS example: **Ferguson** equitable distribution, **Armstrong** alimony, the **MS child-support guidelines**).
Gather the whole picture even though each **filing** stays one issue.

## Say this first — candor compact (money edition)
> "Be honest about **every dollar** — income, accounts, debts, even what you'd rather not mention. Hidden money found
> later destroys your credibility on *everything*, including custody. There's nothing we can't work with if I know it —
> but only if I know it."

## How to ask
A few questions at a time; calm; **never ask whether info is "usable" — gather it.** Write answers to `case-profile.md`
and the **Marital Estate Inventory** (`references/marital-estate-inventory.md`) as you go. For each item, ask **"what
proves it?"** (statements, deeds, tax returns, deposits, written admissions).

## Question bank (grouped; ask in batches)

### A. Income — all sources *(child support; alimony)*
1. Both parties' income: wages, self-employment, bonuses, overtime, commissions, tips? 2. Benefits/disability/VA/Social Security? 3. Rental, investment, or side income? 4. Pay stubs + tax returns (last 3–5 years) available? 5. Any income that's irregular, cash, or hard to document?

### B. Expenses & standard of living *(alimony need + ability to pay)*
6. Monthly expenses (housing, utilities, food, insurance, transport, child costs)? 7. The marital standard of living during the marriage? 8. Who pays what right now?

### C. Assets — the whole marital estate *(Ferguson)*
9. Real estate (marital home + any other) and rough value? 10. Bank/checking/savings — all of them, both names? 11. Retirement — 401(k), pension, IRA, PERS — balances, vesting? 12. Investments/brokerage/crypto? 13. Vehicles? 14. Business interests or self-employment assets? 15. Valuable personal property (jewelry, tools, collectibles)? 16. Life insurance with cash value; tax refunds owed? 17. For each asset — **marital or separate? acquired when and how? in whose name?**

### D. Separate property & tracing *(Ferguson adjustment — gold when documented)*
18. Any inheritance, gift, or pre-marital asset? 19. Was it kept separate or mixed into joint accounts/the home? 20. Where did it go (e.g., into the house, a car, debts)? 21. What proof traces it (deposit records, deeds, the other spouse's written admission)?

### E. Debts & liabilities
22. Mortgage balance(s)? 23. Credit cards, loans, medical, taxes owed? 24. Whose name is each in; who's paying now; joint or separate?

### F. Contributions & dissipation *(Ferguson)*
25. Each spouse's financial contributions? 26. Non-financial contributions (homemaking, child-rearing, supporting the other's career/education)? 27. Any waste/dissipation — hiding money, big spending, gambling, vacations while bills went unpaid, draining a child's account — with dates + proof?

### G. Earning capacity & career *(Armstrong — rehabilitative)*
28. What education/credentials did each spouse earn during the marriage, and who funded/enabled it? 29. Did one spouse sacrifice career/earning for the family? 30. Current earning capacity each? 31. Lapsed credentials — restoration cost + time + income unlocked? 32. What support tied to specific milestones would make you self-sufficient?

### H. The marital home
33. Value (a real appraisal — **not** a Zillow estimate)? 34. Equity, mortgage, who's paying? 35. Keep or sell — and who lives there now? 36. Any solo payments you've made (running total = potential credit)?

### I. Child-related costs *(support)*
37. Childcare cost/provider? 38. Health insurance for the child — who carries it, cost? 39. Medical, school, extracurricular costs? *(These feed the child-support guideline.)*

### J. Tax & insurance
40. Filing status; who claims the child as a dependent? 41. Health/life insurance arrangements?

### K. Your OWN vulnerabilities *(defensive prep)*
42. Anything they could raise — undisclosed accounts, your own spending/waste, a hidden asset? 43. Honest context + proof it's accounted for.

## After intake
Hand to the Financial & Property bench (`references/financial-property-team.md`): **Forensic Accountant, Asset-Tracing,
Business Valuation, Real-Estate Appraiser, QDRO/Retirement, Vocational/Earning-Capacity, Support Calc, Tax.** Build the
**Marital Estate Inventory**; map to Ferguson/Armstrong/guidelines; present **routes & outcomes** (negotiate / mediate /
trial; settle the whole estate). **Scrutinize valuations** — a Zillow estimate and a "net-difference" retirement split
are **not** court-standard. One issue per filing; verify-first; end with a NEXT ACTION.
