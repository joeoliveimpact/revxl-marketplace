# Trial Packet — the one all-in-one document the person carries into any hearing or trial

> **Jurisdiction note (Clawyer):** this file was written for Mississippi family court. Outside Mississippi, or outside a family case, treat every rule number, case name, deadline, fee and form in it as an example of what to look up, not as your law. Family-law steps in it do not apply to other kinds of case. Confirm your own state's version first and tag anything unconfirmed `CONFIRM-BEFORE-CITING`.

> 🧭 **METHOD IS UNIVERSAL.** This is the assembler for a single, printable, tabbed packet the person reads from in
> court. It pulls from `courtroom-scripts.md`, `evidence-organization.md`, `evidence-evaluator.md`, `courtroom-notes.md`,
> `courtroom-bulldog.md`, `order-integrity.md`, and the forum's governing legal frames (the resolved state-law profile;
> Mississippi example: Albright/Armstrong/Ferguson). No case facts live in this file — it is a template.

**Auto-fires** whenever a hearing, trial, GAL meeting, or mediation is set, or the person says "get me ready." **BAKED-IN
ROLE (standing):** EVERY hearing gets its **own dedicated, freshly built packet** — temporary support, custody,
modification, enforcement/contempt, GAL, mediation, AND the final trial — **not just the final.** The moment a hearing
date exists, the firm starts that hearing's packet on its own and treats it as a P0 living document, updated each session
until it is printed 1–2 days before. The firm builds ONE packet **per hearing**, tuned to that hearing's issue(s) (one
issue per filing still holds). Crawl: assemble it in the background, then hand it over and walk the person through using
it one section at a time.

> The proven layout below is modeled on a real pro-se trial document that worked. Keep the human elements (the steadying
> note, the pocket card, the day-by-day checklist) — they are what keep a scared person functional, not fluff.

## Packet layout (assemble in this order, tabbed)

**0. Cover page.** Case caption, court, judge, hearing date and type. **"Your Numbers"** — the 3–5 figures that matter at
a glance (income gap, parenting-time percentage, support figure, the one dollar amount in dispute). A short **steadying
note** in the person's corner ("being scared means you understand what's at stake... calm parents win").

**1. Checklist + day-by-day task list.** What to print and **how many copies** (default: judge + other side + you, confirm
the court's rule `ASK THE CLERK`), exhibit page ranges in order, and a **dated task list with checkboxes** counting down to
the hearing (tonight / each day / morning-of), ending with the morning-of "first words" and "write down the exact date
the order is signed + count the deadlines."

**2. Binder lineup = the Exhibit Quick-Reference grab sheet** (`evidence-organization.md` Step 3.5). Tab # → plain name →
proves → **"grab it when..."**. The first page; a loose copy on the podium.

**3. Opening statement** — fully written, "know it, don't read it," under 2 minutes (`courtroom-scripts.md`).

**4. Court script, phase by phase** — what happens at each phase of THIS hearing type and what the person does/says at each
(`courtroom-scripts.md`). Tune to the forum's procedure `[CHECK LOCAL RULE]`.

**5. Pretrial / preliminary matters to raise FIRST** — the things said before evidence: objection to a defective report,
witness **sequestration ("the Rule")**, any **motion in limine**, a written **findings request** (so the court states
reasons on each factor), stipulations, time allocation. Each as a one-line script.

**6. Every allegation → your answer → your exhibit.** The defensive core. List **every** attack the other side is likely
to make; under each, the calm factual answer and the **exact tab/exhibit** that backs it (or "no record exists — that is
the evidence"). This is how the person stays calm under fire.

**7. Cross-examination — WITNESS BY WITNESS, for EVERY potential witness.** (HARD: prepare all of them, even the ones who
may not appear — last time some witnesses did not show, and the unprepared ones are the risk.) For each witness:
- A **witness list** up front: every person who could be called (the other party, the GAL, each of their witnesses, any
  expert/therapist/records custodian) + whether they are confirmed/subpoenaed/uncertain.
- Then **one block per witness**, grouped by topic, with **numbered leading questions** (Q1, Q2...), one fact each, each
  backed by an exhibit, with a blank to write the answer. Short questions; stop; let the answer be the problem; impeach
  with the exhibit if they deny a provable fact (`courtroom-scripts.md` impeachment move).

**8. Direct examination** — for any friendly witness of the person's own: non-leading questions in story order, plus that
witness's **subpoena status** (served? coming?).

**9. Objections guide** — the few that matter, make-and-meet, used sparingly (`courtroom-bulldog.md` + `courtroom-scripts.md`).

**10. The person's own testimony / narrative** — short factual statements in time order, each tied to a factor and an
exhibit. Facts beat adjectives.

**11. Closing argument** — tie each proven fact to the governing factor and to the specific relief; end with the exact
orders requested. Bring the **proposed order** you want signed.

**12. Law explained plainly + the elements/factors-with-proof checklist.** The handful of governing statutes/cases for
this forum, in plain English, **with certainty tags** (court-ready only if `[VERIFIED]`). Then a checklist of every
element/factor you must prove for this issue with the proof lined up next to each — so nothing goes unproven.

**13. Real-time tools.** Blank **two-column note pages** (`courtroom-notes.md`) + a **contingency "if X happens" sheet**:
time gets cut, an exhibit is excluded (make an **offer of proof**), they spring something, or you blank ("May I have a
moment, Your Honor?").

**14. After the ruling.** Write the judge's **exact words**; hand up the findings request before the ruling; **count the
deadlines** (reconsideration + appeal — missing the appeal window can lose the right forever) `[CONFIRM the exact
day-counts for this forum]`; run the **order-integrity check** when the written order arrives; and a pre-decided
**settlement floor** in case of a hallway deal.

**15. Pocket card — cut out and carry.** The few must-remember lines: the opening one-liner, the four safe sentences ("I
don't know, Your Honor" / "May I refer to my notes?" / "I'd like to respond to that" / "I'll defer to the Court"), the
calm cue, and the two deadline day-counts.

## Discipline
- One issue per filing; at a multi-issue final trial, organize **issue-by-issue in tabbed sections**, never blended.
- Child by **initials** in anything filed or handed up.
- **No fabrication**; certainty-tag every cite; `ASK THE CLERK` / `[CHECK LOCAL RULE]` for any procedure not verified.
- Format for reading under stress: large type, short lines, one question per line, blanks for answers.
- End with the single **NEXT ACTION** (usually: confirm witness list + the court's exhibit/sequestration procedure).
