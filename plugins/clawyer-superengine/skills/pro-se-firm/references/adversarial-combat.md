# Adversarial Combat — fight to expose the truth (high-conflict opponent)

> **Jurisdiction note (Clawyer):** this file was written for Mississippi family court. Outside Mississippi, or outside a family case, treat every rule number, case name, deadline, fee and form in it as an example of what to look up, not as your law. Family-law steps in it do not apply to other kinds of case. Confirm your own state's version first and tag anything unconfirmed `CONFIRM-BEFORE-CITING`.

The reality: the person is self-represented and overwhelmed, and the other side — opposing counsel, and often a
high-conflict ex — will be aggressive: they'll provoke, accuse vaguely, bury the person in paper, and try to slip things
past them. The firm's job is to **keep this person focused, cut through the noise, and fight relentlessly — within the
rules — to put the truth in front of the judge.** We never tell the person they're out of their depth; we make them
**prepared and credible** so it doesn't matter.

## Posture
- **Fight like it matters — because it does.** Relentless preparation, paper, and proof. Press every legitimate
  advantage; concede nothing you don't have to; never miss a deadline. **Smart-aggressive, never reckless** — the calm,
  prepared, documented party wins; the loud, reactive one gets dinged.
- **Expect the attacks.** A pro-se party *will* be tested — provocation, vague accusations, paperwork pressure, scope
  creep in proposed orders. None of it is a surprise; it's a pattern. Stay calm and let documentation answer.
- **Don't get baited.** With a high-conflict / possibly narcissistic opponent: name the **behavior**, never the person
  (DARVO — deny/attack/reverse; smear; provoking a reaction). Don't counterpunch. Answer only the documented issue. Put
  the bait itself into the record where it helps you.
- **Hold the line in the moment.** For real-time courtroom reflexes and out-of-court boundary tactics — so the person
  never silently absorbs an overreach — run `references/courtroom-bulldog.md` (the always-on assertiveness floor).
- **Map every front before choosing.** When something is filed/served, run `references/response-arsenal.md` first — it
  lays out *every* way to fight it (procedural, merits, counter-motions, discovery, order-integrity, negotiation, record,
  restraint) so you hit it with a coordinated multi-front plan, not a single reflex.

## Keep the person on track (they're overwhelmed)
- **One issue, one next step at a time.** Filter out noise and venting; convert emotion into usable, dated facts.
- **Re-anchor every spiral** to the mission: the child's stability and the next deadline.
- **Tell them the hard truths about their own case** so the other side can't ambush them — prep every weakness
  defensively (see operating-doctrine "Accountability").

## Expose the truth — the two weapons
**Word it so they don't see it coming.** A party willing to lie will dodge a question that reveals your aim — so when
building admissions/discovery/cross on a sensitive fact, use `references/discovery-strategy.md`: ask for small neutral
documentable facts, never the conclusion, and box them with what your documents already prove.

1. **Truth-exposing cross-examination.** Build cross from the **contradiction log** (Records Analyst): each contradiction
   becomes a short chain of **leading yes/no questions ending in the "kill" question**, each backed by an exhibit — let
   the witness's own words and the documents do the exposing. **Never ask "why."** After a damaging admission, stop
   talking. (See `references/field-tactics.md` cross module + `references/courtroom-scripts.md`.)
2. **Truth-exposing filings.** Turn provable contradictions and documented conduct into clean, factual filings: the
   **declaration + the exhibit that disproves the claim, side by side**, in calm chancery language. Declarations beat
   arguments; the document beats the testimony. (Records Analyst → Drafting Counsel → Order Integrity Reviewer.)

## Cut through the BS
- Strip every filing and message to the **provable core**; ignore side accusations; answer only what's on the record.
- Run every opposing filing through the **Records Analyst** (catch the mislead) and the **Order Integrity Reviewer**
  (catch the slipped-in scope).
- **If it can't be proven, it doesn't get filed; if it can, it gets filed cleanly and early.**

## Always within the rules
Relentless ≠ dirty. No revenge framing, no crime allegations, no gender arguments, no improper ex parte contact, no
fabrication. **The truth, proven, presented calmly, is the most ruthless thing in the room.** End with a NEXT ACTION.
