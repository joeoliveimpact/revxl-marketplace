# Name Change (and legitimation) — Mississippi § 93-17-1

## The statute
**Miss. Code § 93-17-1(1):** the **chancery court (or chancellor in vacation) of the county of the petitioners'
residence** has jurisdiction, **"upon the petition of any person to alter the names of such person,"** and to make a
living out-of-wedlock child **legitimate** and an heir of the petitioner. **§ 93-17-1(2):** an out-of-wedlock child
becomes legitimate if the **natural father marries the natural mother and acknowledges the child.** `[VERIFIED — FindLaw
published Miss. Code § 93-17-1(1)-(2), read verbatim 2026-05-24]`

## Three common situations
1. **Adult name change** (e.g., restoring a former/maiden name) — a petition to "alter the name" in the chancery court of
   the petitioner's county. **A name restoration is also commonly handled inside the divorce judgment itself** — ask for
   it in the divorce so a separate action isn't needed. `[CONFIRM BEFORE CITING — confirm whether the divorce decree can
   restore the name vs. a standalone petition in the user's county]`
2. **Minor child's name change** — petition to alter the child's name; the court applies the **child's best interest**,
   and the **other parent must get notice** and may object (their relationship/rights are weighed). `[CONFIRM BEFORE
   CITING — verify the minor-name-change best-interest standard + notice requirement / controlling case before filing]`
3. **Legitimation** — establishing a child as the legitimate child/heir of the father (often paired with **paternity** —
   see `references/paternity.md`), by petition or by the father marrying the mother + acknowledging the child
   (§ 93-17-1(2)).

## Cautions
- A minor name change over the **other parent's objection** is contested and fact-specific — don't treat it as
  ministerial.
- Name change does **not** by itself change custody, support, or paternity — keep issues separate (one-issue rule).
- After any name change, update the birth certificate / Social Security / records (process is administrative, not the
  court's job). `[CONFIRM BEFORE CITING — birth-record amendment procedure]`

## Filing sequence
1. Pick the situation (adult / minor / legitimation) and the **county of residence** (jurisdiction).
2. If mid-divorce and it's an adult restoration → **request it in the divorce** (Drafting Counsel adds the prayer).
3. Otherwise a **Petition to Change Name** in chancery (Drafting Counsel; `references/filing-templates.md`); for a minor,
   **serve/notice the other parent** and be ready on best-interest.
4. Proposed order/decree → **Order Integrity Reviewer**; then update records.
