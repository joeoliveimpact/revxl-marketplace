## Guardian vs. conservator (name which you need)
- **Guardian of a minor** = authority over the **child's person** (custody-like care: where they live, school, medical).
- **Conservator** = authority over the **child's property/money** (e.g., an inheritance, settlement, or benefits).
A case may need one or both. `[CONFIRM BEFORE CITING — confirm conservatorship sections (§ 93-20-401 et seq.) before filing]`

## The standard for a minor's guardian — § 93-20-201
**"A person becomes a guardian for a minor only on appointment by the court."** The court may appoint a guardian for a
minor who has none **if appointment is in the minor's best interest, and:**
- **(a)** each parent, after being fully informed, **consents**; **or**
- **(b)** all **parental rights have been terminated**; **or**
- **(c)** there is **clear and convincing evidence** that [the parents are unwilling/unable to exercise care — verify the
  exact (c) text]. `[VERIFIED — FindLaw published Miss. Code § 93-20-201(1)-(2)(a)-(c), read verbatim 2026-05-24; confirm
  the full (c) clause before filing]`

## How guardianship differs from a custody fight
- It runs against the **fact that parents have superior rights** — without consent or TPR, the (c) clear-and-convincing
  route is a **high bar** (similar in spirit to the natural-parent presumption — see
  `references/grandparent-third-party-custody.md`).
- It's often the **right tool when parents are absent/unable** (incarceration, illness, deployment, death) and a relative
  is already caring for the child — frequently **consensual** under (a).
- A guardianship can be **modified or terminated** (e.g., a parent rehabilitates and seeks the child back).
  `[CONFIRM BEFORE CITING — § 93-20-112 / termination sections]`

## Filing sequence
1. Name it: **guardian of the person**, **conservator of the estate**, or both.
2. Determine the route: **(a) parental consent**, **(b) prior TPR**, or **(c) clear-and-convincing** unfitness/inability.
3. **Petition for Appointment of Guardian** in chancery (Drafting Counsel; `references/filing-templates.md`); give
   **notice to the parents** and required parties; the court may appoint a **GAL** for the child.
4. Be ready on **best interest** + the route's proof; expect **bond/reporting** duties if a conservatorship of money.
   `[CONFIRM BEFORE CITING — bond/accounting requirements]`
5. Proposed order → **Order Integrity Reviewer**.
