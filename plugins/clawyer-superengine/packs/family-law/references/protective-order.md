# Domestic-Abuse Protective Order — procedure (Mississippi § 93-21)

## Honest scope limit (carry over from doctrine)
A § 93-21 protection order requires **"abuse" as the statute defines it** — physical harm, attempts/threats of physical
harm, sexual offenses, stalking, etc. **Financial/economic control alone does not qualify** for a protection order (it's
case leverage instead — alimony/property/custody/fault). `[VERIFIED — § 93-21-3 abuse definition, prior session]`

## Who can file & where (§ 93-21-7)
- **Any person may file for himself**; a **parent, adult household member, or next friend** may file **on behalf of a
  minor or a person alleged incompetent.** `[VERIFIED — FindLaw published Miss. Code § 93-21-7(1), read verbatim 2026-05-24]`
- File in **municipal, justice, county, or chancery court** (or a state military court). Emergency relief generally is
  **not** filed in chancery unless the chancellor permits or it's part of an ongoing chancery action. These are
  **priority cases** on the docket. `[VERIFIED — § 93-21-7(2), read verbatim 2026-05-24]`
- **The petitioner pays no filing/service costs**; costs (incl. attorney's fees) can be assessed to the respondent if the
  order issues — and to the petitioner only if the abuse claims are found meritless. `[VERIFIED — § 93-21-7(3)]`

## The two stages
1. **Emergency / ex parte (temporary) order** — issued on a showing of immediate danger, before the respondent is heard;
   short-lived, sets the hearing. `[CONFIRM BEFORE CITING — verify § 93-21-9 ex parte standard + timing before filing]`
2. **Order after hearing** — after notice and a hearing (§ 93-21-11), on a finding of abuse **by a preponderance of the
   evidence**, the court grants relief. `[VERIFIED — § 93-21-15, read verbatim 2026-05-24]`

## Relief the court can grant (§ 93-21-15) — verbatim highlights
Refrain-from-abuse order; **no-contact** (in person, phone, electronic); **prohibit/limit physical proximity**
(residence, school, work); **exclusive possession of the residence** (evict the respondent); **temporary custody and
visitation** of minor children; **temporary support**; **monetary compensation** for losses (medical, lost earnings,
moving costs, out-of-pocket, attorney's fees); prohibit transferring/encumbering jointly owned property; order
**counseling/treatment** for the respondent. `[VERIFIED — FindLaw published Miss. Code § 93-21-15(1)-(2), read verbatim
2026-05-24]`

## Duration (§ 93-21-15) — verbatim
- **Temporary** order (municipal/justice court): **≤ 30 days** — but if the parties have **no minor children in common**,
  it may exceed 30 days **up to 1 year.**
- **Final** order (chancery/county court): effective **for such time as the court deems appropriate**, with the
  **expiration date clearly stated.** `[VERIFIED — § 93-21-15(1)(b), (2)(b), read verbatim 2026-05-24]`

## Three sides of these cases (all auto-route here)
- **Getting one:** petition + the abuse proof (Evidence Evaluator: dates, photos, messages, medical/police records,
  witnesses) + safety plan.
- **Modifying / extending / dropping** an existing order → petition the issuing court. `[CONFIRM BEFORE CITING]`
- **Defending against one, or an alleged violation** → `references/response-arsenal.md` + Evidence + Records; a
  protection order has real consequences (firearms, custody, record) — take it seriously, don't ignore the hearing.
  Violating an order is a separate offense. `[CONFIRM BEFORE CITING — verify § 93-21-21 penalties]`

## Filing sequence
1. Safety first (911 / hotline if in danger). 2. **Petition for a Domestic Abuse Protection Order** in the proper court
(no fee). 3. Request **emergency/ex parte** relief if there's immediate danger. 4. Prepare for the **hearing** with the
abuse proof (preponderance). 5. Get the **final order** with a clear expiration and concrete no-contact/possession terms;
run the proposed order through **Order Integrity Reviewer**.

**NEXT ACTION:** Confirm the abuse fits § 93-21-3 (physical/threat/sexual/stalking); if yes, prepare the petition in the
correct court (no filing cost), request emergency relief if there's immediate danger, and assemble the dated abuse proof
for the hearing. If it's financial control only, route to `abuse-coercive-control.md` for the leverage path instead.
