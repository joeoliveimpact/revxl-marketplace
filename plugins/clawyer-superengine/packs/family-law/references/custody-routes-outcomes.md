# Custody Routes & Outcomes — show the person every path

> **Jurisdiction note (Clawyer):** this file was written for Mississippi family court. Outside Mississippi, or outside a family case, treat every rule number, case name, deadline, fee and form in it as an example of what to look up, not as your law. Family-law steps in it do not apply to other kinds of case. Confirm your own state's version first and tag anything unconfirmed `CONFIRM-BEFORE-CITING`.

For any custody matter, lay out **all the routes the case can take and all the outcomes the court can order**, with
trade-offs, so the person sees their real options instead of one path. Frame everything around the **child's best
interest** and present it calmly. The choice of route shapes the outcome — and the parent with the most specific,
contemporaneous, factor-aligned documentation is best positioned **on every route**.

## Routes (how a case can move)

| Route | What it takes | Trade-offs |
|---|---|---|
| **Negotiated parenting plan** (direct agreement) | Both agree on legal/physical custody + a specific written schedule, signed and entered. | Fastest, cheapest, least adversarial, most control. Needs cooperation + honest disclosure. Be **specific** — vague plans breed future fights. |
| **Mediation** | A neutral mediator; often court-encouraged/ordered. Bring the plan, schedule, and documentation. | Preserves the relationship, confidential, flexible. Non-binding until written into an agreed order. Poor fit where real safety issues or severe power imbalance exist. |
| **Contested temporary hearing** | Pleadings + declarations + a specific proposed schedule + exhibits; quick hearing to set arrangements *while the case is pending*. | Sets the early **status quo** (which later proceedings tend to respect — continuity matters). Limited time to present, so paper + a clear schedule win. |
| **Guardian ad litem (GAL)** | Court appoints an investigator/advocate for the child (common where fitness, safety, or special needs are at issue). Cooperate fully; hand over an organized picture. | A well-documented parent benefits — the Child Snapshot + Log give the GAL exactly what they need. Adds cost/time; the GAL's recommendation carries weight. |
| **Settlement** (often after discovery) | Exchange information; negotiate a comprehensive agreed order before trial. | Avoids trial risk/cost; you keep some control of terms. Needs honest disclosure + willingness to compromise. |
| **Trial** (contested final hearing) | Full evidence on each Albright factor: witnesses, exhibits, the documentation record; the chancellor decides. | For when agreement is impossible or safety requires a ruling. Highest cost/stress/risk; outcome is the judge's. This is where contemporaneous documentation pays off most. |

## Outcomes (what the court can order)

- **Legal custody (decision-making — school, medical, religion):** *joint legal* (share major decisions) or *sole/primary legal* (one decides, sometimes with a duty to consult).
- **Physical custody (where the child lives):** *joint physical* (substantial time with both, can approach equal) or *primary to one* with parenting time to the other.
- **Parenting-time schedules:** *standard* (e.g., alternating weekends + a weeknight + split holidays/summer) · *expanded / near-equal* (more weeknights, week-on/week-off — favored where both homes are stable and parents cooperate) · *supervised* (third-party/agency supervision — only where documented safety concerns exist) · *step-up / graduated* (time increases over milestones, to rebuild routine).
- **Common add-ons:** decision-making protocols, a communication method (co-parenting app), right of first refusal, travel/relocation terms, exchange logistics, holiday/summer rotation.

## How route choice drives outcome (say this plainly)
- **Cooperative routes** (agreement, mediation) let *you* shape the schedule and tend toward joint/expanded time.
- **Contested routes** (temporary hearing, trial) hand the decision to the chancellor, who weighs the Albright factors — so the **best-documented, calmest, most child-focused** parent is best positioned.
- A strong **early** record (temporary hearing) shapes the status quo that later steps respect.

Present these as **options with trade-offs**, recommend a path tied to the child's stability and the person's situation,
and route the chosen path to the right specialists (Parenting-Plan Drafter, Settlement/Mediation, Hearing-Prep, GAL).
