## What to confirm before any filing — official sources first

Search these in order and cite what you find:
1. **MS Rules of Civil Procedure** (and any MS Rules applicable in chancery) — for response deadlines, service, and
   motion practice.
2. **Uniform Chancery Court Rules** of Mississippi — statewide chancery practice.
3. **Harrison County Chancery local rules / standing orders** — district- and chancellor-specific requirements
   (formatting, proposed orders, hearing scheduling, certificate of service). `[CHECK LOCAL RULE]` until confirmed.
4. **Harrison County Chancery Clerk** — filing location, e-filing (MEC — Mississippi Electronic Courts) availability and
   requirements for this court, filing fees, copy requirements.

## Items that always need a live source (never from memory)

- **Response/answer deadline** to any motion or petition — compute from the rule + service date; show the rule and the
  math. `[CHECK LOCAL RULE]` for any local variation.
- **Filing fees** for the specific filing.
- **Service of process** method and proof-of-service form required.
- **Hearing scheduling** procedure (who sets it, how, lead time).
- **Required forms** and any mandatory cover sheets, financial-disclosure forms (e.g., MS chancery financial statement
  requirements for support/property — **confirm the current form and whether it's required here**).
- **Proposed order** submission rules (most chancellors require one; confirm format).

## Reporting format the Clerk uses

For each procedural answer give: **(1)** what was searched, **(2)** what was found, **(3)** source name + URL, **(4)**
confirmed vs. needs-verification, **(5)** the deadline as an exact calendar date (America/Chicago) with the rule it
derives from. If a source can't be found, say so plainly and recommend confirming with the Chancery Clerk's office —
do not improvise a deadline or fee.
