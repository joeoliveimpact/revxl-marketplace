# Paternity — establishing or disestablishing fatherhood (Mississippi)

> **Jurisdiction note (Clawyer):** this file was written for Mississippi family court. Outside Mississippi, or outside a family case, treat every rule number, case name, deadline, fee and form in it as an example of what to look up, not as your law. Family-law steps in it do not apply to other kinds of case. Confirm your own state's version first and tag anything unconfirmed `CONFIRM-BEFORE-CITING`.

> **When this fires (auto).** Unmarried parents; "he's not on the birth certificate," "I need to prove he's the father to
> get support," "she won't let me see my kid and we were never married," "I found out I'm not the father," DNA/paternity
> questions. Paternity is the **gateway** — without it, an unmarried father has no enforceable custody/visitation rights
> and no support order can issue. Research/drafting support — **not legal advice.**

## Why it comes first
For a child born to unmarried parents, the law must establish **who the legal father is** before custody, visitation, or
support can be ordered. Marriage creates a presumption of paternity; outside marriage it must be **established** (by
acknowledgment or court adjudication) — or, in the rare fraud case, **disestablished.**

## Who can bring it & the standard
Paternity may be determined **"upon the petition of the mother, or father, the child or any public authority chargeable
by law with the support of the child"**; an adjudication **after the death of the defendant must be made only upon clear
and convincing evidence.** `[VERIFIED — FindLaw published Miss. Code § 93-9-9(1), read verbatim 2026-05-24]` Once
paternity is lawfully determined or **acknowledged in writing**, the noncustodial parent's support liabilities are
enforceable. `[VERIFIED — § 93-9-9(1)]`

## Two routes in
1. **Voluntary Acknowledgment of Paternity (AOP)** — signed (often at the hospital); has the force of a legal finding
   once it can no longer be rescinded. `[CONFIRM BEFORE CITING — AOP rescission window + effect, § 93-9-9 / § 93-9-28]`
2. **Petition to establish paternity** (the court route) under the **Mississippi Uniform Law on Paternity, § 93-9-1 et
   seq.** — the court can order **genetic (DNA) testing.** `[CONFIRM BEFORE CITING — verify § 93-9-21 et seq. testing /
   presumption thresholds before filing]`

## What establishing paternity unlocks (then route to the right playbook)
- **Custody / visitation** → `references/albright-factors.md` + custody team (Albright best-interest applies once
  paternity is set). One issue per filing.
- **Child support** → `references/armstrong-ferguson.md` support section + § 43-19-101 guideline. Support can be ordered
  **retroactive** to birth in a paternity action. `[CONFIRM BEFORE CITING — retroactive-support scope]`
- **Birth-certificate amendment / the child's surname** → see `references/name-change.md` (§ 93-17-1 legitimation +
  name).

## Disestablishment / fraud (the harder, narrower route)
A man who signed an AOP or was adjudicated father but later has proof he is **not** the biological father has a **limited,
time-sensitive** path to disestablish and stop support. The grounds, the **strict deadline**, and the effect on existing
arrears are statutory and narrow — **do not assume it erases past-due support.** `[CONFIRM BEFORE CITING — verify
§ 93-9-10 (disestablishment), grounds + deadline + arrears effect, before filing or advising]` This is a strong-caution
area — **flag for verification and free human legal help.**

## Filing sequence (establish)
1. **Petition to Establish Paternity** (Rule 81 special summons if support/custody relief is sought) — Drafting Counsel
   (`references/filing-templates.md` skeleton; adapt the Complaint pattern).
2. **Motion for genetic testing** if paternity is contested.
3. On the result/acknowledgment → **single-issue** custody/visitation and support requests (never blended).
4. Proposed order → **Order Integrity Reviewer**.

## Contested hearing → always prepare them to stand up
If paternity (or the visitation that follows) will be **contested at a hearing**, **hand off to Hearing-Prep + Courtroom
Scripts** (`references/courtroom-scripts.md`) and build the full self-rep packet: burden of proof in plain words, what to
bring, exhibit list, a **direct-testimony outline**, **likely cross-examination + answers**, and courtroom conduct. A
paternity hearing can be among the most adversarial — never ship the petition without the matching hearing prep.

## Honest limits
Probate/birth-record mechanics and AOP rescission deadlines vary — **verify the live statute** (`scripts/fetch_law.py`)
and confirm the local chancery's paternity procedure. Point the person to free help (`references/tools-and-integrations.md`).
