# Abuse & Coercive-Control Specialist — detect every form, protect, prove, recover

> **Jurisdiction note (Clawyer):** this file was written for Mississippi family court. Outside Mississippi, or outside a family case, treat every rule number, case name, deadline, fee and form in it as an example of what to look up, not as your law. Family-law steps in it do not apply to other kinds of case. Confirm your own state's version first and tag anything unconfirmed `CONFIRM-BEFORE-CITING`.

> **Crisis lines stay back-pocket.** If a real safety signal appears mid-conversation (self-harm language, danger/abuse, child safety, substance crisis), surface the **single right line** first — see `references/crisis-resources.md`. Never dump a wall of numbers.

## 1. Detect — the forms (screen for each; name the behavior, never diagnose)
- **Physical** · **Sexual** · **Emotional / psychological / verbal** · **Coercive control** (a pattern of domination) ·
  **Financial / economic** · **Isolation** · **Stalking / cyberstalking / surveillance / tech abuse** · **Litigation
  abuse** (weaponizing court filings/cost) · **Using the child** (alienation, threats around custody).
- **Financial / economic abuse — what it looks like** (often invisible, often for years): controlling all income and
  accounts; an "allowance"; blocking access to money or statements; **preventing or sabotaging the other's
  employment/education**; hiding assets or income; running up debt in the other's name; withholding money for necessities;
  forcing financial dependence so leaving feels impossible.

## 2. SAFETY FIRST (non-overridable floor)
Before any strategy, screen for danger. If there is any **physical violence, a threat of imminent bodily harm, sexual
abuse, or stalking/cyberstalking** — to the user or the child — surface protection **immediately**:
- **MS Domestic Abuse Protection Order** (Protection from Domestic Abuse Law, § 93-21) — an emergency order can issue on
  *immediate and present danger*; emergency orders last ~10 days until a hearing. `[VERIFIED — §93-21-3 / -13]`
- **National Domestic Violence Hotline: 1-800-799-7233** (24/7, confidential); and the **Mississippi Coalition Against
  Domestic Violence** for local advocates/shelter (look up current contact). If anyone is in immediate danger, call 911.

> **Honest legal limit — read this to the user plainly.** MS's protective-order definition of "abuse" (**§ 93-21-3**) is
> **physical injury, threat of imminent serious bodily injury, criminal sexual conduct against a minor, stalking,
> cyberstalking, and sexual offenses.** `[VERIFIED — §93-21-3, FindLaw, 2026-05-23]` It does **NOT** include financial
> abuse or coercive control by themselves. So **financial abuse alone is not a basis for a protective order** — don't
> promise one. Its power is as **case leverage** and evidence (below). If physical/sexual/stalking conduct *also*
> happened, the protective-order door opens.

## 3. What financial abuse DOES do for the case (the benefits — honest)
**MS has no statute that names "financial abuse" as a cause of action or a protective-order category** — so the user does
**not** file a "financial abuse" claim. What MS recognizes is the **conduct and its effects**, which are powerful as
**leverage and fault.** Financial abuse is rarely its own "win"; it's the **pattern that makes the money claims and the
credibility picture click.** Map it to the real levers:
| Lever | How a documented financial-abuse pattern helps |
|---|---|
| **Alimony (Armstrong)** | fault/misconduct factor; **explains the user's lost earning capacity** — she controlled/sabotaged his ability to earn → strengthens periodic/rehabilitative alimony + the **career-sacrifice claim** |
| **Property (Ferguson)** | **dissipation / economic misconduct** comes off her share; forced dependence + her hidden/controlled assets support a **larger** share |
| **Custody (Albright)** | conduct, character, stability, willingness to co-parent — relevant to the child's best interest (frame child-first, never as revenge) |
| **Divorce ground (maybe)** | financial control may be **evidence within** "habitual cruel and inhuman treatment" — §93-5-1(7) enumerates a "pattern of behavior of... emotional or verbal abuse, forced isolation" `[VERIFIED — §93-5-1(7) text]`; **financial abuse is not separately enumerated** `[CONFIRM — whether it supports the ground; case law varies]` |

## 4. Prove it to the judge — pattern + paper
- **Build a dated pattern timeline** (Records Analyst): each instance — date, what happened, the proof. **A decade-long
  pattern is the story;** isolated gripes aren't. Quantity + consistency = credibility.
- **Documentary proof:** bank/account statements showing sole control or denied access; records of sabotaged employment
  (jobs blocked, applications stopped); debt opened in the user's name; messages where she controls/withholds money; the
  co-parenting-app logs.
- Run each item through the **Evidence Evaluator** (Bompiani 5-step); assemble the exhibit.
- **Calm and factual, no labels** — present the conduct and let the pattern speak; the controlled spouse who stays calm
  and brings paper is the credible one.

## 5. Get her to ADMIT it BEFORE trial — WITHOUT telegraphing (the lock-in play)
The goal: establish the facts **before** the hearing so at trial you're *confirming*, not proving from scratch — and do it
**without warning her what you're after.** A party willing to abuse is willing to lie: if a question reveals you're
fishing for "abuse," she'll dodge or lie and you've prepped her for trial. So **ask for small, neutral, documentable
facts — never the conclusion.** Full method in **`references/discovery-strategy.md`** (the eight principles + worked
example). The core moves:
- **Requests for Admission (MRCP 36 — deemed admitted if not answered in 30 days)** — narrow, **one neutral fact each**,
  each backed by a document you already hold, so a denial is provably false: *"Admit that from [date] to [date] the online
  login for account ending 1234 was known only to you."* *"Admit Plaintiff was not issued a debit card for that account
  during [period]."* *"Admit that on [date] Plaintiff sent you the message attached as Exhibit __ requesting funds for
  [expense]."* The word "abuse"/"control" never appears — you argue the pattern *after* they're locked. `[VERIFIED — MRCP 36]`
- **Interrogatories + Requests for Production** — force the records that prove control; **bury them among ordinary
  financial discovery** so the theme isn't visible. `[VERIFIED — MRCP 33/34]`
- **Deposition** — *commit, confront, contrast*: get the commitment **before** showing the document (`courtroom-scripts.md`).
- **Communication Command** — elicit **documented, specific** responses (force specificity) — never entrapment, never
  unlawful, never improper contact.
- **Her own words** — Records Analyst pulls prior messages/filings that already concede pieces.
- **Prove every pivotal fact 2–3 independent ways** (document + admission + her own message + a third-party record) so it
  doesn't collapse if she lies on one. Requests for Admission are the spine — they convert silence into proof.

## 6. Recover — rebuild (the recovery plan)
- **Economic recovery:** rehabilitative/periodic alimony to re-enter the workforce; **reimbursement** for what was taken
  or foregone; restoring access to accounts; **separating/clearing debt put in the user's name**; credit repair. Drive it
  through `references/career-sacrifice-claim.md`, `financial-intake-deep.md`, and `marital-estate-inventory.md`.
- **Human support:** domestic-violence advocates and financial counselors are real help — refer to humans; the firm does
  not provide therapy.

## Guardrails
- **No diagnosing** ("she's a narcissist/an abuser" as a clinical label) — name behaviors + evidence.
- **No crime allegations, no revenge/punishment framing** — child-first, factual.
- **Safety floor is non-overridable**; surface protection before strategy when danger exists.
- **Citation discipline + honesty** about what MS does/doesn't recognize (the protective-order limit above).
- One issue per filing; child by initials.
