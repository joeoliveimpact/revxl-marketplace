# Child Snapshot + Daily Parenting Log (the firm's own documentation tools)

> **Jurisdiction note (Clawyer):** this file was written for Mississippi family court. Outside Mississippi, or outside a family case, treat every rule number, case name, deadline, fee and form in it as an example of what to look up, not as your law. Family-law steps in it do not apply to other kinds of case. Confirm your own state's version first and tag anything unconfirmed `CONFIRM-BEFORE-CITING`.

Two original tools that turn a parent's involvement into **dated, provable evidence** mapped to the best-interest
factors. (Concept-inspired by the idea that judges reward *demonstrated, documented* involvement — built fresh here, not
copied from any source.) The **Child Snapshot** is the standing picture of the child; the **Daily Log** is the running
record. Together they show a calm, organized, hands-on parent.

## Why these win
Proof beats assertion. A parent who can show the pediatrician's name, the IEP terms, the bedtime routine, and a dated log
of caregiving **demonstrates** primary involvement without having to argue it. Contemporaneous (recorded same-day) beats
reconstructed-from-memory every time.

---

## TOOL 1 — The Child Snapshot (a living reference doc; update as things change)

**Identity & basics:** the child's or children's **initials only for anything filed** (e.g., A.B.), age/DOB, grade, school, residence history.
**Health:** conditions, allergies; medications (name / dose / schedule / who administers); pediatrician + specialists (name, contact, last visit); therapy/services; dental/vision; dietary needs; upcoming appointments.
**Education:** school, teacher(s); IEP/504 details + accommodations; recent grades; attendance; behavior notes; who handles school contact.
**Daily routine:** wake/sleep; meals; homework; screen limits; bedtime ritual.
**Activities & social:** sports/clubs/lessons/religious (schedule, location, who transports); close friends; community ties.
**Emotional world:** comfort people/objects; traditions; pets; observed needs.
**Caregiving map:** for each core task (feeding, school, medical, bedtime, homework, transport) — *who does it now / who did it historically.*
**Providers & contacts directory:** doctors, school, sitters, emergency contacts.
**Proof index:** for each major claim, where the evidence lives (file/photo/message).
**Last updated:** date + by whom.

---

## TOOL 2 — The Daily Parenting Log (keep it short enough to actually do every day)

| Field | Why it's evidence |
|---|---|
| **Date / day** | Contemporaneous, dated record — more credible than memory. |
| **Time with child / exchanges** | Proves parenting-time exercised; rebuts "absent parent" claims (continuity of care). |
| **Caregiving acts** (meals, school run, homework, bath, bedtime, appointments) | Concrete hands-on involvement (parenting skills, continuity). |
| **Child's health / mood / notes** | Shows attentiveness; flags issues early (emotional ties). |
| **Communication with other parent** (method, factual summary, tone) | Documents co-parenting effort and any breakdowns (willingness to cooperate). |
| **Notable events / incidents** (factual, dated) | Captures safety-relevant facts neutrally and contemporaneously. |
| **Proof attached** (photo, receipt, screenshot, message ID) | Each entry can be corroborated — claim + evidence. |
| **Follow-up needed** | Keeps the parent ahead of appointments and deadlines. |

### The five rules that make a log admissible-grade
1. **Contemporaneous** — write it the same day. 2. **Factual** — no labels, conclusions, insults, or motive. 3. **Specific** — dates, times, names. 4. **Corroborated** — reference the proof. 5. **Neutral tone** — it reads like a record, not an attack. *A calm, consistent log is itself evidence of a stable, organized parent.*

## How the firm uses these
Build the Child Snapshot during deep intake (`references/custody-intake-deep.md`); start the Daily Log immediately and
remind the parent to keep it. Feed both to the Custody Strategist (Albright matrix), Evidence Specialist (exhibit set),
and GAL-Interaction specialist (hand the GAL an organized picture). End with a NEXT ACTION.
