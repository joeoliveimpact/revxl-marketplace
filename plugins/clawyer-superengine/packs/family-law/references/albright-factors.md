# Albright Factors — MS Child Custody Checklist

> **Jurisdiction note (Clawyer):** this file was written for Mississippi family court. Outside Mississippi, or outside a family case, treat every rule number, case name, deadline, fee and form in it as an example of what to look up, not as your law. Family-law steps in it do not apply to other kinds of case. Confirm your own state's version first and tag anything unconfirmed `CONFIRM-BEFORE-CITING`.

**Authority:** *Albright v. Albright*, 437 So. 2d 1003 (Miss. 1983) `[CONFIRM BEFORE CITING — settled MS custody standard; cite is the standard exception but confirm the pinpoint before filing]`.

Mississippi chancellors decide custody by the **best interest of the child**, analyzed through the Albright factors.
**Every custody or parenting-time filing must address the full list** — a chancellor who sees a factor ignored has a
reason to deny. For each factor, state the fact, the source/exhibit that proves it, and tie it to the child's stability
(the minor child appears by initials only — initials per the case profile) — never to either parent's fairness or to the
other parent's character beyond what the evidence shows.

**Apply to the facts in the user's case profile (`case-profile.md`).**

## The factors

1. **Age, health, and sex of the child.**
2. **Continuity of care** prior to separation — who has been the primary caregiver, and what care has each parent
   actually provided.
3. **Parenting skills** and willingness/capacity to provide primary care.
4. **Employment of the parent** and responsibilities of that employment. *(Example: if the opposing party claims a
   parent's job prevents school transportation, meet this factor with a concrete, specific schedule showing it does not.)*
5. **Physical and mental health and age of the parents.**
6. **Emotional ties** of parent and child.
7. **Moral fitness** of the parents. *(Frame from evidence only; no moral judgments, no crime allegations.)*
8. **Home, school, and community record of the child.** *(Pull the child's current school, grade, and community ties
   from the case profile.)*
9. **Preference of the child** at an age sufficient to express a preference (MS: generally 12+).
10. **Stability of the home environment and employment** of each parent.
11. **Other relevant factors** — including the **willingness of each parent to support the child's relationship with the
    other parent** (continuity of medical care and information access maps here; log each blocked-access instance with a
    date).

## Drafting notes — apply to the user's facts

**Apply to the facts in the user's case profile (`case-profile.md`).**

- The **temporary order** terms vs. the other parent's actual enforcement is often a central custody issue — present it
  as a documented pattern (calendar, co-parenting app logs), not a grievance. **Verify the current order's terms against
  the case profile / matter file before asserting them in a filing.**
- Map parenting-time denials to factor 2 (continuity) and factor 11 (support of the relationship).
- Map blocked medical records/information to factor 11.
- Relief = a **specific proposed schedule** (dates, times, exchanges, holidays), not "more time."

**Always one issue per filing.** A custody motion does not mention support or property.
