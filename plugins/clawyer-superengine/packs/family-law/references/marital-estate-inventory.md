# Marital Estate Inventory (the firm's money-side documentation tool)

> **Jurisdiction note (Clawyer):** this file was written for Mississippi family court. Outside Mississippi, or outside a family case, treat every rule number, case name, deadline, fee and form in it as an example of what to look up, not as your law. Family-law steps in it do not apply to other kinds of case. Confirm your own state's version first and tag anything unconfirmed `CONFIRM-BEFORE-CITING`.

The financial equivalent of the Child Snapshot: a complete, organized picture of the marital estate so **nothing is
missed and every claim has proof.** Build it during the deep financial intake and keep it updated.

## The inventory (every asset and debt)
| Item | Marital / Separate | Value (how determined) | In whose name | Acquired (when / how) | Debt against it | Proof on file |
|------|--------------------|------------------------|---------------|-----------------------|-----------------|---------------|
Categories to cover: **real estate** (marital home + any other); **retirement** (each account separately — 401(k),
pension, IRA, PERS); **bank/investment** accounts; **vehicles**; **business interests**; **valuable personal property**;
and a matching list of **debts** (mortgage, cards, loans, medical, taxes).

## Tracing index (for separate-property claims)
For anything claimed as **separate** (inheritance, gift, pre-marital): record the **asset → its source → where it went →
the proof** (deposit records, deed, the other spouse's written admission). Properly traced separate property survives
even when it was commingled — the tracing is what wins the credit.

## Running tallies
- **Solo-payment running total** (e.g., a mortgage paid alone) → potential credit at division.
- **Net marital estate** and **each side's proposed division** — updated as values come in.

## Valuation discipline
Use **court-standard** valuations only: a real **appraisal** for the home, **account statements** for finances, a
**QDRO / coverture-fraction** calc for retirement. A **Zillow estimate** and a naive **"net-difference"** retirement
split are **not** court-standard — flag and challenge them.

## Feeds
Forensic Accountant + Asset-Tracing (build/verify it), Drafting Counsel (the Ferguson distribution argument), Order
Integrity Reviewer (check any proposed division against this), and Settlement/Mediation. End with a NEXT ACTION.
