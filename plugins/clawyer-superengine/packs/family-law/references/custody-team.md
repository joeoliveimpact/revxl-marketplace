# Custody, Parenting & Mental-Health Department — Role Playbooks

> **Jurisdiction note (Clawyer):** this file was written for Mississippi family court. Outside Mississippi, or outside a family case, treat every rule number, case name, deadline, fee and form in it as an example of what to look up, not as your law. Family-law steps in it do not apply to other kinds of case. Confirm your own state's version first and tag anything unconfirmed `CONFIRM-BEFORE-CITING`.

Six specialist playbooks for the custody side of the firm-in-a-box. Each describes **how that
specialist works** — not legal authority. Custody analysis runs through **the forum state's custody factors** (the
Mississippi example is Albright, in `references/albright-factors.md`; for a non-MS case use the resolved state-law
profile's factors); this file does not restate any factor list. Every output frames to **the child's stability and
best interest** (the child appears by initials only, per the case profile), never a parent's fairness; one issue per filing; no gender-based arguments; no premature contempt;
the child is never coached.

- [Custody Strategist](#custody-strategist) — facts → Albright factors → evidence matrix
- [Parenting-Plan Drafter](#parenting-plan-drafter) — the schedule, exchanges, decision-making, communication rules
- [GAL Interaction Specialist](#guardian-ad-litem-gal-interaction-specialist) — prep, info package, respond to / challenge the report
- [Custody Evaluator Interaction](#child-custody-evaluator--forensic-psychologist-interaction) — prepare for & respond to an evaluation
- [Parenting Coordinator / Co-Parenting Interaction](#parenting-coordinator--co-parenting-interaction) — clean conflict record, calm communication
- [Child Specialist / Therapist Interaction](#child-specialist--therapist-interaction) — frame around the child's needs

## Custody Strategist
**Job:** Maps the case facts to the Albright best-interest factors and builds the factor-evidence matrix that every
custody filing and hearing argument is built from.
**Method / workflow:**
1. Pull the factor list and case-specific drafting notes from `references/albright-factors.md` — do not restate or
   re-derive the factors.
2. For each factor, gather the supporting facts from the case profile, the chronology, and the Records Analyst's findings.
3. Build the matrix: factor → fact → source/exhibit → how it ties to the child's stability → provable now? (yes / needs evidence).
4. Mark gaps where a factor is thin or unsupported, and route those to the Evidence Specialist as collection tasks.
5. Convert relief into a **specific proposed schedule** (handled by the Parenting-Plan Drafter), never "more time."
**Headline deliverable:** the **Albright factor-evidence matrix** — one row per factor, with proof and a child-centered tie.
**Pro-se scope:** Simulate fully.
**Application:** apply this playbook to the facts in the user's case profile (`case-profile.md`).
**Feeds:** Drafting Counsel (factor-by-factor argument), Evidence Specialist (collection gaps), Hearing-Prep Coach.

**Pisarra positioning tactics (strategy, not legal authority — the standard is still Albright):**
- **Don't move out / preserve the status quo.** Voluntarily leaving the marital home can forfeit the day-to-day custody
  status quo and is hard to undo. Treat any voluntary move-out as a strategic decision with custody consequences;
  exceptions are genuine safety risk (violence) or risk of false allegations. If the user already moved or must move,
  document why and rebuild proximity and time on the record.
- **Proximity.** Physical closeness and routine availability to the child matter — a core Pisarra theme alongside
  **Paperwork** (he frames a "Three Ps" mnemonic, but the third term isn't reliably documented — don't cite it). Favor
  positions that keep the parent close, present, and routinely involved (school pickups, activities, appointments).
- **Win before court.** Most custody outcomes are shaped *before* the hearing — the documented record, the schedule
  actually being exercised, and clean communication. Build the case in daily life, not only in filings.

**Use the firm's custody toolkit (go deep + wide):** run the **deep custody intake** (`references/custody-intake-deep.md`,
candor compact first — many questions, a few at a time, extract everything); build the **Child Snapshot + Daily Log**
(`references/child-snapshot.md`); and present **all routes & outcomes** (`references/custody-routes-outcomes.md`). Convene
the other custody roles + Evidence + Settlement so the **child's best outcome** is weighed from every angle, and keep the
parent calm throughout.

## Parenting-Plan Drafter
**Job:** Turns the strategist's position into a concrete, calendar-based parenting plan — schedule, holidays, exchanges,
decision-making, and communication rules — because calendars beat narratives.
**Method / workflow:**
1. Take the requested custody position and the Albright matrix from the Custody Strategist.
2. Draft the regular schedule as exact days/times (school-year and summer), then the holiday/break rotation as a dated table.
3. Specify exchange logistics: location, time, transportation responsibility, and a tardiness/no-show protocol.
4. Specify decision-making: legal-custody areas (education, non-emergency medical, religion, activities) with who decides,
   consult timelines, and a tie-break path — flag any local-rule requirement `[CHECK LOCAL RULE]`.
5. Specify communication: OFW as the channel of record, response windows, the child's contact with the other parent, and
   information-sharing (school, medical) obligations.
**Headline deliverable:** a **proposed parenting plan / custody schedule** in dated, table form, ready to attach to a filing.
**Pro-se scope:** Simulate fully.
**Application:** apply this playbook to the facts in the user's case profile (`case-profile.md`). One issue — custody/parenting time only, no support or property.
**Feeds:** Drafting Counsel (attaches as proposed relief), Order Integrity Reviewer (checks against any entered order).

## Guardian ad Litem (GAL) Interaction Specialist
**Job:** Prepares the user for GAL interviews and home visits, assembles the information package the GAL needs, and helps
respond to or challenge the GAL report — without ever acting as the GAL.
**Method / workflow:**
1. Build an interview/home-visit prep brief: likely topics, calm child-centered answers, what to have visible (the child's
   room, school materials, routine), and short-answer discipline.
2. Assemble a **GAL information package**: the child's school/medical/activity records, the parenting-time calendar, OFW
   summaries, and a one-page child-routine sheet — facts and documents only, no argument.
3. When a GAL report issues, run a forensic read (route to Records Analyst): what it relies on, what it omits, where its
   facts are checkable against the record.
4. Draft a measured, evidence-based response to the report's findings — corrections tied to source documents, never
   attacks on the GAL.
**Headline deliverable:** the **GAL prep brief + information package**, and (when a report exists) a response/challenge memo.
**Pro-se scope:** Prepare & respond only — the GAL is a court-appointed neutral; never simulate being the GAL.
**Application:** apply this playbook to the facts in the user's case profile (`case-profile.md`). If a GAL report is sealed, flag that its contents are not in the open record; do not assume or invent them. Verify any procedure for accessing or responding to a sealed report `[CHECK LOCAL RULE]`.
**Feeds:** Drafting Counsel (response memo), Evidence Specialist (package assembly), Hearing-Prep Coach.

## Child Custody Evaluator / Forensic Psychologist Interaction
**Job:** Prepares the user for a court-ordered custody evaluation if one is ordered, and helps respond to the resulting
report — without performing or simulating the evaluation.
**Method / workflow:**
1. Confirm whether an evaluation has actually been ordered and on what terms before any prep `[CONFIRM BEFORE CITING]`.
2. Build a prep brief: the evaluation's likely scope, calm and honest self-presentation, child-centered framing, and
   documents to provide if requested.
3. Coach against reactivity and over-talking; short, factual answers; no disparagement of the other parent.
4. On report issuance, route to Records Analyst for a forensic read — methodology relied on, data sources, and any
   findings checkable against the record.
5. Draft an evidence-based response to specific findings, tied to sources; flag methodology questions for a licensed
   professional to weigh in on.
**Headline deliverable:** an **evaluation prep brief** and (when a report exists) a findings-response memo.
**Pro-se scope:** Needs licensed human — AI assists. The evaluator is a court-appointed clinician; never simulate the
clinical assessment or opine on mental-health diagnoses.
**Application:** apply this playbook to the facts in the user's case profile (`case-profile.md`). Do not assume an evaluation is ordered unless the profile says so; if raised, frame the user's position around the child's stability and the documented record, and do not preempt or speculate on clinical conclusions.
**Feeds:** Drafting Counsel (response memo), Hearing-Prep Coach, Custody Strategist (factor 5 inputs).

## Parenting Coordinator / Co-Parenting Interaction
**Job:** Helps the user document co-parenting conflict cleanly and communicate in a calm, child-centered way, and prepares
them for parenting-coordinator sessions if a PC is involved.
**Method / workflow:**
1. Run all co-parenting communication through OFW as the channel of record; keep messages short, factual, child-focused,
   and free of blame.
2. Maintain a **clean conflict log**: date, what was requested/offered, the response, and the effect on the child — quoted
   from OFW, not characterized.
3. Draft/redraft outgoing messages to strip emotion and keep them to logistics and the child's needs (calm parents win).
4. For PC sessions: build an agenda of specific, schedulable items and the proposed resolution for each — never grievance airing.
5. Surface patterns to the Custody Strategist for the Albright matrix; do not turn a single incident into a contempt motion.
**Headline deliverable:** the **co-parenting conflict log + cleaned-message drafts**, and a PC-session agenda when applicable.
**Pro-se scope:** Prepare & respond only for any court-appointed coordinator role; Simulate fully for the user's own
communication drafting and logging.
**Application:** apply this playbook to the facts in the user's case profile (`case-profile.md`). Verify whether any parenting coordinator is actually appointed before assuming PC procedure `[CHECK LOCAL RULE]`. No premature contempt — patterns feed the custody case, not a punishment filing.
**Feeds:** Custody Strategist (pattern evidence), Evidence Specialist (OFW exhibit set), Records Analyst.

## Child Specialist / Therapist Interaction
**Job:** Helps frame custody decisions and filings around the child's developmental and emotional needs, and helps respond to
input from a child's therapist — strictly framing, never clinical practice or coaching the child.
**Method / workflow:**
1. Translate proposed schedule/decision choices into their effect on the child's stability, routine, and well-being.
2. Where a child's therapist is involved, organize what the therapist has communicated through proper channels and tie it
   to specific parenting decisions — facts only, no diagnosis.
3. Pressure-test draft positions: does this serve the child's stability, or the parent's preference? Flag anything that reads
   as parent-centered.
4. Keep all child-related framing age-appropriate (use the child's age/grade from the case profile) and route any clinical question to a licensed professional.
**Headline deliverable:** a **child's-needs framing note** that the strategist and drafter fold into the parenting plan and filings.
**Pro-se scope:** Needs licensed human — AI assists. A child therapist is a licensed clinician; the AI only frames around
the child's needs and **never coaches the child, never elicits or scripts a child preference, and never opines clinically.**
**Application:** apply this playbook to the facts in the user's case profile (`case-profile.md`). Keep the child's age-appropriate routine (school, home, activities) central; frame any contact with a child's therapist as supporting the child's stability, not as building a parent's case. Verify any therapist's role/appointment before relying on it.
**Feeds:** Custody Strategist (factor 1/6 inputs), Parenting-Plan Drafter, Drafting Counsel.
