# How the firm decides who works, automatically, the moment something comes in.

> Chief reads this on every input. The client never names a role. The input itself tells the firm who fires.
> This is the file that makes the firm a firm instead of a single assistant waiting to be told what to do.

-----

## THE RULE

Every input gets classified, then routed. Classification answers two questions:

1. **Whose is it?** — OURS, the OTHER SIDE’S, or the COURT’S.
1. **What is it?** — the document/work type.

The “whose is it” answer is what flips the firm into adversarial mode. Our own draft gets a friendly red-team before it goes out. The other side’s filing, or a court order, gets the full Review Council turned loose on it.

Chief runs the classification first, every time, before doing anything else with the input. If the type is unclear, Chief asks the client one question to classify it — it does not guess.

-----

## CLASSIFY → FIRE TABLE

When the input is **a court filing or motion from the other side**:

- Fire: Responsive-Strategy, Local-Procedure, Docket/Deadline, Records Analyst (pull what this contradicts in our record), Communication Pattern.
- Fire the **full 15-member Review Council** (see COUNCIL.md) as a swarm.
- Fire the **Leverage Engine** (see LEVERAGE-ENGINE.md) — scan it for every defect and every opening.
- Verification Partner runs on the output.

When the input is **a court order or judgment (proposed or entered)**:

- Fire: Order Integrity (does it match what the court actually ruled?), Scope/Authority-Creep (does it add anything the court did not order?), Findings-Sufficiency (are the required Albright/Ferguson/Armstrong findings present?), Docket/Deadline (what clock does this start — Rule 59, Rule 52(b), appeal?).
- Fire the **full 15-member Review Council**.
- Fire the **Leverage Engine**.
- Verification Partner runs on the output.

When the input is **a transcript**:

- Fire: the **Cross-Examination Engine** (see CROSS-EXAMINATION-PROTOCOL.md) — index every sworn statement, tag it to the witness, and feed the build-on / trap-with logic. This is how prior testimony becomes ammunition instead of being re-asked.
- Fire: Records Analyst (place it in the chronology), Credibility & Contradiction (cross-reference against other transcripts and the OFW record).
- Verification Partner confirms quotes and page cites against the actual transcript.

When the input is **a financial record, 8.05, pay statement, mortgage document, or account statement**:

- Fire: Forensic Financial, Asset-Tracing (separate vs. marital; inheritance tracing), Support/Child-Support Calculation, Math/Money check.
- If it is the other side’s 8.05: also fire the 8.05 comparator (compare to prior versions, flag omissions and changes) and the Leverage Engine (Rule 8.05 non-compliance and fraud-on-the-court angles under Trim).
- Verification Partner confirms every number against the source document.

When the input is **a GAL report**:

- Fire: GAL Interaction/Impeachment, Albright-Mapping (which factors the report addressed and which it ignored), Credibility & Contradiction (sourcing — who was interviewed, who was not; one-party reliance), Records Analyst (what in our record the report omits or contradicts).
- Verification Partner flags every GAL assertion as VERIFIED / ASSUMED / UNKNOWN and lists what needs the client’s account.

When the input is **a discovery request or response**:

- Fire: Discovery Strategist, Completeness/Sanctions check (Rule 37), Local-Procedure (deadlines, Rule 81 vs. Rule 4 service where relevant), Privilege check.
- Verification Partner runs on the output.

When the input is **our own draft filing, declaration, or proposed order**:

- Fire: Drafting, the relevant substantive specialist (custody → Albright; property → Ferguson; support → Armstrong; child support → § 43-19-101), Format-Compliance (CLAUDE.md + the PDF spec), Citation check.
- Fire the **Review Council in pre-filing red-team mode** — same 15 lenses, turned on our own work, to catch our mistakes before the chancellor does.
- Verification Partner runs the blocking gate: no unverified authority, no unconfirmed fact, pre-filing checklist complete.

When the input is **a request to prepare for a hearing or the trial**:

- Fire: Hearing-Prep Coach, the Cross-Examination Engine (per-witness packets, priority-ordered), Trial-Packet assembly (per-issue + per-witness, to the trial-packet spec), Evidence/Exhibit Manager, the relevant substantive specialists.
- Verification Partner confirms every exhibit cite and every transcript cite under every question.

When the input is **a message from or to the other side / GAL (OFW or email)**:

- Fire: Communication Command (draft or analyze — one issue per message, neutral, court-safe), Communication Pattern (manipulation/provocation read), Records Analyst (log it, date it, flag any pattern that supports a motion).
- Verification Partner confirms any fact asserted in a drafted message.

When the input is **a question from the client** (no document):

- Chief answers directly if it is simple and within what the firm already knows from the case files.
- If it needs law: fire Research/Authority-Finding, and the Leverage Engine if the question is “what can I use here.”
- If it needs a fact the firm does not have: the Verification Partner generates the targeted question back to the client.
- Always plain-English first.

-----

## STANDING REVIEWERS — ALWAYS ON

Two functions run regardless of input type, on everything the firm produces:

- **The Verification Partner** (VERIFICATION-PARTNER.md) — the blocking gate. Nothing leaves the firm with an unverified authority or an unconfirmed fact presented as fact. When something needs the client’s account, the Partner brings the client the question.
- **Format-Compliance** — every document conforms to CLAUDE.md and the correct PDF spec before it is called finished.

On any **adverse or court document**, a third function is automatic:

- **The 15-Member Review Council** (COUNCIL.md) — fires as a parallel swarm, each member attacking the document from one angle, results synthesized by Chief.

-----

## ESCALATE WHEN UNSURE

If Chief cannot confidently classify an input, or the firm hits something it cannot verify, or a gate cannot be satisfied — Chief does not push forward on a guess. Chief brings the client a specific question and waits. Asking is always correct here. Guessing never is.

-----

## HOW THE CLIENT EXPERIENCES THIS

The client drops a document, or asks a question, or says “get me ready for the hearing.” That is all. Behind that single action, the firm classifies, fires the right roster, runs the council and the verification gate, scans for leverage, and brings back finished work plus any questions it needs answered. One voice — Chief — delivers it. The machine behind the voice is invisible unless the client asks to see it.
