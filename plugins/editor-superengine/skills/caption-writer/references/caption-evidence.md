# Caption evidence

The numbers behind caption-writer's takeaways. They come from a study of 189 outlier reels against 1,486 control reels from 51 creators (08.03.26). An outlier is a reel at 3x or more of its own creator's median views; the control is every other reel from the same creators. Comparing each creator against themselves takes out account size.

**Read every number here as correlational.** The creators sat in one niche (AI tools for business), so the keyword lists are that niche's. The study shows what outliers tend to do, not that doing it causes reach. Use it to place words with confidence. Don't quote it to a client as proof that moving a keyword earlier will lift their views.

## 1. Keyword in the first two sentences

| signal | outliers | control | lift |
|---|---|---|---|
| named keyword in sentence 1 | 33% | 22% | +10.7pp |
| **named keyword in first 2 sentences** | **47%** | **33%** | **+14.1pp** |
| named keyword in first 4 sentences | 54% | 40% | +14.7pp |
| CTA is sentence 1 | 42% | 34% | +8.1pp |

A keyword in the opening separates outliers more strongly than opening with the CTA does, and the gain levels off at sentence 2. So sentence 1 may spend itself on the CTA (42% of outliers do), as long as the keyword has landed by the end of sentence 2. A typical shape: sentence 1 is `Comment "GUIDE" for the full guide`, sentence 2 names the topic.

## 2. Keywords versus audience qualifiers

Measured in the same corpus and against live TikTok search (median views of the top results for that search):

| term | corpus lift | live search |
|---|---|---|
| `prompt(s)` | +7.1pp | `claude prompts`: 28,267 |
| `claude` | +4.4pp | (not run) |
| `business` | -4.1pp | `ai prompts for business`: 2,702 |
| `coach/coaching` | +0.2pp (only 5 cases, can't be tested) | `ai for coaches`: 1,079 |

`ai for coaches` returned real coach content (84% of results mention coaching) that gets very little reach. The qualifier isn't wrong, it's a small lane. A coach searching that phrase is a high-intent viewer, and a comment-to-DM funnel converts on intent, not views. So: **keywords earn reach, qualifiers earn lead quality.** Keywords go in sentences 1-2, qualifiers in 3-4.

A bare `ai` measured **-6.1pp**: common enough to be well sampled, too generic to separate anything.

## 3. Which words, not how many

Outliers carried a median of 7 content words in their first 2 sentences, the same as the control (14 vs 13 across four sentences). Winners don't stuff more words in. What separated them was which terms: `code` +6.4pp, `claude code` +6.1pp, `prompts` +4.9pp, `prompt` +4.5pp, `claude` +4.4pp, `chatgpt` +4.1pp. Hence 2-3 specific, searched terms rather than a dense block.

Caveat: this partly measures whether the reel is *about* a searched thing, not where the keyword sits. It is topic choice as much as caption craft.

## 4. Caption length

Across 189 winners (3x or more of own median), 1,291 middle and 195 losers (0.5x or less of own median):

| length | winners | losers | win minus lose | winner median views |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| micro, up to 125 characters | 24.3% | 26.2% | -1.8pp | 175,138 |
| short, 126-300 | 18.5% | 16.4% | +2.1pp | 249,143 |
| **mid, 301-600** | 23.8% | **30.8%** | **-7.0pp** | **108,376** |
| long, 601-1,000 | 19.0% | 18.5% | +0.6pp | 226,827 |
| **very long, 1,000+** | **14.3%** | 8.2% | **+6.1pp** | 215,609 |

There is no winning length, but there is a losing one. Median length barely separates the groups (winners 458 characters, losers 420, middle 353), and the spread inside every group is huge. 301-600 characters is where losers cluster, and winners there earn the lowest median views of any band. Too long for a punchy ask, too short to stand alone as value. Under about 300 (the CTA rides on the video) or over about 1,000 (the caption is value on its own) both do better.

Caveat: small effects on about 190 reels per group, and length probably follows format rather than causing anything. Never pad a caption to reach a band.

## 5. Emoji

Openers with at least one emoji: 31% of outliers, 29% of control (+1.2pp). Median emoji count was 0 in both, and 65% of outlier captions had no emoji at all. Emoji are not a growth lever.

| emoji | outliers | control | lift | how it was used |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 👇 | 11.6% (22) | 8.5% | +3.1pp | all 22 at the end of their sentence, 91% on a CTA sentence, 20 of 22 in sentence 1 |
| 🔥 | 5.8% (11) | 0.9% | +4.9pp | 55% at sentence end, only 9% on a CTA: hype, not a pointer |

👇 works as a CTA ending: it sits at the end of the ask and points at the comment box, across 7 different creators. 🔥 has the bigger raw lift but it's a tone choice, not a mechanic. Both were checked for being one creator's habit, and neither is.
