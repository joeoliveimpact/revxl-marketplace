# Optional glass card for reels

Use 3D sparingly in reels when one short line or a number the speaker said
benefits from a pause and emphasis. Use at most one card per beat. It is an
optional overlay over the speaker, with a clear frame around it.

The glass-card-3d GPU scene brings in luminous glass, a blooming accent edge,
diagonal specular sweeps and seeded dust in front and behind the card. Depth
of field turns far dust into bokeh. Brightness is mapped once after the optical
effects with ACES filmic; a final sharp pass keeps the words clear and white.
The CSS fallback keeps its soft glow and Canvas dust. The panel fades in and
settles from a pronounced tilt into a slow breathing tilt, ending visible.
Text is limited to 28 characters; keep it short enough to read comfortably.
The accent and font follow the selected brand parameters.

Choose the top or bottom band. In the 1080 by 1920 master, the top band spans
x 60 to 960 and y 260 to 760; the bottom spans x 60 to 960 and y 1100 to 1460.
The far edges are exclusive. The entire picture, including glow and dust,
is clipped to the chosen band inside the text-safe area.

The pre-render head-band check compares that whole band with the protected
face area over the beat, including framing moves. If it would cover the
speaker's face, move the card to the other band. Without face measurements,
the check warns that the spot cannot yet be confirmed. Review it against the
speaker before rendering.

Automatic mode uses WebGL when a usable hardware context is available.
With no GPU, software graphics or a graphics failure, it automatically shows
the CSS card and canvas dust instead. A single fixed-time GPU check also uses
this fallback if the card is dark, missing, flat or low in contrast, before any
seek is served. The client still sees the glass panel,
words, accent and gentle motion. No manual GPU setup is needed. The explicit
webgl and css modes support production testing.
Tone-map-once and dead-frame ideas from viettranx/3dviz-pro-max (MIT).

The seed chooses the dust arrangement: the same seed gives the same dust
at the same time, including after a seek. Change it for a different pattern.
Keep the seed fixed when revising text or comparing renders.

## Capture-friendly design

- Use literal colours on the captured card and all its children; resolve
  brand colours into inline values rather than CSS variables.
- Use an explicit colour with alpha instead of the transparent keyword.
- Give edges thinner than four pixels a solid fill rather than a gradient.
- Keep every gradient stop at alpha 0.15 or higher.
- Mark decorative elements that cannot follow these rules data-no-capture;
  their entire subtree stays out of the captured card.
- A captured element with class scene needs an explicit background-color.
  When using shader scene capture, match that colour in its capture setup.

Rules adapted from HyperFrames (heygen-com/hyperframes, tag v0.8.184, d15a274), skills/hyperframes-animation/transitions/overview.md, Apache-2.0

The scene validator accepts category 3d (root z-index 300, layers 300-399,
three.js only through the local import map), and a reel render places a 3D
scene in the 3D band under the 2D scenes, copying the engine files into the
scene's vendor folder.
