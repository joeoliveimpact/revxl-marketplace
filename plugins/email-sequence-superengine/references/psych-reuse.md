## Adapt for cold/email context
The sales frameworks are for live calls. For email, lean on the structural sales-resistance principles
(Ben Settle vault): the reader must NOT feel hunted.
- **Anti-Pitch / Takeaway** — sell the frame, not the intent; lower the stakes of the CTA.
- **Ideal-Customer-Filter / disqualify** — pre-qualify and actively disqualify bad fits; lowers the good-fit reader's guard.
- **Indifference / Agree-with-enemy** — genuine selectivity reads as confidence.
- **Open-loop / curiosity** — forward momentum the reader chose (resistance-reduction).
- **Objection reframe** — Acknowledge → Align → Reframe → Ask (one objection per email, max).
