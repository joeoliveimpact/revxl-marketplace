# Launch Framework — 7-Day Open-Cart Sequence

Source: Email Nurture Architecture Research (Type 2). Broadcast: built once, fires to the segment when
cart opens. Specificity from coach voice + avatar pains, never individual facts.

**Objective:** drive enrollments in a defined window via structured scarcity + social proof.
**Trigger:** subscriber finishes pre-launch runway / masterclass / waitlist (tag `launch-open`).
**Cadence:** 7 emails over 7 days; intensify the final 48h. Format: text-first; D3/D4 light-HTML allowed.

| # | Send | Purpose | Lever | Subject angle | CTA |
|---|------|---------|-------|---------------|-----|
| 1 | D1 | open-cart opportunity | Opportunity/Anticipation | "doors are open" + the core transformation | apply |
| 2 | D2 | curriculum / mechanics | Cognitive Ease | "exactly how this works" (phases + support) | view blueprint |
| 3 | D3 | client case study | Social Proof/Identification | a transformation the avatar sees themselves in | read + apply |
| 4 | D4 | origin narrative | Liking/Relatability | the coach's own struggle → why this exists | apply |
| 5 | D5 | objection Q&A | Objection Prevention | top 3 objections from avatar config | read FAQ + secure spot |
| 6 | D6 (24h left) | scarcity escalation | FOMO | "closes in 24 hours" — capacity capped | submit before deadline |
| 7 | D7 (cart close) | cost of inaction | Loss Aversion | "doors close tonight" — short plain-text 4h prior | secure spot |

**Pitch + CTA per email** (see ${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/references/cta-patterns.md): D1 `soft` — apply (9, soft frame) · D2 `none` — open-loop read (3) · D3 `none/soft` — open-loop case (3) · D4 `none` — open-loop read (3) · D5 `soft` — apply after objection-strike (9) · D6 `hard` — deadline secure (10) · D7 `hard` — deadline secure (10). **Escalates soft → hard.**

**Story dose: CORE** (see ${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/references/story-engines.md). The 7-day run is a serialized arc — chain open loops day-to-day; D3 = Third-Person Proof storyline, D4 = origin/Loss&Redemption; rotate engines; optional P.S. subplot across the week. Don't make it a bullet-list announcement sequence.

**Hormozi Fast Cash upgrades (fold in IF the coach has bonuses — conditional, not mandatory):**
- **Bonus-stacking** — layer a new bonus with each step from ~D4 on; each addition lifts buy-likelihood.
- **Sales-update email** — ~D6 (or a D5.5): "X spots sold, Y left" (real numbers) — social proof + scarcity.
- **Countdown subject lines** on D6/D7 — minutes/hours remaining in the subject ("14 minutes left"). Highest-value bonus added 24h out.

**Levers:** Social Proof/Identification (avatar-similar transformations read as attainable); Regret Minimization.
**Mistakes to avoid:** skipping pre-launch warmup (cold offer); NOT mailing on deadline day (>50% of revenue lands in the final 48h).
**Benchmarks:** cart-open open 25-35%; lead→sale 1-2%; application close 20-30%.
**Companion (flag only):** SMS on D6/D7; retarget ad to openers.
