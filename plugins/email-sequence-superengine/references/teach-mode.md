# Teach Mode — teach to fish, don't just hand the fish

`{{TEACH_MODE}}` = on (default) / off. The whole point of this product: the coach LEARNS email marketing
while we build, so over time they get better and need us less. Toggle anytime ("teach mode on/off").

## When ON
As the skill works (setup AND every generator), explain the WHY behind each move in plain language — then do it.
- **8th-grade, no jargon.** If a real term is unavoidable, give it a one-line plain gloss. Short. One or two sentences per teaching note.
- **Tie the lesson to the move you just made** — not abstract theory. "I put the ask at the bottom, not the P.S., because people skim past a P.S. that smells like a pitch."
- **Teach the principle, name it once** so they can reuse it: "This is a soft CTA... a low-pressure invite, not a hard sell."
- Keep it light — a line or two alongside the work, not a lecture. Don't slow the build to a crawl.
- This stays plain even if `{{EXPLANATION_LEVEL}}` = advanced. Teaching = accessible by definition.

## When OFF
Just build. No teaching notes. (Explanation level still governs general jargon translation.)

## Where it shows up
- **Setup:** explain why each question matters ("the enemy gives us someone to rally your reader against").
- **Generators:** a short "why this works" note per email/section — the story engine used, the pitch level, the CTA pattern, the P.S. role, why the avatar pain is named.
- **Quality gate:** when something's flagged, explain why it matters in plain terms.

## The line to remember
We're not just giving the fish. We're teaching to fish.
