# Post-Call Follow-Up Framework — 4-Touch (No Close)

Source: Email Nurture Architecture Research (Type 6). Broadcast: built once, fires when a call logs
no-close. Specificity from coach voice + avatar pains, never individual facts.

**Objective:** handle objections, keep momentum, secure a decision from warm non-buyers.
**Trigger:** call logged `No Close — Follow-Up Required`.
**Cadence:** 4 touches over 17 days (3-7-7). Format: text-only (all four).

| # | Send | Purpose | Lever | Subject angle | CTA |
|---|------|---------|-------|---------------|-----|
| 1 | +2h | post-call recap | Cognitive Clarity | "your plan recap" — anchor avatar pains + next steps | confirm alignment |
| 2 | D3 | objection case study | Social Proof | a client who had the avatar's #1 objection + won | read + apply |
| 3 | D10 | gentle timeline check | Consistency | re-anchor the timeline they stated | adjust scope or quick call |
| 4 | D17 | respectful breakup | Loss Aversion | "closing your file" | self-service calendar |

**Pitch + CTA per email** (see ${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/references/cta-patterns.md): E1 `soft` — reply (confirm alignment, 1) · E2 `soft` — open-loop case (3) · E3 `soft/hard` — gated adjust or quick-call (9/7) · E4 `soft-hard` — self-service rebook breakup (8). **Soft → firmer breakup.**

**Story dose: HEAVY on E2** (see ${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/references/story-engines.md). E2 is a Third-Person Proof story — a client who carried the avatar's #1 objection and won (skepticism → struggle → triumph). E1/E3/E4 stay functional (recap, timeline, breakup), light touch only.

**Levers:** Mirrored pain vocabulary (use the avatar's words); Structured breakup.
**Mistakes to avoid:** generic "just checking in"; delaying the recap past +2h.
**Benchmarks:** E1 open 55-65%; E1 reply 20-30%; post-call close 15-25%.
**Note:** E1 recap is generic-but-specific — anchor on the avatar's shared pains, not one prospect's call.
**Companion (flag only):** SMS recap nudge at +2h.
