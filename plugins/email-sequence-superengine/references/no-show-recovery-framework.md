# No-Show Recovery Framework — 4-Touch Reschedule

Source: Email Nurture Architecture Research (Type 5). Broadcast: built once, fires when a session is
marked no-show. Specificity from coach voice + avatar pains, never individual facts.

**Objective:** recover missed sessions, reschedule without defensiveness.
**Trigger:** session marked `No-Show`.
**Cadence:** 4 touches over 10 days, starting +15 min. Format: text-only (all four).

| # | Send | Purpose | Lever | Subject angle | CTA |
|---|------|---------|-------|---------------|-----|
| 1 | +15 min | warm check-in | Empathy/Safety | "everything okay?" (<4 sentences) | reschedule (2 alt slots) |
| 2 | +24h | resource delivery | Reciprocity | "pulled this for our session" — no hard feelings | grab a spot |
| 3 | D5 | timing check | Permissive Candor | "is the timing off?" — give an easy out | pause or next month |
| 4 | D10 | clean breakup | Loss Aversion | "closing your file" — door stays open | self-service booking link |

**Pitch + CTA per email** (see ${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/references/cta-patterns.md): E1 `soft` — reply-trigger + rebook (1+7) · E2 `soft` — resource + rebook (4+7) · E3 `soft` — permission/easy-out (8) · E4 `soft` — self-service rebook (8). **Soft throughout — empathy, never hard.** (Pitch floor doesn't push these harder.)

**Story dose: OFF / touch only** (see ${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/references/story-engines.md). These are short and empathy-first (E1 <4 sentences). A story would bloat them. At most a single relatable line in E2; no full engine.

**Levers:** Empathy/Safety (bypasses defensiveness); Permission to say no.
**Mistakes to avoid:** slow follow-up (>24h loses the window); guilt-tripping ("I sat on Zoom waiting").
**Benchmarks:** reschedule recovery 20-30%; E1 open 50-60%; response 35-45%.
**Companion (flag only):** SMS at +15 min (speed-to-reschedule).
