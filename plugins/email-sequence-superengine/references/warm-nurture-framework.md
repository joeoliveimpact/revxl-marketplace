# Warm-Nurture Framework — Selectable Weekly Pattern (1-7/week, default 3)

Source: Email Nurture Architecture Research (Type 3) + Ben Settle vault. Broadcast: an evergreen repeating
weekly pattern, fires to the ongoing-nurture segment. Specificity from coach voice + avatar pains, never individual facts.

**Objective:** deliver value, build authority, generate steady discovery calls without list burnout.
**Trigger:** subscriber finishes welcome/lead-magnet/launch, tagged `nurture-ongoing`.
**Format:** text-only default. Mornings (9-11am local).

## ENTERTAINMENT-FIRST — earn the open (read ${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/references/story-engines.md — required for warm)
Warm nurture is the trust-builder and the moneymaker, and it works ONLY if people actually want to open it.
The job of each email is to be a genuinely good read... fun, surprising, worth opening for its own sake.
NOT an ad with a few lines of banter on top. **Test every email: strip the CTA — is it still worth reading?
If no, rewrite it.**
- **90/10 entertainment-to-offer.** Most sends are story/entertainment/value with a light or ZERO offer-ask.
- **Most emails do NOT sell the program.** They entertain + teach and end with a throwaway link, a "reply and tell me yours," or nothing. The offer concentrates on the ~1-in-4 invite.
- **The win is them opening tomorrow**, not today's click. Entertainment is the moat (Settle: "make emails so good people hunt for them").
It lives or dies on STORY, not bullet-point tips. Every send runs on a story engine; this is NOT a generic-tips newsletter and NOT a daily pitch.
- **Every email picks an engine** from story-engines.md (Story Selling / Analogy-Seinfeld / HSO·HIPS·PAS) and **rotates** — never the same shape two sends running.
- **Rotate the Attractive-Character storyline** across weeks (Loss&Redemption · Us-vs-Them · Before&After · Amazing Discovery · Secret-Telling · Third-Person Proof) so a long nurture never repeats its angle.
- **Chain a weekly open loop** — end one send teasing a NAMED next one; resolve it, open a fresh loop. (Zeigarnik / Settle.)
- **Run a P.S. subplot thread** (Chaperon) — a separate personal narrative living only in the P.S. across sends. Trains readers to scroll to the P.S. NOT the CTA.
- **The seam is blunt** — hard-pivot story→point→one CTA ("Anyway, here's why I bring this up:"). No subtle fade.
- **Antenna up** — source stories from the coach's real life + the VoC profile, in the coach's voice. No canned copy.

## Frequency — ask the coach (1-7/week), DEFAULT 3
Research spans the whole range: floor ~5-8 value emails/month (≈1-2/wk), canonical warm-list 2-3x/wk
(Tue/Wed/Thu), ceiling = Ben Settle daily (engaged lists). Right number depends on LIST SIZE + NICHE
appetite + the coach's content capacity. Default 3 (the researched warm-list pattern). Always keep the
80/20 value-to-pitch ratio: most emails give, ~1 in 4 invites.

Email ROLES to draw from (mix to fit the chosen count): **lesson/myth-bust** · **client story** ·
**direct invitation** · **second lesson / Q&A** · **personal/Seinfeld story** · **case study** · **contrarian/POV**.

| Freq | Pattern (roles) | Notes |
|------|-----------------|-------|
| 1/wk | value+soft-invite combined | smallest lists / low capacity |
| 2/wk | lesson (Tue) + invite (Thu) | |
| **3/wk (default)** | **lesson (Tue) + story (Wed) + invite (Thu)** | **the researched warm-list pattern** |
| 4/wk | + Q&A / second lesson | the "may be 4" tier — fine for engaged mid-size lists |
| 5/wk | + personal/Seinfeld story day | |
| 6-7/wk | daily rotation (lesson/story/myth/personal/case/POV + 1 invite) | Settle cadence; engaged lists only, watch deliverability |

**Invite count rule:** ~1 invite per 4 emails regardless of frequency (so 1/wk = invite ~monthly inside the combined email; 7/wk = ~1-2 invites/wk). Never more pitch than value.

**Length is goal-indexed — set it from the RUN'S GOAL, not a flat number (see ${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/references/copy-format-rules.md → Email length).** Ask/infer the goal first: **Reactivate** = short-heavy (5 punchy / 4 mid / 1 long per 10) · **Nurture** = balanced (3 / 6 / 1, the default) · **Prime** = long-leaning (2 / 4 / 4) · **Convert** = punchy closes (120-200). Bands: punchy 150-200 · mid 350-500 · long 700-1000. Long-form is earned + a minority (Settle's own rate ~10%); mid carries; punchy keeps rhythm. **Stagger + space** — no same-length emails back-to-back. (Anchor: measured across 2,220 Settle emails — median body ~390, NOT 150.)

**Pitch + CTA per role** (see ${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/references/cta-patterns.md) — entertainment-first, offer concentrated on the invite:
- lesson/myth-bust → the email entertains/teaches and stands alone; end with a "reply and tell me" engagement nudge (1) OR a pure-value link (3) OR nothing. NOT an offer-ramp. Under soft-floor a throwaway link MAY ride at the very bottom; under value-first, none.
- story / personal / Seinfeld / POV → same: it's a good read first. Soft/none. Engagement reply is fine; offer-ask optional and light.
- client story → open-loop (3), `soft` — still entertainment-led, not a case-study pitch.
- direct invitation → book-the-call (7), `hard`-ish. **This is the one email that sells.** ~1 in 4.
Rule of thumb: 3 of 4 sends carry no real offer-ask (engagement/value only); the 4th invites. A reply/engagement nudge is NOT a pitch. Primary offer CTA (on the invite) sits at the body close, never the P.S.

**Dormant / cold-list relaunch — do NOT open with the weekly pattern.** If the list is dormant (reactivation), the FIRST send is a re-intro with pitch=`none` (reply-ask only — see HARD NO-PITCH CONTEXTS in ${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/references/copy-format-rules.md). Then a short build-in-public reactivation arc (re-intro → invite-to-free → resource), THEN the repeating weekly pattern. Open with a pitch (even soft) on a dormant list and you trade the inbox for nothing. Scale frequency UP from 2/wk only once opens/clicks recover.

**Levers:** Reciprocity (non-generic advice → receptive reader); Conversational Directness (plain-text super-signature reads exclusive).
**Mistakes to avoid:** corporate multi-topic newsletters (dry); no CTA (trains passive consumption); over-mailing a cold/unengaged list (deliverability hit — scale frequency to engagement).
**Benchmarks:** open 25-35%; click 3-5%; discovery booking 2-5%.
**Note:** generate ONE week's pattern at the chosen frequency as the reusable template; the coach repeats/varies it weekly.
**Companion (flag only):** repurpose the lesson email as a social post.
