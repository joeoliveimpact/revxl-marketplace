# Pre-Call Video Script Framework — 2-5 Min Pre-Sell VSL

Source: Email Nurture Architecture Research (Type 4) + Orchestrating Trust (transfer-of-trust video).
Broadcast: ONE script, recorded once, shown to every booked prospect (on the confirmation page OR
emailed 24h before via precall E2). Specificity from coach voice + avatar pains, never individual facts.
Output is a SCRIPT (spoken), not an email — but the same voice + avatar rules apply.

**Objective:** eliminate objections, explain the mechanism, pre-sell before the live call.
**Trigger:** embedded on the booking confirmation page OR emailed 24h before the call.
**Length:** single **2-5 min** video (default — shorter holds attention; completion beats runtime). 7 min only if the coach insists, and teach the attention-span why (every extra minute sheds viewers). [Default tightened to 2-5 min from a sourced 5-7 min: shorter holds attention.] The timestamp table below is the 5-min reference cut — compress the section beats proportionally for a tighter 2-3 min version (keep all 4 beats, just shorter).

| Section | Timestamp | Purpose | Lever | Beat |
|---------|-----------|---------|-------|------|
| 1 | 0:00-1:00 | hook + relevance frame | Direct Relevance | confirm they're in the right place; validate the booking; set a supportive-diagnostic frame (not a pitch) |
| 2 | 1:00-3:00 | core mechanism contrast | Contrast/Authority | why the standard approach (the avatar's failed past attempts) breaks; the coach's system as the real alternative |
| 3 | 3:00-4:30 | proof | Social Proof | a case study the avatar identifies with; mechanism > willpower |
| 4 | 4:30-6:00 | rules of engagement + CTA | Scarcity/Mutual Respect | limited slots; show on time, quiet space, notepad, ready to decide on mutual fit |

**Pitch + CTA** (see ${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/references/cta-patterns.md): single `soft` close — book-the-call framed as mutual fit (7, takeaway framing). Pre-sell, never needy. Direct ask at the end, not throughout.

**Story dose: HEAVY** (see ${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/references/story-engines.md). §2 mechanism contrast + §3 proof are spoken story beats (Loss&Redemption / Third-Person Proof). Open with a hook, carry one through-line, blunt seam to the close. Written for the ear.

**Levers:** Authority (explain the mechanism); pre-emptive objection prevention; transfer-of-trust (if a setter/closer takes the call, the founder edifies them here).
**Mistakes to avoid:** unstructured rambling (kills retention); needy/salesy tone.
**Benchmarks:** page play 65-75%; retention to 5 min 45-55%; workbook/pre-work compliance 70-80%.
**Output format:** spoken script with section headers + timestamps + [B-roll/on-screen] notes optional. Write for the EAR (how the coach talks), not the page. No em dashes; natural spoken pauses.
**Companion:** pairs with precall E2 (the email links to this video). Flag that connection in the package.
