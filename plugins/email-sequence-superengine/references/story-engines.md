# Story Engines — the trust-building / storytelling toolkit

The narrative engine for warm-nurture (the moneymaker) and any story-driven email (launch case-study/origin,
precall E2). Sources: vault (Ben Settle email-reference-library, swipe-level) + REVUP Email Nurture notebook
(named frameworks). NOT canned copy — these are STRUCTURES to rotate, executed in the coach's voice.

## Doctrine (governs every story email)
- **EARN THE OPEN (entertainment-first) — the #1 rule.** The email's job is to be a genuinely good read: fun, surprising, worth opening for its own sake. "Make emails so good people hunt for them" (Settle/vault Deliverability Mindset). Test: **strip the CTA — is it still worth reading?** If no, it's an ad in banter, not a Settle email. Rewrite.
- **90/10 entertainment-to-offer** (notebook). Most warm emails are 90% story/entertainment/value with a light or ZERO offer-ask. The reader opening tomorrow is the win, not today's click.
- **Not every email bridges to the offer.** Many just entertain + teach, ending with a throwaway link, a "hit reply and tell me yours," or nothing to sell at all. The offer concentrates on the ~1-in-4 invite. Do NOT arrow every story at the program.
- **"Sell in every email" ≠ "pitch in every email."** A soft/throwaway link MAY ride along (Wilkerson: a lazy bottom link beat the no-sell version) — but it rides UNDER the entertainment, never replaces it.
- **Forced connection ("Bill Burr secret")** — when you DO bridge, take any real event and find the keen connection to ONE point. Use it for the invite emails and the occasional value→point; do not make it the mechanical ending of every send.
- **Hard seam, not subtle** — pivot story→sell bluntly with a literal connective line. Settle: "there'd be no 'subtle' pitch. Screw that sideways."
- **Rotate formats** — do NOT tell a story every day. "Storytelling is just ONE of many formats... a new surprise every day." Mix engines (below) so the list never gets predictable.
- **Antenna up — source REAL stories, never invent.** Pull from the **story-bank** (${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/references/story-bank.md — real coach moments captured via the `story-intake` Q&A) and the VoC profile. If the bank is empty/thin → run `story-intake` FIRST. If the coach gives nothing → FLAG that output will be generic and ask for one real moment. Do NOT fabricate "a coach I worked with..." slop. Real beats invented, every time.
- **Authentic voice > template** — write like the coach talks. Anti-swipe. The scaffolds are skeletons; the voice is the coach's.
- **Daily = relationship** — frequent email builds the bond; once it exists, the coach's NAME drives the open, not the subject. Opt-outs are the price of admission; purge lukewarm to protect deliverability.
- **One Big Idea per email** — one idea, one CTA, one destination.

## The engines (pick + rotate per email)

### 1. Story Selling (forced-connection bridge) — the default
Raw event/observation (drop straight in, no preamble) → tell it as entertainment → **seam** → ONE marketing lesson → ONE CTA. [vault: Story Selling node, "Bill Burr secret"]

### 2. Analogy / "Seinfeld" (mundane → lesson)
A small everyday scene in sensory detail (coffee, a workout, an argument) → hard cut → the parallel to the reader's situation → CTA. [vault: Analogy Bridge / Analogy Teaching · notebook: Seinfeld daily]

### 3. HSO / HIPS / PAS (body shapes — rotate)
- **HSO:** Hook → Story → Offer. [notebook]
- **HIPS:** Hook → Interest (why now) → Parable → Slide (to CTA). [notebook: McIntyre]
- **PAS:** Problem (their raw words) → Agitate (cost of inaction) → Solution (the method). [notebook + vault]

### 4. Serialized arc (multi-part / Soap-Opera-style) — for runs, not single sends
Numbered parts; each advances the arc and carries the loop forward; builds to a close. The notebook's 5-beat
arc (use when running a mini-series): Set the Stage → High Drama/The Wall → Epiphany → Hidden Benefits → Urgency,
mapped to the 50% emotion / 25% logic / 25% fear buyer split. [notebook: SOS · vault: Serialized Content / "Taste of the Apprentice" 4-part]

### 5. Attractive-Character storylines (ROTATE to prevent fatigue)
Loss & Redemption · Us vs Them (name the enemy) · Before & After · Amazing Discovery · Secret-Telling (insider) · Third-Person Proof (client journey, lead with their skepticism). [notebook] Rotate across weeks so a long nurture never repeats its angle.

## Open loops (the connective tissue — largest vault node)
- **Short loop:** open in subject/first line → story → payoff right before the CTA (within one email).
- **Long loop:** open at the end of one email, defer to a NAMED future email ("tomorrow: 'My $40k failure'"), resolve next send, then open a fresh loop. Chains reads across the week. [vault: Open Loop · notebook: Zeigarnik]
- **Nested loop:** open thread A → start thread B → teach → close B → close A. Keeps the subconscious engaged across touches.
- **P.S. Subplot Method (Chaperon):** run a SEPARATE personal narrative ONLY in the P.S. across emails (the dog, the coffee habit, a running challenge). Trains readers to scroll to the P.S. first. The P.S. is a subplot device — NOT the CTA (see copy-format-rules).

## Infotainment devices (sprinkle for the entertain layer)
Pattern interrupt (shock/abrupt open) · pop-culture hook (borrow a known character/story) · self-deprecation · polarization / enemy-framing · breaking the 4th wall. Rule: "if you have fun writing it, they'll have fun reading it."

## The seam (story → sell) — execute bluntly
Literal connective lines that hard-pivot to the pitch, e.g.:
- "Anyway, here's why I bring this up:"
- "The point?"
- "That's what you have to do too..."
Then ONE CTA at the body close. No fade, no apology.

## 2026 don'ts (notebook)
- Heavy HTML newsletters (templates/columns/stock photos) — trigger spam filters + get mangled by AI summarizers. Write a plain left-aligned letter.
- Fake urgency / clickbait subjects with a vanilla body — readers + algorithms punish it.
- Over-personalization (name in every line, scraped data). Personalize on BEHAVIOR (what they clicked), not demographics.

## Anti-template guardrail
These are scaffolds, not swipe. Never ship fill-in-the-blank canned copy. Pick an engine + a storyline, source a real story (coach's life / VoC), write it in the coach's voice, rotate so no two weeks feel the same.
