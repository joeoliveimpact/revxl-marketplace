# Story craft — how a story set earns the tap and the reply

Method distilled from story sets that ran on a real coach account. Use it for every set.

## The job of a story set

A feed post is found. A story is *shown* to people who already follow you, one tap at a time, about
five seconds a frame. So a set has one job: keep them tapping to the last frame, then get one small
action (a reply, a tap, a DM). Nothing else.

## Shapes that work

**Guide set (sells a lead magnet), 4–6 frames:**
1. **Hook frame:** a line that names the pain or picks a fight, on a photo that shows the feeling.
2. **Proof or reframe:** why the obvious fix fails, or what the real cause is.
3. **What's inside:** two or three short lines of what they get. Often the real cover of the guide.
4. **The ask:** "reply KEYWORD" plus what they get for it, on a photo of the coach.

**Opinion set (the coach's own words, 4–6 frames):** one beat per frame, the coach's lines in their
order, last frame = the ask. Long copy is fine across frames; it is never fine inside one frame.

**Quote card (one frame):** one of the coach's lines as a post-style card. See quote-card.md.

## Rules that never bend

- **The coach's words are theirs.** Offer numbered suggestions (missing ask, spelling, frame split,
  photo, render risk) and let them accept or skip each one. Never rewrite their voice for taste.
- **Every claim comes from the source.** A guide set uses only what the guide says. No invented
  numbers, results or client stories.
- **One text style across the whole set.** Mixed styles read as a template mill. Default `strong`.
- **One beat per frame.** If a frame needs more than about 25 words, split it.
- **Text never sits on a face.** Put lines in open space: sky, wall, the hood of a car, plain black.
- **The last frame always has a real ask** with a keyword that something actually answers. Before
  posting, the coach confirms their DM tool listens for that keyword on **story replies**, not only
  on comments. These are separate triggers in most tools.
- **No fake numbers on screen.** No made-up likes, views or follower counts.
- **Generated photos get the AI label** when the network asks (`--ai`). Real photos and real audio don't.
- **Show before it posts.** The coach sees the contact sheet and says go. Nothing schedules without it.

## Line breaks inside a bubble

- Break where a phrase ends, never mid-phrase to make a shape.
- Keep each line under about 26 characters in `strong` at the default size. The render check blocks
  a line that runs off the screen and notes one that runs into the side margin.
- Two short lines read faster than one long one.

## Picking photos

- Hook frame: emotion matching the line (cringe, laughing, focused, shocked).
- Ask frame: the coach, or a photo that pays off the keyword.
- Middle frames: plain black with color bubbles keeps the eye on the words.
- Use the coach's own photos first. Generated images are fine for organic stories; mark them.

## After it posts

Read it back after the post time (both networks should say PUBLISHED). Story numbers sync late
(often over an hour). Story replies arrive as DMs; that is where the keyword works.
