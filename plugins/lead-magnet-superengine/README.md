# lead-magnet-superengine

Turn any starting point into a high-converting lead magnet. Start with blank canvas, your old material, or someone else's—lead-magnet asks you questions to clarify your offer, then shapes a PDF designed to capture emails. Works day one with zero setup.

Say "I need a lead magnet" (the `lm-start-here` router) and it picks the right door for you.

## Three Entry Doors

**1. Blank Slate (`/lm-create`)** — Start from zero. Answer questions about your offer, ideal customer, and promise. lead-magnet builds the magnet structure.

**2. Improve Existing (`/lm-revamp`)** — Paste or upload your old lead magnet. Lead-magnet audits it for weak hooks, unclear CTAs, and missed urgency signals, then suggests rewrites.

**3. Inspired By (`/lm-inspired-by`)** — Paste a competitor's magnet. Lead-magnet reverse-engineers their positioning and shows you how to claim adjacent ground without copying.

Plus **`brand-brain`** — capture your real voice from calls and content (shared across all REVXL engines), so every magnet reads like you wrote it.

## Zero Setup, Optional Upgrades

Lead-magnet ships with built-in copywriting and PDF generation. Works day one.

For live research—current market rates, trending pain points in your niche, competitor positioning—run `/lm-setup` to connect optional research sources. Each one is explained before install, and you control which to add.

## Perfect For

Coaches shipping lead magnets fast. Built to ask the right questions, not create blank pages.
