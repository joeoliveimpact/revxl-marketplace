# Nurture Handoff (delivery → email sequence → booked call)

> Bundled knowledge reference. The lead magnet is step one; this ref covers what happens after opt-in. Ties into the coach's email engine (see the email-sequence-superengine plugin if installed). The magnet's CTA hands off here.

---

## Cadence (timed to the format's consumption speed)
- **Email 1 — immediate.** Fires within seconds of opt-in. Any delay makes them doubt the download worked and kills enthusiasm.
- **Fast-consumption formats** (checklist, template, calculator — lead is in implementation mode): **Day 1–2** quick tip · **Day 3–4** case study · **Day 7** direct CTA.
- **Slow-consumption formats** (ebook, comprehensive guide — ~39-hour download→open lag): space it out — **Day 3** tip · **Day 6** case study · **Day 14** CTA. Pitching within 24h of an ebook download reaches them *before* they've felt the value → counterproductive.

## First email anatomy (delivery)
1. **Prominent download/results button** — single obvious focal point.
2. **Consumption tip** — "Start with section 3 for the fastest win."
3. **Expectation-setting** — what's coming over the next week (primes opens).
4. **A quick-win / reflection prompt** — small action builds rapport + list health.
5. **Avoid:** aggressive pitch, company-history paragraphs, competing CTAs.

## The 5–7 email welcome sequence (one job per email)
- **E1 — Deliver & set expectations (Day 0):** hand over the magnet, preview the week.
- **E2 — Story / quick tip (Day 1–3):** a common mistake or fast actionable win; coaches share their personal "why" for human connection.
- **E3 — Case study & proof (Day 3–6):** one client transformation — before-struggle → exact process → measurable result.
- **E4 — Soft program intro (Day 5–9):** connect the value already given to the offer, framed as the vehicle that removes the time/effort/sacrifice of doing it alone.
- **E5 — Direct CTA (Day 7–14):** clear, low-pressure invite (clarity call / trial) + ethical reason to act now (cohort capacity).

## Segment by magnet topic (zero-party data)
Generic follow-up wastes the capture. Use what the magnet learned:
- **Quiz/assessment score** → route by result (resume-help vs interview-prep; "high stress" → stress protocol).
- **Calculator/goal** → segment by primary goal (fat-loss vs energy vs muscle) so E2/E3 speak to their exact profile.

## The touchpoint threshold (don't quit early)
**~80% of conversions happen after the 5th touchpoint** — yet 44% of sellers stop after one follow-up. A disciplined 5–7 email sequence automates the touchpoints that dissolve resistance before any sales conversation.

## Bridging to the booked call (multi-stage, filters tire-kickers)
1. **Thank-you page redirect (immediate bridge):** a **7–12 min unedited authority video** proving your mechanism with real client data — belief-shifts at peak attention, before Email 1.
2. **3-question qualification filter:** don't hand booking links to everyone. Measure goal alignment / budget readiness / timeline; qualified → calendar, lower-readiness → secondary resources.
3. **Outcome framing:** never "sales call" or "free consultation" (triggers pitch anxiety). Use **"Clarity Call," "Transformation Audit," "Personalized Roadmap Session"** with 3 named takeaways → an objective consultation, not a pitch.
