# Format-by-Niche Matrix (pick the right magnet for the right audience)

> Bundled knowledge reference. Distilled from the "Lead Magnet Strategy for Coaches" research notebook (2025–2026 sources). Use this to choose the magnet FORMAT once the niche + audience sophistication are known. Pairs with `lead-magnet-frameworks.md` (the type/why) and `conversion-benchmarks.md` (the numbers).

---

## Two rules that override format choice

**1. "Add for [Niche]" specificity.** Generic magnets ("Get My Free Marketing Guide") speak to everyone and resonate with no one. Adding **"for [Niche]"** to the title can lift opt-ins **3×–5×**. "Meal Prep Guide" → "Weekly Meal Prep Checklist **for Busy Families**" → "High-Protein Snacks **for Active Women Over 40**." Narrow the audience until the prospect feels it was built for them.

**2. Sub-10-minute consumption.** Cold visitors give a landing page ~**47 seconds** before deciding (down from 2.5 min in 2004). A 50-page PDF triggers "I'll read it later" → never opened. Effective magnets are **consumable in under 10 minutes** — checklists, calculators, quick templates — delivering an immediate **micro-win** that builds trust in your full method.

---

## Audience sophistication (the column that decides format)

- **Early / Awareness** — just realizing they have a problem. Want quick-win, low-friction, self-discovery formats. Curiosity-driven.
- **Sophisticated / Ready-to-Buy (Level 5)** — already know the generic solutions, skeptical of sales-pitch freebies. Want precise diagnostics, proof of mechanism, and a personalized action plan before committing.

The same niche needs a *different* magnet depending on which of these the traffic is.

---

## The matrix

| Niche | Early / Awareness | Sophisticated / Level 5 |
|---|---|---|
| **Fitness** | Diet cheat sheet / visual habit tracker | 30-day challenge (daily micro-tasks) OR fitness scorecard |
| **Health / Wellness** | Health assessment quiz ("What's Your Stress Type?") | Macro calculator + customized 7-day meal plan |
| **Business** | Diagnostic checklist (e.g., launch-prep, discovery-call) | ROI/pricing calculator OR raw Loom case-study walkthrough |
| **Life** | Personality / goal-setting quiz | Wheel of Life assessment OR limiting-beliefs identifier |

---

## Why, per niche

**Fitness.** Beginners are intimidated by rigorous programming — a one-page **diet cheat sheet** (portions, snack swaps) gives instant utility, zero friction. Experienced consumers want structure/progression — a gamified **30-day challenge** builds momentum and community, or a **fitness scorecard** surfaces blind spots in their current routine and positions your method as the fix.

**Health / Wellness.** Broad-symptom audiences (fatigue, gut, stress) are pulled by self-discovery — a **health assessment quiz** converts 40%+ because it answers "why do I feel this way?" Advanced prospects want data to act on — a **macro calculator** delivers exact metabolic baselines, which exposes the next problem (how to implement) that your **7-day meal plan** + call solves.

**Business.** Early owners need operational frameworks — a **diagnostic checklist** is a functional asset they use immediately. Established owners are immune to generic PDFs and want financial proof — an **ROI/pricing calculator** quantifies exactly what they're losing, and a **7–12 min Loom walkthrough** of a real client's dashboard gives unedited proof of mechanism, breaking "this is just a pitch" skepticism.

**Life.** Growth-seekers respond to identity framing — a **personality/goal quiz** is low-friction and highly shareable. Sophisticated buyers who've consumed personal-development content need a structured audit — a **Wheel of Life** (score 8–10 dimensions) maps their exact gaps and shows where 1:1 coaching pays off fastest.

## How a skill uses this
1. Establish niche + audience sophistication (ask if unknown).
2. Pick the format from the matrix cell.
3. Apply "add for [niche]" to the title and enforce the sub-10-minute rule.
4. Confirm the format's micro-win reveals the next problem the coach's core offer solves (see `lead-magnet-frameworks.md`).
