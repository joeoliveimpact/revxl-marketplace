# Lead Magnet Mistakes (why they fail — and the fix)

> Bundled knowledge reference. Distilled from the "Lead Magnet Strategy for Coaches" notebook. A skill should screen every drafted magnet against these five failure modes before finalizing.

---

## Mistake 1 — The "overgiving" trap
**Problem:** Either you publish shallow fluff (prospects assume your paid offer is equally weak), OR you solve the *entire* core problem for free and **satiate** the prospect. Hormozi's analogy: serve a free full entrée and they won't buy a second — not because it was bad, but because they're no longer hungry.
**Fix:** *Give away the secrets, sell the implementation.* Teach the **what** and **why** free (builds trust); sell the **how / who / support**. Solve one **narrow "pre-coaching" problem** whose micro-win **reveals the next problem** your core offer solves (the salty-pretzel → thirst mechanic).

## Mistake 2 — Topic too broad / generic
**Problem:** "Get My Free Marketing Guide," "Fat Loss Tips" speak to everyone, resonate with no one, and attract freebie-seekers with zero buy intent — diluting the list and lowering downstream sales.
**Fix:** Apply **"add for [niche]"** (3×–5× opt-ins): "Weekly Meal Prep Checklist **for Busy Families**," "5-Day Content Calendar **for Life Coaches**." Promise a specific, desirable outcome, not a theoretical topic.

## Mistake 3 — Vague / non-actionable / overwhelming
**Problem:** The padded 50-page PDF is dead. Consumption gap: **60–70% never opened, only ~12% of openers finish.** Requires hours → saved to a folder → never seen.
**Fix — the SAGE screen (every asset must pass):**
- **S – Short:** consumable in <10 min (written 5–15 pages; video clips <10 min).
- **A – Actionable:** template / swipe file / 10-item checklist, not a brand-centric explanation of "your process."
- **G – Goal-oriented:** one clear, concrete result they can picture achieving today.
- **E – Easy:** mobile-first single-column, file <5MB, capture form **≤3 fields** (ideally name + email).

## Mistake 4 — Format mismatched to funnel stage / sophistication
**Problem:** Forcing cold TOFU traffic into high-friction formats (webinars with 70%+ no-shows, credit-card trials) asks for commitment before trust. And feeding surface-level content to a skeptical **Level-5 (ready-to-buy)** buyer backfires — they read generic how-tos as disguised pitches and tune out.

```
              FORMAT × FUNNEL SELECTION
 Stage   Warmth                     Best formats
 TOFU    Cold / Awareness (47s)     Checklists, Quizzes, Calculators
 MOFU    Warm / Consideration       Templates, Mini-courses, Frameworks
 BOFU    Hot / Level 5 (wants proof) Case studies, Audits, Trials, Scorecards
```
**Fix:** Early/awareness → low-friction quiz/checklist (curiosity, self-discovery). Level 5 → skip fluff; shift belief with unedited Loom case-study walkthroughs (real dashboards), diagnostic scorecards, or ROI calculators. (See `format-by-niche-matrix.md` for the niche axis.)

## Mistake 5 — No clear next step / competing CTAs
**Problem:** Quick win delivered, then *nothing* — a dead end, or 3 competing CTAs. Wasted acquisition spend.
**Fix:**
- **One direct CTA** per asset. Be clear, not clever: "Book your 15-minute strategy call here."
- **Ethical urgency/scarcity** from real limits ("I onboard 5 clients/week," "cohort closes Friday") — never fake countdowns.
- **Automated 3–5 email sequence** on download (see `nurture-handoff.md`): E1 deliver · E2 tip · E3 case study · E4 soft intro · E5 direct call/offer.

---

## The Level-5-Buyer lens (screens mistakes 1, 2, 4)
Awareness ladder: most freebie traffic is early-stage; the *buyers* are "Level 5" — problem-aware, solution-aware, skeptical, ready within ~30 days. A magnet built for Level 5 (specific, diagnostic, proof-carrying) attracts fewer but far higher-intent leads. Broad/fluffy magnets pull volume with no buyers.
