# Hooks & Titles (the snap-decision layer)

> Bundled knowledge reference. Extends the naming section of `lead-magnet-frameworks.md` with the testing method + why it matters. The title is where most of the opt-in decision is made.

---

## Why the title carries the opt-in
~**5× more people read the headline than the body** — the opt-in is decided almost entirely on the title/packaging. Hormozi/Ogilvy: "when you've written your headline, you've spent 80 cents of your dollar." Improving the name/headline/display can **2×/3×/10×** engagement without touching the content.

## Proven title formulas
- **Number + Outcome + Timeframe** — "3 Emails That Turn Cold Leads Into Clients in 24 Hours"
- **How to [outcome] without [obstacle]** — "How to Build a Funnel Without Hiring a Copywriter"
- **The [adjective] [thing] That [result]** — "The Lazy Funnel Template That Converts Like Crazy"
- **X Mistakes Keeping You From [outcome]** — "4 Mistakes Keeping Your Business Under $1M"

Every formula pairs a **specific outcome** with either a **timeframe**, an **avoided obstacle**, or a **curiosity gap**.

## The specificity multiplier
Always apply **"add for [niche]"** (3×–5× opt-ins): "Meal Prep Guide" → "High-Protein Snacks **for Active Women Over 40**." Own the keyword in the prospect's head. (Full logic in `format-by-niche-matrix.md`.)

## Mobile-first constraint
58%+ of traffic is mobile → titles truncate. Put the core value proposition in the **first ~50 characters**.

## Testing order (cheapest signal first)
1. **Headline** — test first (biggest lever).
2. **Image / cover** — test second.
3. **Subheadline** — test last.
Get directional feedback cheaply before a full launch: audience polls, story-question stickers, or a text to your list. Then A/B the landing page one variable at a time.

## Screen against the mistakes
A title that's broad ("Free Marketing Guide"), vague, or promises a topic instead of an outcome fails before the content is even seen. Cross-check `lead-magnet-mistakes.md` (Mistakes 2 & 3).
