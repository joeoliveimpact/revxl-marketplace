## The bimodal pattern (no productive middle)

The market split; padded 40–50-page generic PDFs are dead (AI answers those questions with no email gate).
- **Camp 1 — fast utilitarian (TOFU):** checklists, cheat sheets, templates, calculators, quizzes. Consumed in <10 min for an immediate micro-win. Where TOFU conversion lives.
- **Camp 2 — substantive definitive (MOFU/BOFU):** holds ground **only** when packaged with original benchmark surveys, proprietary research, or raw case-study data. (NetLine 2025: B2B ebook registrations +71.4% YoY, but broad "AI-topic" ebooks −15%.)

The generic Google-compiled ebook in the middle is effectively dead.

## Consumption → booked call: NO single published benchmark (state this honestly)

**There is no published metric for the joint rate at which a downloader both consumes the magnet AND books a call.** Do not invent one. Instead, reconstruct the trajectory from the documented funnel steps:

**Consumption proxies (the consumption gap):**
- **60–70% of downloaded ebooks/whitepapers are never opened** (PathFactory / Dashform).
- Of those who open a static guide, **only ~12% finish** (Subpage.co 2026).
- B2B ebooks have a **~39-hour lag** between download and read (NetLine 2025).
- Interactive bypasses the gap: quizzes **65%** start-to-finish (Interact); wellness assessments **80–85%** (Heyflow/ScoreApp).

**Lead-to-booked-call proxies:**
- Coaching industry benchmark: **~15% of downloads → booked discovery call within 14 days**; AI 5-minute qualification lifts to **25–30%**; general consideration funnels 15–25%.
- Format-specific (Heyflow/ScoreApp) — bookings scale with format friction/specificity:
  - Personality quiz (low-friction TOFU): **5–10%** book
  - Diagnostic assessment (MOFU): **15–25%** book
  - Product recommendation (mid-BOFU): **20–35%** book
  - Grader / qualifier (high-friction BOFU): **25–40%** book

**Downstream nurture-to-sale:** short-form checklists **11.4%** to-sale in a 30-day email window vs **4.7%** for long PDFs (CMI); B2B SaaS download→trial/demo 2–5%.

**How a skill uses this:** if a coach asks "what conversion should I expect," give the *format + traffic-source* range and the honest consumption caveat — never a single made-up "X% will consume and book" figure.
