# Hook library — 29 formulas in 10 types

Distilled from the vetted creative playbook (07.06.26). Every hook lands in
the **first 40 characters** (mobile "See More" cutoff) and the first 3
seconds of video. Fill templates with the AVATAR'S OWN words from
voc-profile — never generic category language.

## 1. Curiosity & open loop
| Formula | Template |
|---|---|
| Habit Gap | "Most people don't know this about [common habit] — and it's costing them." |
| Guru Disruption | "There's one [action] that [benefit] for our client, and it's not what any guru is teaching." |
| Secret Cure | "This one thing completely cured my [pain point] (and it's not what you think)." |
| Scroll Interrupter | "You are scrolling past the one thing that could change [outcome]." |

## 2. Callout & identity (a hard routing signal — the machine reads it)

## Type → awareness pairing (which types fit which concept)
- Unaware: curiosity, contrarian, native-conversational (earn the next
  second — never sell).
- Problem-aware: pain-point, callout, loss-aversion.
- Solution-aware: how-to, contrarian ("why normal approaches fail").
- Product-aware: social proof, confessional.
- Most-aware: transformation + offer-forward.

## Rules
- 40-character front-load, always.
- Numbers in hooks must be REAL and the coach's own (canon: no invented
  stats, no fake scarcity).
- A hook variant of a proven body gets its own `hN` id (naming grammar) —
  it's a Creative-Testing-Tool candidate, not a new concept.
