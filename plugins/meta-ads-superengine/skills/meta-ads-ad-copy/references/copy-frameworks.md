# Copy frameworks — the 5 structures + rules

Distilled from the vetted creative playbook (07.06.26). All conversion-lift
percentages the source material attached to these frameworks were
stat-laundering and are STRIPPED (canon rule 1) — the structures hold on
their own.

## The 5 frameworks

| Framework | Steps | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **PAS** (Problem-Agitation-Solution) | Specific visceral pain → deepen the emotional/physical impact → clear bridge to the outcome | The coaching gold standard; problem-aware prospects |
| **BAB** (Before-After-Bridge) | Painful status quo → vivid desired state → your method as the path | Life/mindset/wellness (emotional transformation) |
| **SLAP** (Stop-Look-Act-Purchase) | Jarring/relatable hook → linger on the visual/copy → micro-action ("comment GUT") → lead-magnet handoff | Reels/fast-scroll discovery |
| **Claim → Reason → Outcome** | Bold trend-disrupting claim → the mechanism why → a verifiable specific result | Solution-aware prospects comparing options |
| **H-P-O-CTA** (Hook-Problem-Offer-CTA) | One-sentence hook → 2-sentence empathy max → offer with numbers/timeline → exactly ONE action | Direct conversational copy ("Message me the word MOBILITY") |

**Worked PAS reference:**
- *(P)* "POV: It's 3:00 AM. You're staring at the ceiling and cannot turn your brain off."
- *(A)* "The mental fog bleeds into your day. You're snapping at your kids, second-guessing decisions, running on four coffees."
- *(S)* "You don't need sleep medication. You need the 5-Minute Executive Reset protocol."

## Framework → awareness pairing
- Problem-aware → PAS (validate the visceral pain).
- Solution-aware → Claim-Reason-Outcome (prove the unique mechanism).
- Product-aware → proof/logic + risk reversal.
- Most-aware → offer-forward + guarantee (real ones only).

## Objection handling in copy
- **"But you might be wondering…" block** — a dedicated objection section
  right after the solution (cost / time / complexity).
- **Extreme risk reversal** — "No discovery call. No credit card." — for
  hesitant product-aware retargets. Only promises the coach will keep.

## Hard rules
- **Limits:** primary text 125 chars VISIBLE (2,200 max — write for the
  visible zone) · headline 40 · description 30.
- **5-slot rule:** copy variations live INSIDE one ad (up to 5 primary
  texts + headlines) — never as separate near-duplicate ads.
- **CTA:** default buttons + a strong in-copy CTA; button micro-testing is
  dead weight.
- **Copy is a targeting signal** — the machine reads it. Mine the avatar's
  real language (voc-profile); category jargon routes the ad to nobody.
- **AI-tells avoid-list:** "delve", "unlock", "unleash", "game-changer",
  "in today's fast-paced world", em-dash chains, symmetric triads,
  "it's not X, it's Y" scaffolds — templated copy clusters as
  duplicate-adjacent AND reads as AI.
- **Zero unattributed stats.** A percentage without a primary source does
  not ship (canon rule 1). Real deadlines only — no fake scarcity.
- Headlines: short and front-loaded ("[Pain]? [Short solution]" ·
  "[Outcome] in [Timeframe]" — timeframes the coach can defend).
