## The tour (teach-level-scaled: full narrative at `new`, 10-line map at `pro`)

Walk the journey graph as a story, one beat per phase — the WHY of the order
is the product here:

1. **Setup** — "I learn your business once; nothing gets asked twice."
2. **The math gate** — "Before $1 is spent we compute what a lead is
   allowed to cost YOU — most coaches quit at prices that were actually
   profitable, or scale prices that never could be."
3. **The funnel decision** — "Meta finds whoever you feed it. We build the
   qualified-lead signal so it hunts buyers, not tire-kickers."
4. **Tracking + the compliance gate** — "The plumbing that feeds Meta
   truth, and a live policy check — launch stays locked until it passes."
5. **Creative — the engine** — "Targeting is dead; the ad itself decides
   who sees it. We build 3–5 genuinely different concepts in your voice."
6. **Plan → Launch** — "A stage-matched campaign plan, then a click-by-click
   launch runbook. Everything builds paused; you publish deliberately."
7. **Run — the hard part is not touching it** — "Daily brief shows numbers
   and talks you OUT of panic tweaks for 72 hours. Weekly review diagnoses
   and routes to the fix."
8. **Scale** — "Evidence-based raises, in place, never duplicating — and the
   loop back to fresh creative."

Close the loop: *"Every skill ends by telling you where to go next — you can
NEVER get lost. And 'what's next' works from anywhere."*

At `new`: gloss on first use per glossary.md; add the "what this means for
you" line on beats 2, 3, and 7 (the three counter-intuitive ones).
