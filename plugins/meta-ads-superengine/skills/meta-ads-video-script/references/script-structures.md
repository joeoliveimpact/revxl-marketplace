# Video script structures — the 3 architectures + pacing

Distilled from the vetted creative playbook (07.06.26). Bipolar lengths
(canon): 15–30s direct assets AND 90–120s+ VSL — one-size 30s scripts are
legacy.

## A. High-ticket VSL (cold → aware in one video, 90–120s+)
| Beat | Time | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0:00–0:03 | curiosity/pattern-interrupt (silent symptom or contrarian truth) |
| Agitation | 0:03–0:30 | frame the problem as systemic failure, not personal laziness |
| Education | 0:30–1:15 | WHY normal approaches fail (contrast negation) |
| Bridge | 1:15–1:30 | your method as the container of the solution |
| Proof | 1:30–2:00 | real case study / client result |
| CTA | 2:00+ | apply or watch the full training |

**CTA past ~1:15, never earlier** — a late product intro drops raw views
and improves lead quality; the people still watching are the buyers.

## B. Talking-head / founder story (15–30s or 60–90s)
Vulnerability hook → crisis/catalyst → what you unlearned + the system you
built → measurable transformation → "borrow my system" CTA. The coach
default — founder-face outperforms polished production.

## C. UGC / social-native ("reply-to-comment")
Comment-bubble or "No because why…" opener → contrarian agitation → raw
screen-share proof (real doc/pipeline, with consent) → frictionless
micro-CTA ("comment VEGAN").

## Pacing rules (all video)
- **Frame zero (0–3s):** hook in the first 40 chars; Silent Stare (2s of
  direct eye contact before speaking) or Graphic Slam (one bold word fills
  the frame).
- **Bridge (3–10s):** do NOT hard-pitch right after the hook — retention
  collapses; blend the mechanism before naming the program.
- **Captions mandatory** — most watch soundless; burn them in.
- **Ratios:** 4:5 native for feed; 9:16 for Reels/Stories with safe zones
  14/35/6. Never let Meta auto-crop.
- **Post-ID synergy:** publish as organic Reels first; run the week's top
  organic performers as ads via their Post IDs (compounding social proof).
- Teleprompter formatting: short lines, breath breaks, spoken-word
  contractions — written to be SAID, not read.

## AI-avatar caveat (canon: Compliance)
Synthetic/AI-avatar video gets auto-labeled (C2PA, from Jun 2026). No
instant-rejection regime, but expect the label + a trust/conversion cost.
Real footage beats synthetic for coaches; re-check policy before scaling
AI-avatar creative.
