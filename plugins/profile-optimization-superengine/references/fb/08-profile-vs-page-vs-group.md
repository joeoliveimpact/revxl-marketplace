# 08 ... Profile vs. Page vs. Group Strategy

> **Scope:** When and how to use a Facebook Personal Profile, Business Page, and Facebook Group ... including the Hub-and-Spoke model, Professional Mode, monetization tools, and the recommended hybrid strategy for coaches.

---

## The Three Facebook Assets

### 1. Personal Profile ... The Lead Generation Engine

The personal profile is the most powerful organic asset for B2C fitness/health coaches. Designed for authentic 1:1 connections, which is exactly what wellness prospects buy into.

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Organic reach** | Guaranteed ... Facebook must show profile posts to keep users on-platform |
| **Reach vs. Pages** | 5-10× more organic reach than Business Pages |
| **DM capability** | ✅ Can proactively initiate Messenger conversations with anyone who engages |
| **Content formats** | Unrestricted posting, Lives, Stories, Reels with native DM funnels |
| **Friend limit** | 5,000 friends + unlimited followers (with Professional Mode) |

#### Professional Mode (Must Activate)

| Benefit | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Unlimited followers** | Removes the 5,000 friend cap by adding a separate "Followers" audience |
| **Professional Dashboard** | Unlocks analytics ... track reach, engagement, and top-performing content |
| **Meta Verified** | Optional paid badge ($10-20/month) ... builds authority and trust |
| **Limitations** | Cannot assign team members or VAs; risk of 24-hour blocks for spammy activity (e.g., mass friend requests) |

---

### 2. Business Page ... The Ad & Scaling Sandbox

**🚨 Do NOT rely on a Business Page for organic lead generation.**

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Organic reach** | Near-zero ... Meta suppresses Page posts to push paid boosts |
| **DM capability** | ❌ Cannot initiate Messenger conversations ... must wait for inbound |
| **Team access** | ✅ Can assign admin/editor roles via Meta Business Suite |
| **Ad capability** | ✅ Required for running Meta Ads (Advantage+ campaigns) |

#### When to Use a Business Page
1. **Running Meta Ads:** Legally required to have a Page for paid campaigns
2. **Team delegation:** When VAs or team members handle content creation and engagement

---

### 3. Facebook Group ... The Nurture & Conversion Vessel

Groups are NOT for cold traffic ... they're for deep-nurturing warm leads into paying clients.

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Internal reach** | High within niched groups without ad spend |
| **Algorithm requirement** | Members must engage (read, watch, click, like, comment) ≥ 2×/month ... inactive groups are being killed off |
| **Lead capture** | Membership Questions extract email addresses in exchange for entry |
| **@everyone tag** | Bulk-notify all active members about key content or offers |
| **Exclusive Events** | Host private Live trainings (e.g., 5-day mobility challenge) |
| **Content scheduling** | Built-in post scheduling for batch content planning |

## The Hub-and-Spoke Model (Recommended Hybrid Strategy)

Deploy this exact ecosystem to maximize coaching revenue:

### The Hub ... Personal Profile + Professional Mode
- Post 4-pillar content (Education, Social Proof, Lifestyle, Offer)
- Acts as top-of-funnel traffic driver
- When prospects engage, initiate 1:1 Messenger conversations

### The Spoke ... Facebook Group
- Invite warm DM leads into a private, niched group (e.g., "Weight Loss for Busy Executive Dads")
- Nurture with weekly anchors: Mindset Monday, Workout Wednesday, Weekend Wins
- Pin a short welcome video with your story, rules, and program links
- Host private Lives (5-10 min) for form demos, Q&As, and motivation

### The Conversion Engine
- Nurture leads inside the group with exclusive resources and live videos
- Tag new members on high-value posts to build immediate engagement momentum
- Pitch coaching offers to an audience that already trusts you

### The Ad Scaler ... Business Page
- Only activate when ready to scale with paid spend
- Boost top-performing organic profile posts as ads
- Leverage Meta Business Suite for team management and advanced targeting

## Decision Framework for Coaches

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| "Where should I post content?" | Personal Profile (Hub) ... always |
| "Do I need a Business Page?" | Only if running paid Meta Ads or delegating to a team |
| "Should I start a Group?" | Yes ... for nurturing warm leads toward high-ticket ($2,000+) offers |
| "Can I use all three?" | Yes ... the Hub-and-Spoke model uses all three strategically |
