# 02 ... Profile Picture & Cover Photo Design

> **Scope:** Facebook personal profile picture (the circular avatar) and cover photo/banner ... the two largest visual trust signals on a coach's profile.

---

## Part A ... Profile Picture

### Dimensions & Format

| Attribute | Specification |
|---|---|
| **Upload size** | 320 × 320 px |
| **Desktop display** | 176 × 176 px |
| **Mobile display** | 196 × 196 px |
| **Crop shape** | Circle ... keep all key elements in the center safe zone |
| **File type** | JPG for photographs; PNG for brand logos or sharp color graphics |

### Professional-Casual Hybrid Requirements

For B2C fitness/health/wellness coaches, the "professional-casual hybrid" is the gold standard:

- **Headshot:** High-resolution, smiling face in natural workout attire (e.g., clean gym tank top)
- **Lighting:** Soft, natural light; clean, uncluttered background
- **Framing:** Face takes up 60-70% of the frame
- **Eye contact:** Direct eye contact with camera ... converts 40% better for trust-building
- **Subtle branding:** Branded wristband or top in primary brand color

### What Works vs. What Fails

| ✅ Works | ❌ Fails |
|---|---|
| Clean, well-lit solo headshot | Business logo on personal profile (kills 1:1 trust) |
| Face at 60-70% of frame | Group shots or cropped party photos |
| Natural workout attire | Heavy Instagram-style filters |
| High-resolution upload at 320×320 | Low-resolution files that pixelate on compression |

## Part B ... Cover Photo / Banner

### Dimensions & Safe Zones

| Attribute | Specification |
|---|---|
| **Master canvas** | 820 × 360 px (aspect ratio ~2.63:1) |
| **Desktop crop** | 820 × 312 px (top/bottom cropped) |
| **Mobile crop** | 640 × 360 px (~90px cropped from each side) |
| **Safe zone (all devices)** | Central 640 × 312 px (x:90-740, y:24-336) |
| **Profile pic overlap** | Avoid x:0-250, y:200-360 (bottom-left) |
| **File format** | JPG for photos; PNG for sharp text/logos |
| **File size** | Under 100 KB for fast loading |
| **Export quality** | 80-90% to prevent Facebook compression blur |

### Mobile-First Composition (90% of views are mobile)

Design using a **"journey arc"** composition (left → right: before → action → after):

| Zone | Y-Range | Content |
|---|---|---|
| **Top third** | 0-120 | Visual hook ... inspirational skyline, gym scene, or brand gradient |
| **Middle third** | 120-240 | Hero coach photo or client transformation (face forward, centered) |
| **Bottom third** | 240-360 | CTA + social proof ... keep out of bottom-left profile pic overlap |

### CTA Overlay & Text Rules

| Rule | Specification |
|---|---|
| **Text coverage** | 20-40% maximum |
| **Typography** | Bold sans-serif (Montserrat, Roboto Bold) |
| **Font size (Desktop)** | H1: 80-120pt / Subtext: 40-60pt |
| **Font size (Mobile)** | H1: 60-100pt / Subtext: 32-48pt |
| **Drop shadow** | 2-4px at 30% opacity for contrast |
| **Placement (Desktop)** | x:250-570, y:150-220 (center-right bias) |

**Mobile text placement (general guideline, not a pixel spec).** Mobile crops roughly 90px off each side (visible width ~640px) and hides less top/bottom than desktop, so desktop's center-right bias can push text off-screen on a phone. There are no separate exact mobile coordinates to give... instead, follow the rule: **center all text horizontally within the shared safe zone (x:90-740) and keep it in the middle band vertically (about y:120-240), well clear of the bottom-left profile-pic overlap (x:0-250, y:200-360).** Since 90% of views are mobile, design mobile-first and then confirm it still reads on desktop, not the other way around.

### Algorithm-Compliant CTA Examples
- "Join 5K Transformed Clients"
- "Free 7-Day Challenge ... Start Now"
- "DM 'START' for your free health audit 👇"

**🚨 Avoid:** "Click the link" (algorithm-suppressed language)

### Action Shots vs. Transformations

- **Action shots** convert 25-40% higher for fitness coaches ... evoke aspiration and forward motion
- **Transformations** work for specific niches but risk clutter on mobile screens
- A/B testing shows action shots boost DMs by 18%

### Color Psychology ... 60-30-10 Rule

| Color | Hex | Signal | Use As |
|---|---|---|---|
| Green | #00A676 | Vitality | Base or secondary |
| Blue | #007BFF | Trust | Base or secondary |
| Orange | #FF9500 | High energy | Accent (10%) |

Fitness brands see 15-20% boost in conversions with warm "power" colors. Maintain 4.5:1 contrast ratio for accessibility.

### Design Tools
- **Canva:** Search "Facebook Cover Fitness" for 820×360 Pro templates with safe zones (80% of coaches use this)
- **Snappa / Outfy:** Auto-safe zone overlays, fitness template packs
- **Photoshop:** Custom at 1640×720 @2x retina

### Refresh Cadence
Rotate cover photo **every 4-6 weeks** to align with new campaigns, seasons, or lead magnets. Refreshing sends an update to followers' feeds, bumping profile engagement by +12%.
