## The Engagement-First Algorithm (2025-2026)

The Meta algorithm prioritizes original content that drives deep engagement, longer watch times, and "qualified views." The four signals it rewards:

| Signal | Weight | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Shares** | Highest | Most people don't share ... signals uniquely valuable content |
| **Saves** | Highest | Signals content worth revisiting |
| **Comments** | High | Drives threaded conversations |
| **Reactions** | Standard | Likes, loves ... baseline signal |

### Golden Rules

1. **No AI-generated copy.** AI text gets 35% less reach and engagement. Use AI for ideas and research, then write in your own voice.
2. **Reply to every comment.** Like + reply to each one. Move interactions into Messenger DMs strategically.
3. **No links in captions.** Facebook restricts reach on posts with outbound links. Drive traffic to DMs or Link-in-Bio.

## The 4-Pillar Content Framework (80/20 Rule)

Follow the **80/20 value-to-sales ratio**: 80% value deposits, 20% conversion withdrawals.

### Pillar 1 ... Education / Quick Wins (Value)

**Purpose:** Solve immediate problems to establish authority.

- **Structure:** Hook (common mistake) → Science/Correction → Quick Takeaway → "Save this" CTA
- **Template:** *"Still struggling with [Pain Point]? Stop doing [Common Mistake]. Here's the exact 3-step fix I use with my 1:1 clients: 1. [Step 1], 2. [Step 2], 3. [Step 3]. Save this post for your next workout! 📌 👇"*

### Pillar 2 ... Transformation / Social Proof (Trust)

**Purpose:** Answer "Will this work for me?" with relatable client stories.

- **Structure:** Client's Starting Point (Pain) → Coaching Intervention → Tangible Result → DM CTA
- **Template:** *"Meet [Client Name]. They came to me feeling [Pain Point]. We ditched restrictive diets and implemented [Your Method]. In 12 weeks, they achieved [Specific Result]. Drop a 'GOAL' in the comments and DM me 'PLAN' if you want my exact framework! 🔥"*

### Pillar 3 ... Lifestyle / Personal Brand (Relatability)

**Purpose:** Humanize your brand and build trust through authenticity.

- **Structure:** Behind-the-scenes visual → Relatable struggle → Engaging question
- **Template:** *"Here's exactly how I prep meals for the week in under 20 minutes... [Steps]. It doesn't have to be perfect; just consistent. What's your biggest struggle with meal prep? Let me know below! 👇"*

### Pillar 4 ... Offer / CTA (Conversion)

**Purpose:** Generate inbound leads via lead magnet or intro session. This is the 20% "withdrawal."

- **Structure:** Call out Target Audience → Introduce Free Asset → DM CTA
- **Template:** *"Are you a [Target Audience] working 50+ hours and struggling to [Goal]? I just put together a [Free Asset]. DM me the word 'READY' and I'll send it over for free! 🚀"*

## Caption Structure

| Section | Content | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Hook (Line 1)** | Call out ideal client's pain point | Must stop the scroll |
| **Body** | Short paragraphs (1-2 sentences max) | Use emojis as bullet points for scannability |
| **CTA (Final)** | Algorithm-compliant DM trigger or bio redirect | Never use "Click the link" or "Share this" |

## Hashtag Strategy (2025-2026)

Hashtags remain relevant as Facebook pivots to search-engine discovery:

- Use **3-5 hyper-targeted, industry-specific keywords** (e.g., #BusyMomFitness, #ExecutiveWellness)
- Create and consistently use **one branded hashtag** to consolidate community content
- Avoid generic, oversaturated tags (e.g., #fitness, #healthy)

## Post-to-Profile Connection

Your content strategy feeds directly into your optimized profile:

1. **CTA Bridge:** "Tap the link in my bio" → user clicks your profile → if profile is unoptimized, you lose the lead
2. **Visual Validation:** After watching a Reel, the prospect lands on your profile → your 820×360 cover photo must validate your authority
3. **Featured Section Closer:** Posts drive traffic → your Featured Section (4-5 rotating assets) silently captures leads your content generated
