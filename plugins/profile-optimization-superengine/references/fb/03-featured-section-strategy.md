# 03 ... Featured Section Strategy

> **Scope:** The Facebook personal profile Featured Section ... the content showcase that appears directly below the cover photo and bio, acting as a highly visible mini-funnel.

---

## Strategic Purpose

The Featured Section is **not a scrapbook** for random photos or outdated albums. It is a structured sales funnel that guides a cold profile visitor from initial value all the way to booking a call ... without them leaving your profile.

It sits prominently below the cover photo, making it one of the first things prospects see after landing on your profile.

---

## The Mini-Funnel Framework

Pin exactly **4-5 high-value, rotating assets** in a specific psychological progression of trust:

| Slot | Asset Type | Purpose | Content Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | Lead Magnet Carousel | Value-first hook | 5-slide carousel: "Free 7-Day Wellness Challenge PDF" with DM keyword CTA |
| **2** | Client Testimonial Video | Social proof | 45-second video of a client endorsing your coaching (under 60 seconds) |
| **3** | Before/After Reel | Visual transformation | Trending-audio Reel showing side-by-side client transformation |
| **4** | Live Workout Replay | Authority builder | 10-minute Live replay coaching viewers through an equipment-free circuit |
| **5** | Booking Link Post | Direct conversion | Post with direct link to "Free Health Audit" call booking page |

---

## Asset Requirements

### Best Content Formats for Featured
- **Carousels** ... visual, swipeable, high perceived value
- **Short-form video Reels** ... algorithm-favored, attention-grabbing
- **Live video replays** ... builds deep authority and trust
- **Link posts** ... routes to booking pages or lead magnets

### CTA Rules for Featured Captions
All featured assets must use **algorithm-compliant DM triggers**:
- ✅ "DM me 'CHALLENGE' to get started"
- ✅ "Comment your biggest obstacle"
- 🚨 Avoid: "Click the link" (suppressed by algorithm)

---

## Implementation Steps

### Step 1 ... Audit & Create
Review recent profile content. Identify your best-performing:
- Lead magnet post
- Short-form testimonial video
- Transformation Reel
- High-value Live replay

If missing any asset type, create it as a standard timeline post this week.

### Step 2 ... Pin to Featured
Navigate to your personal profile → scroll to Featured Section → tap "Add to Featured" → select the 4-5 posts identified.

### Step 3 ... Organize the Order
Ensure assets follow the trust-building progression:
1. Lead Magnet (most visible, leftmost position)
2. Testimonial Video
3. Transformation Reel
4. Live Replay
5. Booking Link

### Step 4 ... Optimize Captions
Before finalizing, review each featured post's caption. Ensure it uses algorithm-compliant CTAs (DM keyword triggers).

### Step 5 ... Schedule Monthly Review
Set a 30-day calendar reminder to review which featured assets generated the most inbound DMs. Rotate out the lowest-performing asset with fresh proof or a new lead magnet.

---

## Rotation Frequency

| Cadence | Trigger |
|---|---|
| **Monthly (default)** | Swap lowest-performing asset based on DM volume |
| **Immediately** | When a new powerful testimonial or lead magnet is available |
| **Align with campaigns** | Swap lead magnet asset to match current challenge/offer |
