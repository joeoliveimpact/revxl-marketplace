# 04 ... About Section Optimization

> **Scope:** All fields within the Facebook personal profile About section ... Work, Education, Places Lived, Details About You, and Relationship Status. Treated as a searchable sales page, not a resume.

---

## Why the About Section Matters

Facebook operates as a search engine. When users search for "online fitness coach" or "weight loss help," Meta scans profile metadata to deliver results. Meta's AI memory tools (2025+) learn from user interactions to boost accuracy and recommend relevant profiles.

If your About section is filled with irrelevant old jobs instead of keyword-rich coaching terms, **you will not appear in search results** for your ideal clients.

---

## Field-by-Field Optimization

### 1. Work Field (Sales Tool, Not Resume)

**Strategy:** Do NOT list traditional employment. Treat Work as a billboard for your current offer. List your coaching role first so it displays at the top of your profile preview.

**Framework:** `[Job Title/Outcome] at [Your Brand/Company Page] | [Core Offer]`

**Examples:**
- `Online Health Coach @ [YourBrand] (2020-present) | 1:1 transformation programs`
- `Head Fat Loss Coach @ [YourBrand] | Helping busy moms lose 20lbs`
- `Holistic Nutritionist @ [YourBrand] | Gut health & metabolism repair`
- `Online Personal Trainer @ [YourBrand] | Pain-free mobility for executives`

**Tactic:** Always hyperlink the "company" to your Facebook Business Page or booking site. **Delete or hide all irrelevant past jobs** that dilute your authority.

---

### 2. Education Field (Authority Builder)

**Strategy:** Your audience doesn't care where you went to high school. They care whether you're qualified. Highlight industry-specific credentials only.

**Framework:** `[Name of Certification] at [Certifying Body]`

**Examples:**
- `NASM Certified Personal Trainer`
- `Precision Nutrition Level 1 Coach`
- `Certified Pre & Postnatal Coaching Specialist`
- `Registered Dietitian (RD)`

---

### 3. Places Lived (Relatability)

**Strategy:** Online coaching removes geographical barriers, but people still buy from people they relate to. Tie your global business to your local roots.

**Framework:** `[Hometown/Origin] + [Current Base]`

**Example:** *"Grew up in Chicago, now coaching worldwide from Austin, TX"*

---

### 4. Details About You (The Mini-Sales Letter)

**Display rules:** Facebook previews the first ~1,000 characters. The full field supports 65,000+ characters. **Lead with the transformation ... never bury the lead.**

**Conversion Structure:**

| Section | Content | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Dog Whistle Hook** | "Are you a busy professional working 50+ hours, struggling to find time for your health?" | Call out exact target audience |
| **Origin Story (Brief)** | "I used to be burnt out and 30lbs overweight, which is why I created…" | Build empathy |
| **Methodology (Keywords)** | Macro tracking, strength training, metabolic conditioning, habit coaching | Search-friendly terms for discovery |
| **Algorithm-Compliant CTA** | "DM me 'START' to begin your journey" | Drive DM conversions |

### CTA Rules for the About Section

| 🚨 Suppressed Language | 🔥 Amplified Language |
|---|---|
| "Click the link below" | "DM me 'START' to begin your journey" |
| "Message me now" | "Shoot me a DM with the word 'TRANSFORM'" |
| "Sign up here" | "Tap the link in my bio" |

---

### 5. Relationship Status

Set to **"Work"** or a custom setting to keep the focus entirely professional.

---

## The Responsive DM Badge

If you consistently reply to inbound DMs within 1 hour, Facebook auto-applies a **"Responsive" badge** to your profile. This visual trust signal heavily boosts prospect confidence and conversion rates (up to 25% improvement).

---

## Common About Section Mistakes

| Mistake | Impact |
|---|---|
| Treating it like a resume | "Bartender at Target (2012-2014)" kills your positioning as a health authority |
| Wasting the 1,000-char preview | If the first 1,000 characters are about pets/hobbies, prospects bounce |
| Dead links in Work field | Generic company name with no hyperlink is a dead end |
| Algorithm-penalized links | Cramming external links into text suppresses reach ... drive to DMs instead |
