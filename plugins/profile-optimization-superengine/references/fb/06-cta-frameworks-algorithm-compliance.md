# 06 ... CTA Frameworks & Algorithm Compliance

> **Scope:** Complete Call-to-Action strategy for Facebook personal profiles ... algorithm-compliant language, DM keyword triggers, soft vs. hard CTAs, the single-direct-link standard, and spam filter avoidance.

## Algorithm Language: The Foundation

The Meta algorithm actively scans post text, captions, and video audio. Using the wrong CTA language triggers reach suppression **before anyone even sees your content**. Facebook also warns that placing hyperlinks directly in captions hurts reach (as of June 2025).

### Master Reference Table

| 🚨 Suppressed (AVOID) | 🔥 Amplified (USE) |
|---|---|
| "Click the link" | "DM me '[KEYWORD]'" |
| "Message me now" | "Comment your biggest [challenge]" |
| "Like and share" | "Save this" |
| "Tag a friend" | "Try this next" |
| "Drop a comment to get X" | "Tap the link in bio" |

## Personal Profile vs. Business Page CTAs

| Feature | Personal Profile | Business Page |
|---|---|---|
| **Initiate DMs** | ✅ Can message anyone who engages | ❌ Cannot initiate ... must wait for inbound |
| **Organic reach** | High (guaranteed exposure) | Near-zero (pay-to-play) |
| **Best CTA deployment** | DM keyword triggers → 1:1 conversation | Hope for inbound messages |

**Bottom line:** Personal profiles are superior for organic CTA deployment because you can proactively start conversations.

## DM Keyword Triggers

The most effective CTA for a coach on a personal profile. Moves the prospect from public feed into private 1:1 conversation.

### Compliant Execution

| ❌ Algorithm-Penalized | ✅ Compliant Alternative |
|---|---|
| "Drop a comment to get my free macro guide" | "DM me 'PLAN' and I'll send you the exact progression framework" |
| "Comment below for the link" | "Comment your biggest obstacle and I'll coach you in the comments" |

### DM Warm-Up Protocol (Avoid Spam Filters)

1. **Warm up the inbox:** Send new followers a brief, non-salesy welcome message (e.g., "Hey Sarah, nice to connect with you here. Enjoy your day!"). Establishes normal messaging history.
2. **Ask for permission:** Never drop links unprompted. Reply to comments: "I have a few ideas on that. Mind if I send you a DM?"
3. **Engage every comment:** Like + reply publicly (e.g., "Just sent you details in Messenger, see it?"). This signals genuine community to the algorithm.

Used on Education, Lifestyle, and Quick Win content. Goal: algorithm-feeding engagement.

| Framework | Examples |

| Framework | Examples |

## CTA Templates by Post Type

### Educational Video Reel
- **Placement:** End of video + bottom of caption
- **Template:** "If your lower back hurts during squats, try these 3 mobility drills next. Save this video so you don't forget it at the gym! 📌 👇"

### Social Proof / Client Transformation
- **Placement:** Bottom of caption after proof
- **Template:** "Sarah trained 3x/week with me and hit her first pull-up in 12 weeks. Comment 'GOAL' and DM me 'PLAN' ... I'll send you the exact progression framework."

### Direct Promotional Offer
- **Placement:** Top of caption (above "see more" fold) + link in comments
- **Template:** "Ready to drop the restrictive diets? Tap the link in my bio to get your free 7-day macro cheat sheet. 🔗"

## The Single-Link Standard (2026)

Since links in captions suppress reach, your bio link carries the traffic... so it has to convert. The 2026 standard is ONE direct link to ONE lead magnet, not a menu.

### Junk Drawer vs. Single-Link Standard

| | Multi-Link Aggregator (anti-pattern) | Single-Link Standard (2026) |
|---|---|---|
| **What it is** | Linktree / Beacons / Stan page with 5-10+ buttons (booking, affiliate codes, podcast, blog, merch) | One direct link to one offer |
| **Prospect experience** | Decision paralysis, "what do I even click?" | Zero friction, one obvious next step |
| **What it signals** | Scattered, unprofessional, hedging your bets | Focused, confident, knows its funnel |
| **Where it points** | Everywhere at once | The lead magnet, specifically |
| **2026 verdict** | Actively hurts conversion in high-ticket coaching | Best practice |

Handing a prospect a page stacked with buttons creates a "link burden." More buttons does not mean more conversions... it means fewer. Legacy multi-link aggregators (Linktree, Beacons, Stan in multi-button mode) read as "digital junk drawers" in the high-ticket coaching space. They only ever appear here as a named anti-pattern to remove.

### What Your One Link Should Point To

Never point cold traffic straight to a high-ticket booking or sales calendar. The correct evergreen sequence is strictly:

**Lead Magnet ... Qualification ... Call**

| Funnel Stage | What Happens | Why It's There |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Lead Magnet** | Free resource, training, or guide (the bio link destination) | Cold traffic is skeptical. You give value first, before any pitch. |
| **2. Qualification** | A few questions on current obstacles + readiness | Filters tire-kickers before they ever reach your calendar |
| **3. Call** | Booking link, offered only to qualified prospects | Anyone who reaches this stage is pre-qualified, not cold |

**Common mistake:** sending every profile visitor straight to a booking calendar. Cold traffic isn't ready to book, so the click is wasted. Point the one link at the lead magnet.

**Note on tracking:** a custom domain later can add UTM tracking and keep users in your ecosystem, but it is still ONE direct destination... not a multi-button aggregator. Fixing the link to a single lead-magnet destination is the quick win.
