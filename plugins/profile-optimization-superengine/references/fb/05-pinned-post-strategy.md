# 05 ... Pinned Post Strategy

> **Scope:** The single pinned post that appears at the top of your Facebook personal profile timeline ... its format, structure, rotation system, and CTA deployment.

---

## Why Pinned Posts Matter

Your pinned post is the **conversion anchor** of your profile. While the bio and cover photo generate interest, the pinned post is the heavy lifter that converts profile visitors into inbound DM leads.

On a personal profile (unlike a Business Page with zero organic reach), a pinned post acts as a highly visible mini-sales page.

---

## Format: Video Reels Only

**🚨 Outdated:** Pinning a text-heavy post with a static photo or link.

In 2025-2026, the Meta algorithm heavily favors **native short-form video (Reels)** over carousels, text, or images. Video captures attention within 3 seconds and holds engagement significantly longer.

**Always pin a Reel as your primary pinned post.**

---

## The Pinned Reel Structure (Under 45 Seconds)

| Timestamp | Section | Content |
|---|---|---|
| **0-3 sec** | Hook | Movement demo, shocking stat, or relatable B2C pain point |
| **3-15 sec** | Body | Demonstrate the solution, framework, or tangible result |
| **15-30 sec** | Proof | Visual social proof or client win |
| **30-45 sec** | CTA | Specific DM keyword trigger |

### Caption Formula
Structure as: **Problem Statement → Core Benefit → Social Proof → Algorithm-Compliant Keyword CTA → Urgency Line**

---

## The 4-Pillar Rotation System

Rotate your pinned post through four content pillars to prevent audience blindness:

| Pillar | Framework | Caption Length | Best CTA |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Education** | "One problem + three solutions" | 40-80 words | "Try this next" / "Save this" |
| **Social Proof** | Client transformation + quantified result | 30-60 words | "DM me 'PLAN'" / "Comment your goal" |
| **Lifestyle/Habit** | Daily win or quick checklist | 50-100 words | "Save this" / "Comment if you do this" |
| **Offer/Conversion** | Free challenge, 7-day starter | 60-90 words | "DM 'READY'" / "Tap link in bio" |

### Pillar Examples

**Social Proof Template:**
> "Sarah trained 3x/week with me and hit her first pull-up in 12 weeks. Comment 'GOAL' and DM me 'PLAN' ... I'll send you the exact progression framework."

**Education Example:**
> Reel demonstrating three ways to fix lower back pain during squats.

**Lifestyle Example:**
> Reel showing Sunday meal prep routine in under 15 minutes.

---

## CTA Placement by Content Type

| Content Type | CTA Location |
|---|---|
| Educational Reel | End of video + bottom of caption |
| Client Win (Proof) | Bottom of caption after social proof |
| Promotional Offer | Top of caption + link in comments |

---

## Algorithm-Compliant CTA Language

| 🚨 Suppressed (AVOID) | 🔥 Amplified (USE) |
|---|---|
| "Click the link" | "Save this" |
| "Like and share" | "Try this next" |
| "Tag a friend" | "Comment your biggest [challenge]" |
| "Drop a comment to get X" | "DM me '[KEYWORD]'" |
| "Message me now" | "Tap the link in bio" |

---

## Refresh Cadence

| Schedule | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Default** | Change every **2-4 weeks**, rotating to the next pillar |
| **Refresh sooner** | If generating < 5 DMs/comments per week |
| **Hold longer** | If post goes viral (50+ DMs in 24-48 hours) |

---

## Connecting to the Profile Ecosystem

1. **Bio Handoff:** Your 101-character bio hook (e.g., "DM 'FIT' for free plan 👇") must be validated by your pinned post delivering that promised value.
2. **Featured Section Synergy:** Featured hosts static assets (lead magnets, booking links); the pinned post is the dynamic, rotating video billboard.
3. **Link-in-Bio Bridge:** If using "Tap the link in bio" CTA, ensure it points to your ONE direct lead-magnet link, not a multi-link aggregator (see File 06).
