# 05 ... Story Highlights Funnel

> **Scope:** Instagram Story Highlights ... the row of circles that sits directly below your bio. For an online fitness, health, or wellness coach, this is your primary sales funnel and website navigation bar, built inside the app.

---

## Strategic Purpose

Highlights are not a scrapbook for your dog photos, vacations, or random weekend meals. They sit in prime real estate directly below your optimized bio, so they capture a profile visitor's attention immediately.

While Reels drive cold discovery, Highlights are what keep a prospect on your page. They systematically build credibility and reduce friction in the buying journey. Instead of forcing a lead to click away to a website to learn how you solve their metabolism or fitness problem, a structured Highlight funnel feeds them exactly what they need to convert.

---

## Highlight Quantity

Limit your profile to **8 to 10 Highlights maximum**. Any more creates digital clutter, overwhelms the prospect, and causes decision paralysis. Quality overrides quantity: if a Highlight doesn't directly serve a business goal, delete it.

---

## The Funnel Order (Highlight Stacking)

Treat your Highlights like the navigation menu of a high-converting landing page, ordered by what a cold B2C prospect needs to see first to make a purchasing decision. Left to right, this is the sequential order:

| Slot | Highlight | Purpose | Content Goes Here |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | Start Here / What I Do | Orients a cold visitor | Your coaching philosophy, your specific niche, the primary transformation you offer |
| **2** | Proof / Testimonials | Most critical Highlight ... profiles with a testimonial highlight convert 47% more visitors into paying customers | Before-and-after visual proof, video testimonials from clients |
| **3** | The Offer / Services | Explains exactly how to work with you | Your 12-week program, your app, your 1:1 coaching structure |
| **4** | FAQ / Pricing | Kills objections before the prospect has to ask | Common questions, pricing structure, what's included |
| **5** | About Me / BTS | Builds parasocial connection | Your own workouts, meal preps, daily routine |

**Crucial:** every single story inside every one of these Highlights needs a clear CTA ... "DM me 'START' for pricing" or "Tap the link in my bio to book a consult." Do not leave the prospect guessing what to do next.

---

## Naming Conventions & Instagram SEO

Highlight names are indexed and searchable. Use highly descriptive, keyword-rich titles.

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Length | 1 to 3 words maximum |
| Avoid | Vague, outdated names like "Stuff," "Me," "Random," or blank titles ... ignored by modern consumers |
| Do | Clear, descriptive, action-oriented names ... 64% of profile visitors click Highlights with clear names |
| Examples | *Client Results*, *Services*, *Fat Loss FAQ*, *Book a Call* |
| Emoji | One on-brand emoji is fine if it fits your aesthetic ... don't stack multiple |

---

## Branded Cover Design Specs

Mismatched, messy Highlight covers look cheap and unprofessional. Cohesive covers build immediate visual authority and brand consistency ... profiles with cohesive highlight covers get 28% more profile visits than ones with mismatched, random cover photos.

| Spec | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Tool | Canva (free) |
| Colors | Stick to 2-3 of your core brand colors |
| Style | Consistent font or one recognizable icon across all covers |
| Text | Massive, high-contrast (e.g. white text on dark background) ... covers render as tiny circles on mobile |
| Dimensions | Design at 1080 x 1350 px, export as PNG, then adjust the circular crop inside the app |

---

## Rotation & Refresh

Highlights aren't "set and forget." Revisit the lineup whenever:

| Trigger | Action |
|---|---|
| New/stronger testimonial comes in | Swap it into the Proof Highlight immediately ... this is your highest-leverage slot |
| Offer or pricing changes | Update the Offer and FAQ/Pricing Highlights same week ... stale pricing kills trust |
| A Highlight stops serving a business goal | Delete it, don't let it sit at 8-10 out of inertia |
| Quarterly profile audit | Re-check cover consistency, naming, and that every story still has a CTA |

---

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Why It Kills Conversion | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| More than 10 Highlights | Clutter causes decision paralysis | Cut to 8-10, delete anything that doesn't serve the funnel |
| Vague names ("Stuff," "Me," "Random") | Ignored by modern consumers, hurts IG SEO | Rename with 1-3 word, keyword-rich, action-oriented titles |
| Mismatched/random covers | Reads as unprofessional, costs profile visits | Design all covers in Canva with 2-3 brand colors and one consistent font/icon |
| No Proof/Testimonials Highlight | Skips the single highest-converting slot | Add it as slot 2, lead with video testimonials and before/afters |
| Stories with no CTA | Prospect doesn't know the next step | Add "DM me '[KEYWORD]'" or "link in bio" to every story in every Highlight |
| Small/low-contrast cover text | Unreadable at the tiny circle size Highlights render at | Use massive, high-contrast text (white on dark) at 1080x1350 |
