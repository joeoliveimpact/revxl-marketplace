# 10 ... Instagram SEO

> **Scope:** The full Instagram search/discovery system in 2026 ... how IG search, Explore, and Google indexing decide who ranks, and where to place keywords across your profile and content to win that ranking. Covers the Name field, bio, captions, alt text, on-screen text/audio, and hashtags as one connected keyword system (not each field in isolation ... see `01-name-field-and-handle-seo.md` and the bio resource for field-level depth).

---

## Why This Matters in 2026

Instagram has fundamentally shifted from a visual scrolling feed into a search engine. Nearly one in three consumers now start their search for products and services on platforms like Instagram rather than Google. If you're still running outdated tactics ... burying 30 random hashtags at the bottom of a post, or using a vague "aesthetic" profile name ... you are invisible to high-intent B2C coaching leads searching for exactly what you offer.

---

## How Instagram Search & Discovery Work in 2026

- **Semantic search, not just engagement history.** The algorithm no longer guesses what users want based solely on their past likes. It relies on explicit search behavior and the "Your Algorithm" feature, which lets users curate and prioritize the exact topics they care about (e.g., "longevity" or "postpartum health").
- **IG indexes spoken words, on-screen text, and captions.** Instagram's AI actively reads and listens to your content to categorize it ... not just the caption text, but what you say out loud and what you type on-screen in Reels.
- **Keywords have replaced hashtags as the primary discoverability driver.** Targeted keywords placed naturally across your profile and content now carry more algorithmic weight than hashtag volume.

**What this means for you:** every field on your profile and every piece of content is a ranking signal. Treat your Name field, bio, captions, alt text, and Reels the same way you'd treat a Google-indexed webpage ... built around the exact phrases your ideal client types.

## Exact Keyword Placement Blueprint

| Placement | Character/Format Limit | What To Do |
|---|---|---|
| **Name Field** | 64 characters (raised from 30 ... stale sources still say 30), word-for-word searchable | Choose ONE primary keyword pillar. Formula: `[Name] \| [Specific Niche]` ... e.g., "Sarah \| Perimenopause Fitness" or "David • Men's Gut Health." (Full field-level tactics in `01-name-field-and-handle-seo.md`.) |
| **Bio (first line + 5 Interests)** | 150 characters | Primary keyword must appear in the very first line. Also tag your account with the 2026 "5 Interests" profile feature (e.g., "Strength Training," "Hormone Health") to feed the recommendation engine. |
| **Captions** | No stuffing | Place your primary search phrase naturally inside the hook ... the first 1-2 lines ... so it's immediately obvious what the post is about. Do not keyword-stuff like a robot. |
| **Alt Text** | Per-image field | Use descriptive alt text on every image to add another indexed layer of search discoverability. |
| **On-Screen Text & Spoken Audio (Reels)** | First 3 seconds | Say your exact target keyword out loud within the first 3 seconds of the video, AND type that exact phrase as on-screen text. A massive share of discovery now happens via Reels, and IG's AI reads text and listens to audio. |
| **Hashtags** | 3-5 max | Outdated 2010s tactic if overused. Since keywords carry more algorithmic weight now, use only a few highly relevant, niche-specific tags (e.g., `#PCOSweightloss`) ... skip generic tags like `#fitness`. |

## How to Rank on Google and the Explore Page

Three backend settings force your profile and content to rank in places beyond the IG app itself:

- **Rank on Google.** Go to Instagram Settings → Account Privacy and enable "Public photos and videos to appear in search engine results." This lets your Reels and profile rank directly on Google search pages alongside YouTube videos, driving external cold traffic straight to your funnel.
- **Rank in Suggested Accounts.** Log into Instagram on a desktop browser (or desktop mode on mobile browser), go to Edit Profile, and manually turn on "Account Suggestions." This gets your profile actively recommended to users who just followed a similar fitness coach.
- **Rank on Explore.** The algorithm's favorite ranking metrics now are Sends (DM shares) and Saves. Build tactical carousels or relatable Reels that prompt users to share into private group chats ... the algorithm treats that share behavior as the strongest quality signal available.

## Keyword Research Tactics (Concrete Workflow)

Stop guessing what your clients are searching for. Run this workflow:

1. **Mine the search bar.** Type your broad niche (e.g., "fix my metabolism") into the IG search bar but don't hit enter. Watch the auto-suggestions ... these are the exact, real-world phrases consumers are actively typing.
2. **Use external tools.** Run the same broad niche through AnswerThePublic.com or Google Trends to surface the exact questions your B2C audience is asking.
3. **Group into 3-5 content pillars.** Take the verified phrases from steps 1-2 and cluster them (e.g., Pillar 1: High-Protein Breakfasts; Pillar 2: Low-Impact Joint Workouts). Build every caption, hook, and Reel cover exclusively around these verified search phrases ... not phrases you assume are popular.

## Common SEO Mistakes

| Mistake | Impact |
|---|---|
| Vague or "aesthetic" Name field with no niche keyword | Invisible to cold, problem-aware search ... the single highest-leverage SEO slot goes to waste |
| Burying 30+ generic hashtags at the bottom of a post | Outdated 2010s tactic; keywords now carry more algorithmic weight than hashtag volume |
| Keyword-stuffing captions unnaturally | Reads as robotic and gets suppressed rather than rewarded |
| Skipping alt text on images | Leaves a free, indexed discoverability layer completely unused |
| Not saying/typing the target keyword in the first 3 seconds of a Reel | Misses the on-screen text + spoken audio indexing that drives most 2026 discovery |
| Never enabling "search engine results" in Account Privacy | Profile and Reels stay invisible to Google's index ... no external cold traffic |
| Leaving "Account Suggestions" off | Profile never gets pushed into Suggested Accounts for similar-niche followers |
| Optimizing for likes instead of Sends/Saves | Misses the metrics Explore's algorithm actually ranks on |
