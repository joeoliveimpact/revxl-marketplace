## Package: "The 2pm Crash Protocol" — Educational/Listicle (Blueprint A)

**Platform:** Instagram (4:5, 1080×1350) · **Objective:** saves primary, DM leads secondary
**Avatar pain targeted:** "I eat healthy all morning and still hit a wall at 2pm" (config pain #2)
**Pillar:** energy/metabolism · **Voice edge:** conversational

### Slide map

| n | Role | Job (≤12 words) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hook #1 (anti-guru) | Break the "healthy breakfast" assumption, promise the fix |
| 2 | Hook #2 (credibility, standalone) | Reframe: crash is mechanical, not willpower |
| 3 | Hook #3 (roadmap, standalone) | Name the 3 culprits, open the #2 loop |
| 4 | Mistake 1 | Caffeine timing... first fix, with the why |
| 5 | Mistake 2 | Naked-carb breakfast... the spike-crash mechanism |
| 6 | Mistake 3 | Skipped morning light... the circadian anchor |

| 10 | Hard CTA | One action: DM keyword |

### Slides

**Slide 1 — Hook**
- **Headline:** "Your 'healthy' breakfast is causing your 2pm crash."
- **Body:** none (headline carries it).
- **Visual:** cream base; headline in deep forest green, 40px bold, upper two-thirds; coach cutout
  bottom-right with white sticker outline, looking AT the text; tiny accent arrow bottom-right.
- **Retention device:** micro-arrow + the accusation opens a defend-myself loop.

**Slide 2 — Standalone hook / reframe**
- **Headline:** "You don't have an energy problem. You have a blood-sugar rollercoaster."
- **Body:** "That wall at 2pm isn't willpower. It's the crash side of a spike you built at 7am."
  (22 words)
- **Visual:** solid forest-green base, cream centered type; simple hand-drawn-style curve graphic:
  spike at "7am" dropping through "2pm"... two labels only.
- **Retention device:** works cold for re-serve entry; curve visually "falls off" the right edge
  (panorama seam into slide 3).

**Slide 3 — Standalone hook / roadmap**
- **Headline:** "3 morning habits build that crash. #2 is the one 'clean eaters' refuse to believe."
- **Body:** none.
- **Visual:** cream base; numbered 1-2-3 list skeleton with items blurred/redacted; continuation of
  the slide-2 curve entering from the left seam.
- **Retention device:** reverse-expectation tease on #2 + visual continuity.

**Slide 4 — Mistake 1**
- **Headline:** "1. Coffee in the first 30 minutes."
- **Body:** "Caffeine on a cold engine borrows energy your afternoon pays back. **Push your first
  cup 90 minutes after waking**... adenosine clears on its own and the 2pm bill never arrives."
  (30 words, bold = skim path)
- **Visual:** cream base; large "1." in accent coral; coffee-cup photo cutout left; headline green.
- **Retention device:** none (clean value slide).

**Slide 5 — Mistake 2**
- **Headline:** "2. A carb-only 'healthy' breakfast."
- **Body:** "Oatmeal + fruit + juice = a naked-carb spike. **Anchor breakfast with 30g of
  protein**... same foods, add the anchor, the curve flattens, the crash never fires." (27 words)
- **Visual:** split layout: "spike plate" left vs "anchored plate" right, real-food photos, thin
  green divider; small curve icon under each (spike vs flat).
- **Retention device:** this is the teased #2... payoff lands, credibility banked.

**Slide 6 — Mistake 3**
- **Headline:** "3. Screens before sunlight."
- **Body:** "Morning light sets the hormone clock that decides your 2pm alertness. **10 minutes
  outside before the phone** beats a second coffee every time." (24 words)
- **Visual:** warm photo of morning light through a window, text on a cream card overlay
  bottom-third.
- **Retention device:** "but here's the catch →" micro-teaser bottom-right corner.

**Slide 7 — Pattern break**
- **Headline:** "Here's the part nobody tells you..."
- **Body:** "Fixing ONE of these does almost nothing. They're a stack. The order you fix them in is
  the whole game... and it's on the next slide." (26 words)
- **Visual:** flip the palette: forest-green base, cream text, accent exclamation mark; nothing
  else on the slide.
- **Retention device:** explicit cliffhanger at the pre-CTA attention dip.

**Slide 8 — Implementation**
- **Headline:** "The 2pm-proof morning (in order):"
- **Body:** checklist card: "① Light first: 10 min outside ② Protein anchor: 30g at breakfast
  ③ Coffee at 90 min, not 0 ④ Carbs AFTER protein, never naked" (25 words)
- **Visual:** the save-magnet card: bordered checklist table, cream on green, generous spacing,
  numbered in accent coral; designed to be screenshot-clean.
- **Retention device:** the promised "order" pays the slide-7 loop.

**Slide 9 — Proof + soft CTA**
- **Headline:** "Same client. 3 weeks. No new supplements... just the order."
- **Body:** "'I stopped planning my day around the crash.' ... [client], 41, desk job, two kids."
  (16 words) + save badge: "**Save this checklist for tomorrow morning.**"
- **Visual:** real client message screenshot (cropped, name covered) on cream base; rounded save
  badge bottom-center in accent coral.
- **Retention device:** soft CTA at the value peak (the ~31% save lift lives here).

**Slide 10 — Hard CTA**
- **Headline:** "Want the full 7-day energy reset?"
- **Body:** "Comment '**ENERGY**' and I'll DM you the complete morning-by-morning protocol. Free."
  (13 words)
- **Visual:** coach headshot circular cutout center; handle small bottom-left; keyword in a
  coral mockup-button; one-line disclaimer footer 12px: "General education, not medical advice."
- **Retention device:** n/a... transaction window, one action only.
