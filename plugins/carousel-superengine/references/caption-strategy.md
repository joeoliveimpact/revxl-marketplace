# Caption Strategy — the second engine

Slides earn the swipe; the caption indexes the post, deepens trust, and closes the DM. A pasted-in
copy of the slides suffocates reach. Two engines, division of labor:

| | Slides (flashcard) | Caption (deep dive) |
|---|---|---|
| Pacing | fast, punchy | conversational, narrative |
| Job | stop scroll, earn swipe, drive save | authority, search indexing, comments/DMs |
| Content | one bold claim per slide | the how-I backstory, technical detail that wouldn't fit under the 20% rule |
| Length | 25-50 words/slide | **150-300 words** (carousel sweet spot) |

Why long works here (and not on Reels): after 8-12 swipes the reader is in high-focus reading mode
and WANTS the full story. Reels captions stay short; carousel captions are a mini-blog.

## Instagram SEO (2026 reality)

Captions are semantically indexed — in-app search AND external engines. Reach is keyword-driven now:
- **Front-load:** primary search phrase inside the **first 125 characters** (the pre-"more"
  preview). "Hey guys, so excited..." wastes the single most valuable line.
- **Write in search queries:** the avatar types problems, not concepts... fold their literal phrasing
  (from `{{AVATAR_PAINS}}` / VoC) into the copy naturally.
- **Custom alt text per slide:** one descriptive keyword-rich line each; never leave auto-generated.

## Hashtags (the 2021 detox)

- **3-5 hyper-targeted tags**, end of caption (in-caption indexes better than first-comment).
- Narrow intent tags over mega-tags... a niche tag the avatar actually follows beats #fitness where
  the post drowns. Volume-stuffing is dead: no reach correlation, reads spammy.

## The 4-part carousel caption

1. **Secondary hook (0-125 chars):** mirrors (not repeats) the cover hook; carries the primary
   keyword + transformation hint.
2. **The how-I context:** the personal or client story behind the slides, in the coach's voice at
   the configured edge. This is where entertainment-first lives in text.
3. **The actionable expansion:** the numbered specifics that wouldn't fit on slides ("Slide 5 said
   fix your sleep... exact protocol: 1... 2... 3..."). This is what makes the SAVE worth it.
4. **The weighted CTA:** the same ONE action as the final slide, restated + keyword trigger.
   Never a second competing ask.

LinkedIn variant: same 4 parts, professional register, hook line written for the feed's "see more"
fold, hashtags 3 max.
