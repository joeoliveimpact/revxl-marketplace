# Platform Nuance — Instagram vs LinkedIn (2026)

Same idea, two different machines. Decide the platform BEFORE structuring; adapt at the design
stage, not after.

## The 6 differences that change the build

| Dimension | Instagram | LinkedIn |
|---|---|---|
| Format | Native multi-image/video post (PNG for text-heavy slides, JPG for photos; mixed video encouraged) | **Multi-page PDF** (native carousels retired; a PDF auto-renders as swipeable document). No video/GIF inside |
| Canvas | **1080×1350 (4:5 portrait)** — 20% more mobile screen than square (1080×1440 3:4 also current-valid) | **1080×1080 (1:1 square)** — stays sharp on desktop + mobile render |
| Count | 2-20 slides; **8-12 sweet spot** | **5-10 pages** (file under 10MB or LinkedIn renders slowly) |
| Tone | Emotional, personal, how-I narrative; edge and humor welcome (per `{{VOICE_EDGE}}`) | Professional, data-driven, framework-first; authority (E-E-A-T) over intimacy |
| Ranking signals | **DM shares (sends per reach)** strongest; dwell time next; saves valuable but 2026-contested weight (see note) | **Dwell time + completion rate** (the 2026 "Depth Score"); long shelf life (quality PDFs resurface for weeks) |
| Mechanics | **Re-serve mechanic:** skipped carousels return opening on slide 2/3 → Triple Hook mandatory. Audio added before posting → Reels-tab eligibility (extra distribution surface, 2026) | No live links on pages → CTA routes to comments/profile/DM; PDFs are downloadable → watermark every page |

> **2026 signal notes (evolving — re-verify before hard-coding):** some 2026 Instagram-sourced
> reporting says saves are downweighted while other current sources still rank them high...
> emphasize shares/sends for reach and treat saves as contested, not dead. LinkedIn's 2026
> dwell-time shift ("Depth Score") also penalizes external links and engagement bait (~60% reach
> cost) — route CTAs per the table. Instagram now supports per-slide captions (live June 2026, up
> to 20 — see @caption-strategy.md).

## Adapting one carousel across both (the 3-rule workflow)

1. **One master brand kit** — same two fonts + palette both places; visual identity never forks.
2. **Design 4:5 IG first, then resize** — duplicate the project, one-click resize to 1:1, re-check
   text sizes (square crops tighten), export PDF for LinkedIn.
3. **Swap dynamic for static** — replace IG's video loop slide with a high-quality chart/photo;
   LinkedIn PDFs are static, and data visuals carry the authority there anyway.

## Content adaptation (more than resizing)

- **Hook:** IG leads personal ("How I..."); LinkedIn leads authority ("Why 70% of [avatar segment]
  hit [problem]: the data").
- **Proof:** IG shows the human receipt (DM screenshot, transformation); LinkedIn shows the chart
  (trend line, before/after table, n= framing).
- **CTA:** IG → DM keyword or save trigger; LinkedIn → follow-the-series, "DM me [WORD]", or
  link-in-comments gateway (see @cta-slide-patterns.md pattern 5).
- **Caption:** IG caption = SEO + mini-blog (see @caption-strategy.md); LinkedIn post text =
  professional hook + skimmable context above the document.
- `{{PRIMARY_PLATFORM}}: both` → build IG-first, deliver both variants in one package with the
  deltas listed, never two full rebuilds.
