# Say This → Not That: line-level rewrites

Niche-agnostic weak→strong rewrites for reel hooks and key lines. Each pair is tagged with
which of the **4 Hook Killers** it fixes (DELAY, CONFUSION, IRRELEVANCE, DISINTEREST; see
`./hook-diagnostics.md`). Use these as the universal screen on every option, and to
flag a weak line after the picks (who fixes it: `../../reel-scripter/SKILL.md` Step 3). Openings themselves come from the `hook` skill; this file
screens them, it does not write them.

Placeholders: `[pain]`, `[outcome]`, `[mechanism]`, `[timeframe]`, `[number]`, `[topic]`,
`[audience]`, `[scapegoat]`.

---

## Fixes IRRELEVANCE: creator-POV "I/my" → viewer-benefit "you"
The viewer asks "is this for me?" in the first 1 to 2 seconds. Reframe the experience as their pain.

| ❌ Not that | ✅ Say this |
|---|---|
| "I discovered a way to [outcome]." | "Here's how you [outcome], even if [common obstacle]." |
| "Let me share my experience with [topic]." | "If you're stuck on [pain], this is the part nobody told you." |
| "My routine for [outcome]." | "The [routine] that fixes [pain] for [audience]." |
| "I struggled with [pain] for [timeframe]." | "You're not failing at [topic]. You're missing [mechanism]." |

## Fixes DISINTEREST: vague → specific or numeric
A flat, true statement has no open loop. Add a number, a contrast, or a concrete stake.

| ❌ Not that | ✅ Say this |
|---|---|
| "There are some things that help with [pain]." | "[number] things quietly cause [pain], and you're doing #[k] daily." |
| "[topic] is really important." | "Everyone gets [topic] wrong. Here's what actually [drives outcome]." |
| "This can improve your [outcome]." | "This cut [pain] in [timeframe], without [common sacrifice]." |
| "A lot of people deal with [pain]." | "Most [audience] do [mistake] without realizing it." |

## Fixes DELAY: buried payoff → front-loaded value
Delete everything before the subject. The first noun phrase carries the point.

| ❌ Not that | ✅ Say this |
|---|---|
| "Okay so, let's talk about [topic]." | "[topic] is why you're still [pain]." |
| "I want to give you some background first." | "Skip the theory. Here's the one move that [outcome]." |
| "So I've been thinking about this lately…" | "Stop [common action]. It's making [pain] worse." |
| "Today's video is about [topic], which matters because…" | "Your [pain] comes down to [mechanism]. Here's the fix." |

## Fixes CONFUSION: jargon or context-assuming → plain and immediate
Fewer words, simpler words, active voice, one idea per sentence.

| ❌ Not that | ✅ Say this |
|---|---|
| "Optimizing your [jargon] via [jargon] can upregulate [outcome]." | "Do this one thing and [outcome] gets easier." |
| "[Topic] is multifactorial and depends on several variables." | "[outcome] really comes down to one thing: [mechanism]." |
| "As I mentioned in my last post about [topic]…" | "Here's the part of [topic] that actually matters." |
| "Leveraging [mechanism] to mitigate [pain] is suboptimal." | "[mechanism] won't fix [pain]. Here's what does." |
| "Here are the [number] ways I grew the most last year." (grew *what*? a split subject) | "The [number] fastest ways for [audience] to [specific outcome]." (one subject, one question) |

## Approach losers: patterns, not lines
These lose at the *approach* level; no single-line rewrite fixes them. Restructure the move.

| ❌ Not that | ✅ Do this | Killer |
|---|---|---|
| Stacked asks: "Comment [X], DM me [Y], save this, AND follow." | **ONE CTA** per reel, the same ask in script and caption. Pick the action that matches the value delivered. | CONFUSION |
| Back-to-back teases: "It gets crazier… but wait, there's more…" with no payoff between | Deliver a real piece of value between every tease: each new loop sits on something the viewer just learned. | DISINTEREST |
| Assuming context: "like I said in part 2…" / hooks that need your last post | Every reel stands alone: the viewer has ZERO context before the hook. | CONFUSION |

## Openers that reliably lose (mapped to the 4 Killers)

| Losing opener | Killer | Why it dies | Fix → |
|---|---|---|---|
| "Okay so… let's talk about [topic]." | DELAY | Throat-clear plus topic announcement; the subject arrives after the viewer has left. | Open ON the first interesting word. |
| "So I've been thinking about this for a while…" | DELAY | Pure preamble; zero subject in second 1. | Delete it; lead with the claim. |
| "Hey guys, welcome back to my page…" | DELAY | The greeting burns the attention window. | Cut the greeting entirely. |
| "I'm gonna try not to ramble, but…" | DELAY / DISINTEREST | The hedge lowers the stakes and signals filler. | State the payoff with confidence. |
| "[One long passive multi-clause sentence that buries the point]." | CONFUSION | Needs a re-read; the viewer scrolls instead. | One idea, active voice, plain words. |
| "Today I want to share my experience with [topic]." | IRRELEVANCE | Creator-POV; the viewer hasn't been told why it's theirs. | Reframe to "you" plus the pain. |
| "[Topic] is really important and something I care about." | DISINTEREST | True but tensionless: no open loop. | Add an A-vs-B contrast. |
| "Let me give you a little background first." | DELAY | Openly puts off the payoff. | Skip the background; deliver, fill in later. |
| "You won't BELIEVE what happened next…" (tease, no subject) | DISINTEREST / CONFUSION | A pure tease with nothing concrete to be curious about. It reads as clickbait, and the loop it opens has no subject to hold. | Name the subject, then open a real gap on it. |
| "Comment 'X' and follow before I even start…" (ask before value) | DELAY | Asks before any value exists; the CTA hasn't been earned and burns the attention window. | Hook first; one CTA after the payoff. |

**One-line test:** if the first 5 spoken or on-screen words don't name the subject AND a
tension or a "you", the opener is leaking one of the four Killers and does not clear the screen.

## Quick reference: the four swaps
| If the line is… | Killer | Do this |
|---|---|---|
| about you, not them | IRRELEVANCE | me/my → you/your; lead with their pain |
| true but flat | DISINTEREST | add a number, contrast, or stake |
| slow to the point | DELAY | cut the wind-up; subject in first 5 words |
| dense / insider | CONFUSION | one idea, plain words, active voice |

**Throat-clears to delete on sight (all DELAY):** "Okay so," "I'm gonna," "Let's talk about,"
"Hey guys/ladies," "So basically," "Just a quick one," "Before I start." The best reels open
*on* the first interesting word.
