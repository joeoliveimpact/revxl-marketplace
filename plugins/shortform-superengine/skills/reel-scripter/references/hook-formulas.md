# Hook Formulas — view-validated templates

A bank of hook templates distilled from **titles/openers that actually performed** (view-selected
across multiple creators and niches). Each formula maps to one of the hook buckets the analysis
detects, so you can go: *analysis result → bucket → pick a formula → fill it in.* These are the
proven *shapes*; `opener-patterns.md` has the per-bucket variants and `story-locks.md` is the
word-level pass that tightens whatever you write here.

Placeholders: `[pain]`, `[outcome]`, `[mechanism]`, `[topic]`, `[number]`, `[timeframe]`,
`[belief]`, `[audience]`, `[role]`, `[expensive thing]`.

> **Reel translation:** these patterns were validated as YouTube titles, where a thumbnail shares
> the load. On a reel there's no title — the hook is **frame-1 on-screen text + first words**. Keep
> each one short enough to burn as a caption and say in one breath. Cross-ref the 4 Hook Killers in
> `_shared/references/hook-diagnostics.md`.

## Generic fill-in examples (niche-agnostic)
- **#1 Autonomy:** "This one system handles my entire [workflow] while I'm offline."
- **#2 Negative kill:** "[Tool] just killed the [expensive thing] you've been paying for — here's proof."
- **#3 Number+timeframe:** "How I'd fix [pain] in [number] days if I had to start over."
- **#6 Contrarian:** "The [outcome] method nobody's talking about — and it's the only one that worked."
- **#9 Equation:** "[Free tool] + [thing your audience already uses] = [the result they want]."
- **#11 Time-compression:** "Everything that actually matters about [topic], in under [X] minutes."

## What the winners confirm (use as a sanity check)
- **Negative frames win biggest** — the category-kill / loss frame (#2, #10) repeatedly out-performs the
  positive version. Reach for it first. (Reinforces Negative-Frames in `story-locks.md`.)
- **Specificity beats vagueness** — every winner carries a number, a named thing, or a concrete outcome
  (the curiosity-gap Goldilocks zone). No vague "[topic] tips" hook ever wins.
- **Pre-sell the payoff** — "here's proof / real test / results" (#5, #7) promises a delayed reward, which
  is exactly what the body should then deliver. Don't promise a payoff the body can't pay off.
- **Sell the outcome, not the feature** — "while I sleep" (#1), "$ outcome" (#12) headline the *benefit*.
  Lead with what the viewer gets, not how the mechanism works.

> **Adaptation rule:** steal the *format*, translate the *topic* to your audience's world. An equation or
> "killed X" shell is niche-agnostic; the nouns you drop in must be the outcome and the pain *your*
> viewer actually cares about — not a borrowed example from another niche.
