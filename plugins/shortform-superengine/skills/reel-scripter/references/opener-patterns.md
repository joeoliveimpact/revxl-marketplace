# Opener Patterns — first line / first 3 seconds

Niche-agnostic opener templates, organized by the hook buckets the analysis detects
(`question`, `numbered/list`, `myth-bust/negation`, `contrarian/curiosity`,
`pain-callout`, `personal/story`, `statement`). Map an analysis result → the matching
bucket → pick an opener. Each template names the **Hook Killer it avoids** and why it
earns the next 3 seconds. Placeholders: `[pain]`, `[outcome]`, `[mechanism]`, `[timeframe]`,
`[subject]`, `[number]`, `[belief]`, `[audience]`.

> The job of the opener is ONE thing: buy the next 3 seconds. Subject first, tension present,
> spoken to "you." See `say-this-not-that.md` for line-level rewrites and
> `_shared/references/hook-diagnostics.md` for the 4 Killers (DELAY, CONFUSION, IRRELEVANCE, DISINTEREST).

---

## HOOK → PROMISE → MICRO-INTRO (the opener structure)

The bucket templates below are the **Hook** itself. This is the structure that *wraps* it. Across a
counted corpus of top performers, the winning open is unanimous — and the loser move (name first) is
the single most common amateur retention-killer:

| Beat | Timing | What it does | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. HOOK** | first phrase | One open loop, stated to "you." | **Name never first.** No greeting, no "today we're talking about." Open ON the first interesting word. |
| **2. PROMISE** | right after | "Here's exactly what you'll walk away able to do — even if [obstacle]." | One concrete payoff. Disarm the viewer's biggest objection here. |
| **3. MICRO-INTRO** | only after 1+2 | "By the way, if you don't know me, I'm [name] — [one proof claim]." | **≤6 seconds, one sentence**, fused to credibility (not a bare name). Signpost it ("by the way…") so it reads as a quick aside, not a bio. |
| **4. CONTENT** | then | "Alright, let's get into it." | Straight into the body. |

- **Why name-never-first:** every counted top performer puts the hook + promise *before* the name;
  zero opened with their name. Leading with "hi, I'm…" buries the hook and loses the scroll.
- **The micro-intro is a credibility stamp, not a greeting** — it's only worth the airtime if the name
  is glued to a proof claim the audience cares about. A bare "my name is [name]" earns nothing.
- **It's optional but high-value for a launching account** — a new viewer has no reason to trust you yet,
  so the proof line is where you earn it. Skip it only if your audience already knows you.

> **IG / Reels note:** on Instagram there is **no title and no thumbnail** — the hook IS the
> **frame-1 on-screen text**. The first spoken (or written) words must double as **burned-in caption**,
> readable in a silent autoplay scroll. Write the hook so it works as text-on-screen at frame 1, because
> that text is doing the entire job a YouTube title+thumbnail would do. See the per-platform attention
> deadline table in `_shared/references/hook-diagnostics.md` (Reel = ~2s cliff).

## question
| Template | Avoids | Why it earns 3s |
|---|---|---|
| "Why is [subject] still [pain] even when you [right action]?" | DISINTEREST | Names a contradiction the viewer is living — opens a loop. |
| "What's the real reason [audience] can't [outcome]?" | IRRELEVANCE | "You/your-audience" framing; promises a hidden cause. |
| "Want to know the fastest way to [outcome]?" | DELAY | Subject is in the first 6 words; question demands an answer. |

## numbered/list
| Template | Avoids | Why it earns 3s |
|---|---|---|
| "[number] [things] that quietly [cause pain] — #[k] surprised me." | DISINTEREST | Finite promise + a tease on one item creates a curiosity gap. |
| "[number] ways to [outcome] without [common sacrifice]." | IRRELEVANCE | Frames the payoff around the viewer's resistance, not the topic. |
| "The [number] [subject] mistakes costing you [outcome]." | DELAY | Subject + stakes land instantly; "you" makes it personal. |

## myth-bust/negation
| Template | Avoids | Why it earns 3s |
|---|---|---|
| "Stop [common action] — it's why you're still [pain]." | DELAY | Command + subject in first 4 words; implies a correction coming. |
| "[belief] is wrong. Here's what actually [drives outcome]." | DISINTEREST | A-vs-B tension stated outright; promises the "actually." |
| "You don't [pain] because of [scapegoat]. You [pain] because of [mechanism]." | CONFUSION | One clean reframe, plain words, reassigns blame fast. |

## contrarian/curiosity
| Template | Avoids | Why it earns 3s |
|---|---|---|
| "Nobody talks about [subject] — so I will." | DISINTEREST | Forbidden-knowledge framing; positions you as the one who'll tell. |
| "Everyone says [belief]. The opposite got me [outcome]." | DISINTEREST | Direct contradiction of consensus = unresolved tension. |
| "The real reason [outcome] happens has nothing to do with [obvious cause]." | IRRELEVANCE | Redirects from what they expect to a payoff that feels for-them. |

## pain-callout
| Template | Avoids | Why it earns 3s |
|---|---|---|
| "If you're [pain] and [pain], this is for you." | IRRELEVANCE | Mirrors the viewer's exact state — "this is for me" lands in <2s. |
| "Tired of [pain] no matter what you try?" | IRRELEVANCE | Names felt frustration + the "tried everything" loop. |
| "[audience]: the reason you're [pain] isn't what you think." | DISINTEREST | Calls the segment, then opens a gap on the cause. |

## personal/story
| Template | Avoids | Why it earns 3s |
|---|---|---|
| "[timeframe] ago I was [pain]. Today I [outcome]. Here's what changed." | DISINTEREST | Before/after gap + an explicit promise to reveal the bridge. |
| "I almost [bad outcome]. One [mechanism] saved it." | DISINTEREST | In-medias-res stakes; the rescue is withheld. |
| "Let me tell you how I accidentally [outcome]." | DELAY | "Accidentally [outcome]" front-loads payoff; story earns the wind-up. |

## statement
| Template | Avoids | Why it earns 3s |
|---|---|---|
| "This is why you're [pain] even though you [right action]." | DELAY | Subject + promise of cause in the first phrase. |
| "[outcome] comes down to one thing most [audience] ignore." | DISINTEREST | Reduces to a single overlooked lever — a gap worth staying for. |
| "Here's the [mechanism] that finally [outcome] for me." | IRRELEVANCE | Concrete mechanism > vague topic; implies transferable result. |

## Openers that reliably lose (mapped to the 4 Killers)

| Losing opener | Killer | Why it dies | Fix → |
|---|---|---|---|
| "Okay so… let's talk about [topic]." | DELAY | Throat-clear + topic-announcement; subject arrives after the cliff. | Open ON the first interesting word. |
| "So I've been thinking about this for a while…" | DELAY | Pure preamble; zero subject in second 1. | Delete it; lead with the claim. |
| "Hey guys, welcome back to my page…" | DELAY | Greeting burns the attention window. | Cut the greeting entirely. |
| "I'm gonna try not to ramble, but…" | DELAY / DISINTEREST | Hedge lowers stakes and signals filler. | State the payoff with confidence. |
| "[One long passive multi-clause sentence that buries the point]." | CONFUSION | Needs a re-read; viewer scrolls instead. | One idea, active voice, plain words. |
| "Today I want to share my experience with [topic]." | IRRELEVANCE | Creator-POV; viewer hasn't been told why it's theirs. | Reframe to "you" + the pain. |
| "[Topic] is really important and something I care about." | DISINTEREST | True but tensionless — no open loop. | Add an A-vs-B contrast. |
| "Let me give you a little background first." | DELAY | Explicitly defers the payoff. | Skip background; deliver, backfill later. |

**One-line test:** if the first 5 spoken/on-screen words don't name the subject AND a tension or a "you," the opener is leaking one of the four Killers — rewrite before scripting the body.
