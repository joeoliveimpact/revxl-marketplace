# CTA Scaffolds — reel calls-to-action by goal

Niche-agnostic CTA patterns, organized by GOAL. Pick the CTA that matches the reel's body
(see `body-structures.md`) and the creator's objective. Each entry gives the scaffold, when to
use it, one generic fill-in example, placement, and the one mistake that kills it.

Placeholders: `[keyword]`, `[topic]`, `[outcome]`, `[resource]`, `[someone]`, `[series]`,
`[offer]`, `[link]`.

> One CTA per reel. Two asks split intent and tank both. State it plainly — the algorithm and
> the viewer both reward a single clear next action.

---

## 1. Comment-to-trigger (keyword → DM → capture)
**Scaffold:** "Comment **[keyword]** and I'll send you [resource]."
**When:** you have a lead magnet / deeper resource and want comments (ranking signal) + DMs (list/warm leads). Best on how-to, listicle, and myth-bust bodies.
**Example:** "Comment **START** and I'll DM you the full checklist."
**Placement:** END is standard; a mid-reel *tease* ("the template's free — I'll tell you how to grab it in a sec") lifts completion. Make the keyword short, ONE word, easy to spell.
**The mistake that kills it:** asking for a comment with no reason to comment — "let me know your thoughts" gets nothing. Tie the comment to a concrete reward.

**The full IG mechanic — keyword → DM-in-<2s → capture (this is the high-converter):**
The reason this CTA beats every other on Instagram is the in-app conversational funnel:
> **comment [keyword] → automated DM fires in <2s → capture the email IN the chat → deliver [resource] + next step**
- **Why it wins:** an in-DM funnel runs **~85% completion / 70%+ email opt-in**, versus **~25%** for a "link in bio → landing-page form." Keeping the whole flow inside the app — never sending them to a form — is the lift. Ask one qualifying question in-DM ("just starting or already doing [thing]?") to segment leads.
- **Make the keyword one short, easy-to-spell word** (`START`, `GUIDE`, `[topic]`). The viewer has to type it exactly for the trigger to fire.
- **Compliance caution (do not skip):** run this on a **Meta-approved tool** (Graph-API / OAuth — e.g. ManyChat, CreatorFlow), **never** a browser/extension bot that simulates a login (ban risk). Respect the rules: ~**1 automated DM per user per 24h** for comment triggers, a **rolling rate limit** (keep automated DMs well under the cap so viral posts queue instead of failing), and the **24-hour window** — a comment/DM opens 24h of follow-up messaging, then closes. If targeting the EU, add consent language + a "STOP" opt-out.

## 2. Save-for-later
**Scaffold:** "Save this so you can [use it when [trigger moment]]."
**When:** dense reference value — steps, lists, checklists, "do this not that." Saves are a strong reach signal and the body must be worth re-watching.
**Example:** "Save this for the next time you [face the pain]."
**Placement:** END, after the value is delivered. A light mid-reel nudge ("you'll want to save this part") works when a key list hits.
**The mistake that kills it:** saying "save this" on a reel with nothing to come back to. If it's a story or a one-time watch, save is the wrong CTA — use share or follow.

## 3. Share-to-someone
**Scaffold:** "Send this to [someone] who [needs it / is dealing with [pain]]."
**When:** relatable, emotional, or validating content. Shares = the highest-reach signal; the reel must make the viewer think of a specific person.
**Example:** "Tag a [person] who needs to hear this today."
**Placement:** END. Name a *specific* recipient ("the friend who always says [thing]") — vague "share with friends" underperforms a named target.
**The mistake that kills it:** asking for a share on content that's about the creator, not the viewer's world — there's no one to send it to. Make it about a shared experience first.

## 4. Follow-for-series / part-2
**Scaffold:** "Follow for **Part [2]** where I show you [next outcome]."
**When:** the body opens a loop you deliberately don't close — multi-part teaching, a cliffhanger result, a "this is step 1 of 3."
**Example:** "Follow so you don't miss Part 2 — the [mechanism] that makes this work."
**Placement:** END, paired with an explicit open loop earlier in the body ("there's a second half to this — I'll cover it next"). The promise must be specific.
**The mistake that kills it:** "follow for more" with no named payoff. Generic "more" gives no reason to follow *now*. Promise the specific next thing.

## 5. Link-in-bio
**Scaffold:** "Full [resource] is linked in my bio — go grab it."
**When:** the destination is genuinely worth leaving the app for (free guide, waitlist, full breakdown). Lowest-friction-to-highest-friction CTA; expect drop-off.
**Example:** "The complete [resource] is in my bio link."
**Placement:** END. Reduce friction: name exactly what's there and why. Pair with on-screen text "🔗 in bio" since spoken links are easy to miss.
**The mistake that kills it:** sending people off-platform too early or for something thin — Instagram suppresses reels that pull users out, and a weak landing page burns trust. Earn the click with the body first.

## 6. Book / DM-me
**Scaffold:** "If you want [outcome], DM me **[keyword]** and we'll [next step]."
**When:** higher-intent offer (call, audit, program). Use sparingly — overt selling caps reach. Best after a body that proves competence.
**Example:** "Want this done for you? DM me **[keyword]** to see if it's a fit."
**Placement:** END only. Lead with the value, ask last. A single, low-pressure line converts better than a hard pitch.
**The mistake that kills it:** pitching before earning it — a sales CTA on a reel that didn't first deliver real value reads as spam and tanks both reach and trust.

## Choosing the right CTA (fast map)
| Body type | Best-fit CTA |
|---|---|
| Listicle / how-to / steps | Save-for-later · Comment-to-trigger |
| Myth-bust / contrarian | Comment-to-trigger · Follow-for-part-2 |
| Story / personal | Share-to-someone · Follow |
| Result / transformation | Follow-for-series · Book/DM-me |
| Deep teaching w/ resource | Comment-to-trigger · Link-in-bio |

## Universal CTA rules
- **One ask per reel.** Stacking "save AND share AND follow" splits attention and gets none.
- **Make the action effortless.** One short keyword, a named recipient, an exact bio item.
- **Always give a reason.** "Save this *because…*", "comment X *to get…*" — the reason is the CTA.
- **Match energy to the body.** Earn the higher-friction asks (link, DM) with value first.
- **Mid-reel tease > end-only beg.** Seed the payoff early; the explicit ask lands at the end.
