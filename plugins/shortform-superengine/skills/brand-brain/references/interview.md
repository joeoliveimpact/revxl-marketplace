# Task — interview (tier-D floor)

Runs when the ladder is empty through tier C. Always available; produces a REAL brain, conservatively stamped.

## 1. Guided voice interview
Short, spoken-feel Q&A (adapt count to the user's patience, ~6-10):
- How would a friend describe the way you talk?
- Words/phrases you use constantly? Words you'd NEVER use?
- Say your core belief about your niche out loud — type it as you'd SAY it.
- Who's your enemy (the approach/industry habit you're against)?
- A story you tell over and over? An analogy you always reach for?
- What do clients laugh at when you say it?
- Edge check: vanilla / conversational / spicy / locker-room — where do you sit?

## 2. Offer/avatar interview
Who exactly do you help (their words, not marketing-speak)? What have they tried already? What do they say on calls or in DMs when they first reach out? What's the offer, price point, promise?

## 3. Write the brain
Same artifacts per ${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/brand-brain/references/artifacts.md, with:
- `voice_confidence: interview` — consumers stay conservative; the brain must not over-claim a voice it hasn't earned.
- VoC bank entries tagged `source: interview` (the coach's recollection of prospect language, not verbatim capture).
- Signature bits ONLY if the user volunteers real ones (their analogy, their joke). Never invent.
