# Call-Time Blueprint Template (Live Mode)

The compressed, scannable reference the closer keeps open DURING the call. One screen where possible. A distillation of the Pre-Call Prep — not a second analysis. Output file naming: `[Prospect Full Name] - LIVE - [MM.DD.YY].md`.

> Built by compressing the deep doc to only what's glanceable mid-call: who they are, the landmines, the exact lines, the price, the top objections, the 3 things. If both modes are requested, generate this FROM the Pre-Call Prep so they stay consistent. `{{curly}}` = config; `[square]` = from DMs.

```template
# [PROSPECT NAME] — LIVE CARD · [Strategy|Triage] · [caller]

**Pain (their words):** "[exact phrase]"  ·  **Close prob:** [H/M/L]  ·  **Top risk:** [one thing]

**🔴 LANDMINES:** [partner? currency? savings gap? — 2-4 max, terse]

---
**RAPPORT:** "[one opening line]"  → collapse silence
**FRAME:** "[one-line frame]"
**PAIN:** "[the one opening pain question]"

**DISCOVERY — hit in this order:**
1. [topic] — [the one question]
2. [topic] — [the one question]
3. [topic] — [the one question]
4. [topic] — [the one question]
5. [topic] — [the one question]
*(confirmed by triage: [list] — deepen, don't re-ask)*

**TEMP CHECK:** "1–10, how you feeling about this?" → wait for 9–10
**PITCH PILLARS:** 1) [pillar]  2) [pillar]  3) [pillar]
**PRICE:** "[your price drop — you supply the number]" → 🤐 STOP TALKING

---
**OBJECTIONS (likely):**
- "[objection]" → [one-line reframe]
- "[objection]" → [one-line reframe]
- Partner → defuse in discovery, not at close

**CLOSE:** [primary close, one line]   ·   stall → [backup]

**⭐ REMEMBER 3:**
1. [core insight]
2. mirror: "[exact phrase]"
3. emotional trigger: [the close anchor]
```

## Field Documentation
| Field | Source | Notes |
|-------|--------|-------|
| Pain (their words) | Snapshot, verbatim | The single most-mirrored line |
| LANDMINES | Flag Check, compressed | 2-4 max — this is a glance, not a read |
| Discovery 1–5 | Top 5 of the ranked 10 | Drop the lower-priority topics for the live card |
| PRICE line | your own pricing | Skill stores no prices — you supply the figure live |
| REMEMBER 3 | Critical Success Factors | Verbatim from the deep doc |

## Section Specifications
- **One screen:** If it scrolls more than a screen or two, it's failing its job. Cut to the top 5 discovery topics and ≤3 objections.
- **Lines, not paragraphs:** Every entry is a phrase the closer can read at a glance while talking.
- **Consistency:** When both modes are produced, derive this from the Pre-Call Prep so the price, pillars, and "remember 3" match exactly.
- **No analysis:** This card carries decisions, not reasoning. The "why" lives in the deep doc.
