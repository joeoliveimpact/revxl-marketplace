# Framework: The RFPDP Method

<purpose>
The call structure for a full strategy / closing call: **Rapport → Frame → Pain → Discovery → Pitch.** This is the backbone of every strategy blueprint. Distilled from Engine For Impact's RFPDP Method doc.
</purpose>

## Core Concepts

### R — RAPPORT (< 30 seconds)
Quickly connect. NOT to become best friends — too much rapport diminishes authoritative leadership. The prospect is here selfishly to solve their problem; they don't care about you yet.
- Questions: *"How's your week going so far?"* · *"Where are you located?"* (tells you timezone + currency).
- **Collapse the gap of silence.** Immediately after they answer, fill the silence so they don't reflexively ask it back to you.
- Tonality: neutral but approachable. Start neutral, then move to match their energy.

### F — FRAME (< 30 seconds)
Take leadership. Tell them exactly what happens over the next 30–60 min. Instill confidence their time won't be wasted.
- Script spine: *"So ___ here's what I want to do. I have your notes in front of me but I have some additional questions, I want to dive deeper, ultimately I just want to see if we can help you. If everything looks good I'm happy to walk you through strategy, investment, ROI, all the nitty gritty. And if it's not a fit, at minimum I can point you in the right direction. Cool?"*
- Must be rattled off from memory — stumbling here kills confidence.
- **NEVER say "so here's how these calls typically go."** People love customization.

### P — PAIN (10 seconds)
Get them to name why they're here, in their own words.
- *"So what's going on in terms of ___ that's got you reaching out for a little help?"*
- *"I have the notes, but I'd love to hear from you directly — what's got you on the call today?"*

### D — DISCOVERY (12–25 minutes)
Uncover specifics and handle ALL objections **before** the pitch.
**Rules:** never pitch during discovery · quantify to absolute numbers (never let them speak in relative terms) · it's a Q&A unless an objection surfaces, then handle it proactively.

**The 10 topics that MUST be covered** (rank by THIS prospect; Pain first, Trust last):
1. **Pain** (FIRST) — what's not working? *"What do you mean by that specifically? How long has it been going on? Why reach out NOW?"*
2. **Timing** — *"Why now? Was there an 'enough is enough' moment?"*
3. **Finances / Resourcefulness** — *"If you found a solution, what's your budget look like?"* If tight: *"the real issue isn't lack of finances, it's lack of resourcefulness — are you willing to let me help you get resourceful?"*
4. **Partner** — if they say "we": *"Who's we? Do they need to be involved in the decision? Should we get them on the call?"* Handle here, NOT at the close.
5. **Current Situation** — quantify last 3 months. *"Do you have a proven, predictable process, or is it hope-and-pray?"*
6. **Goals** — *"Where do you want to be in the next {{PROGRAM_LENGTH}}? Why is that important? What would achieving it allow you to do?"*
7. **Past** — *"Have you hired a coach/program before? What did you like / not like? What do you wish you'd asked?"*
8. **Doubt** — *"You sound clear on what you want — what's stopped you from achieving it on your own?"*
9. **Comparison** — *"Have you looked at anyone else? What are you looking for specifically?"*
10. **Trust** (LAST) — *"Have you been following me a while, or am I newer on your radar?"*

### P — PITCH (10–15 minutes): Pillars → Delivery → Price Drop
Tie the offer directly back to their pain and current situation. Show light at the end of the tunnel via your vehicle.
**Rules:** clarity on deliverables/price · present only ONE offer · check in constantly · move slowly · watch verbal + non-verbal cues · NO graphics/slides · CUSTOM.
- **Temperature check FIRST** — cannot proceed to delivery/price until they're 100% bought in: *"On a scale of 1–10, how are you feeling about this strategy?"* Wait for a 9–10.
- **Pillars** — 2–4 selling points, simple to digest, that leave them feeling they CAN'T do it without you.
- **Delivery** — *"Day 1 we do X, by week 2 Y, by month 3 Z..."* BREVITY. Do NOT detail group-call times, headcount, or hours of content — you'll seed objections.
- **Price Drop** — *"Any questions before I jump into the numbers?"* then state your investment and **STOP TALKING**. Pricing is entirely yours: the skill stores no prices and prescribes no discount, payment-plan, or pay-in-full tactics. How you present the number — and any discounts or terms — is your call. The only mechanic the skill keeps is the behavioral one: drop it, then go quiet.

## Integrating Triage Notes
If triage notes accompany the DMs, that's **confirmed discovery data**. Do NOT regenerate questions for things already answered — note the confirmed data point and build a *deeper follow-up* instead. (e.g., triage confirmed revenue → skip "what's your revenue," ask "you said $4k/mo — what's the ceiling stopping you scaling past it?")

## Anti-Patterns
- ❌ Pitching during discovery → kills trust and surfaces objections early
- ❌ Accepting relative answers ("not enough," "pretty slow") → always get absolute numbers
- ❌ Detailing delivery logistics → seeds objections
- ❌ Talking after the price drop → the first to speak loses
- ❌ Saving the partner question for the close → it parks the deal at "I need to talk to them"
