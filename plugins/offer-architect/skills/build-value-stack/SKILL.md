# offer-architect:build-value-stack

Apply Hormozi's $100M Offers framework — full construction, not just an inventory.

## Step 0 — Read inputs

- Coach Profile
- Market Research
- Feasibility Scorecard (use the **recommended primary** positioning as the anchor for this stack)
- Reference (primary): `references/kb/00-master-protocol.md` (orchestration) + `kb/01-value-equation.md`, `kb/03-trim-and-stack.md`, `kb/04-scarcity-urgency-bonuses.md`, `kb/05-guarantees.md`, `kb/06-naming.md`
- Reference (quick): `references/hormozi-100m-offers-summary.md`

## Step 1 — Value Equation diagnostic

Score the current/proposed offer 0-1 on each of the four drivers, with explicit rationale and an upgrade move for each:

1. **Dream Outcome** (↑) — frame in status terms, viewed from others
2. **Perceived Likelihood of Achievement** (↑) — proof, social proof, expert positioning, guarantees
3. **Time Delay** (↓) — engineer short-term wins; show the first 30 days
4. **Effort & Sacrifice** (↓) — async-first, decisions-made-for-you, done-for-you

Composite: target ≥ 3.0 / 4.0 total. If lower, list which driver to engineer next.

## Step 2 — Trim & Stack inventory

For each significant client problem on the path to the dream outcome, list:
- The problem (in the client's own words)
- Your solution / deliverable
- Perceived market value (anchored to retail or comparable retainer rates)
- Source of the anchor

Target: 10-15 line items. Total perceived value should be 4-7× the actual offer price.

## Step 3 — Bonuses (3-5)

Each bonus:
- Solves a specific objection or fear
- Has an anchored perceived value
- Costs you minimally to deliver
- Has a punchy name

Common patterns:
- Welcome / starter kit
- Spouse / partner brief
- Annual re-test voucher (drives retention)
- Resource library (asynchronous)
- Retreat / community priority access (if applicable)

## Step 4 — Guarantees (stack 3 narrow ones, not 1 unconditional)

- **Performance guarantee** (adherence-gated, conditional, defensible)
- **Format / fit guarantee** (early-program adjustability)
- **Decision-maker veto** guarantee (spouse / doctor pause clause)

Never offer an unconditional money-back at premium price points (Hormozi: refund magnet). Document in the doc why the choice was made.

## Step 5 — Scarcity & urgency

- Scarcity (real): cohort size cap, application-only, retreat seat caps
- Urgency (one mechanism only, honest): founding-cohort pricing with documented trigger to raise price

Reject phony countdowns and limited-time bonuses for premium offers.

## Step 6 — MAGIC naming candidates (3-5)

Apply the MAGIC framework:
- **M**ake a magnetic reason why
- **A**nnounce your avatar
- **G**ive them a goal
- **I**ndicate a time interval
- **C**omplete with a container word

Generate 3-5 candidates. Score each against the framework. Recommend one. Internal tier name stays separate from external offer name.

## Step 7 — Maintenance tier

Define what happens after the core program ends:
- Maintenance price (typically 20-35% of monthly equivalent of hero tier)
- What's included (downgraded async access, quarterly check-in, hub access)
- Upgrade path (renewal into Elite or extension)
- Target retention rate (40-60% of completers)

## Step 8 — "Coach Reviews" thoroughness layer

List explicitly what the coach personally reviews per client, in 10-15 categories. Examples:
- Biochemistry / labs
- Body composition
- Cardiovascular markers
- Metabolic markers
- Hormonal markers
- Recovery metrics
- Training adherence
- Nutrition logs
- Protocol adherence
- Mindset / behavior signals
- Partner / family signals
- Quarterly re-test interpretation

Add a "Weekly Reviewed report" artifact spec (one-page client-facing summary every Friday).

## Step 9 — Save

Use `templates/value-stack-template.md` (lifted from the Jared run). Save to `Clients/[Coach Name]/[Brand] Value Stack - [MM.DD.YY].md`.

## Step 10 — Confirm

Walk the coach through the stack. Total perceived value → price ratio. Flag any single component the coach wants to drop or upgrade. Update before exiting.

## Step 11 — Exit check

Before exiting, run the `build-value-stack` checklist in `references/skill-exit-checks.md`. For each item:

- **PASS** → continue
- **GAP** → surface to coach: *"[Item] is missing/weak. Want to fix it now, or defer with a note?"* If "defer", append to `tasks/findings.md` and footnote the artifact: `> ⚠️ Deferred from exit check: [item] — [reason]`
- **FAIL (hard)** → do not exit. Block until resolved.

Critical FAIL items for this skill:
- Untagged components (every component needs `[confirmed]` / `[coach-to-build]` / `[suggested-optional]`)
- Unhedged absolute outcome promises ("guaranteed X%", "decade reversal" without conditional pairing)

The exit check is the preventive layer. The capstone PSS audit will FLAG/FAIL claim drift downstream — catching it here is cheaper.

## Operating rules

- **Specificity > volume.** Better to have 12 sharp Trim & Stack items than 30 vague ones.
- **Anchor every dollar.** Every "perceived value" number needs a source — retail rate, comparable retainer, named-competitor pricing.
- **Make the coach's review work VISIBLE.** Step 8 is the moat. Most coaches do this work invisibly; competitors can't fake it.
- **Don't reuse Jared's specifics.** The template is a structure, not content. Customize every line to the current coach's niche.
