# Problem-Solution Mapping & Trim and Stack — Agent Execution Reference

> Source: $100M Offers, Ch. 9 — "Problems & Solutions" + Ch. 10 — "Trim & Stack"

## Phase 1: Problem Decomposition

### The Problem Identification Process

Every offer solves a problem. But the **real** offer architecture comes from solving ALL the problems a prospect encounters on their journey to the dream outcome.

#### Step 1: Identify the Dream Outcome
Write the single sentence: "My client wants to ____________."

#### Step 2: List Every Obstacle
Ask: "What are ALL the things that could prevent them from achieving this?"

Organize into categories:
1. **Vehicle-Related Problems** — "I don't believe THIS specific approach will work"
2. **Internal Problems** — "I don't believe I can do it personally"
3. **External Problems** — "I don't believe it will work in MY specific situation"

#### Step 3: Map Problems to the Client Journey

For each problem, identify WHEN it occurs:

| Phase | Typical Problems |
|---|---|
| **Before Purchase** | Skepticism, past failures, fear of wasting money |
| **During Onboarding** | Overwhelm, confusion, buyer's remorse |
| **During Execution** | Procrastination, technical issues, time constraints |
| **After First Results** | Plateau, losing momentum, not knowing next steps |

#### Step 4: Create Solutions for Each Problem

For each identified problem, create a **specific solution**:

```
PROBLEM: [Client fear/obstacle]
SOLUTION: [Specific deliverable that eliminates this problem]
FORMAT: [How it's delivered — video, template, call, tool, checklist]
```

> **Hormozi Law**: "Each solution becomes a line item in your offer stack."

## Phase 2: The Trim & Stack Process

### The Delivery Cube

Every solution can be delivered across multiple dimensions. Map each solution:

#### Dimension 1: What Level of Personal Attention
| Level | Description | Value Perception | Scalability |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1-on-1** | Private, personal delivery | Highest | Lowest |
| **Small Group** | Cohort of 5-20 | High | Medium |
| **One-to-Many** | Webinar, course, broadcast | Medium | High |
| **Self-Paced** | Pre-recorded, automated | Lower | Highest |

#### Dimension 2: What Medium of Delivery
- Live / Recorded
- In-person / Virtual
- Written / Audio / Video
- Synchronous / Asynchronous

#### Dimension 3: What Support Structure
- Community access
- Direct messaging / Voxer
- Office hours
- Email support
- Dedicated account manager

---

### The Trim Process

Once you have your full list of solutions, **trim ruthlessly**:

#### Keep IF:
- High value, low cost to deliver
- The client cannot easily get this elsewhere
- Directly impacts one of the 4 Value Equation variables
- Creates a measurable result

#### Cut IF:
- High cost to deliver, low perceived value
- Client could Google this and find it free
- Doesn't directly connect to dream outcome
- Creates operational complexity without proportional value

> **Key Principle**: "Charge for the what and the why, give away the how."

### The Stack Process

Now bundle the trimmed solutions into a cohesive offer:

#### Stack Architecture
```
CORE OFFER
├── Primary Deliverable (the main thing)
├── Supporting Deliverable 1 (removes obstacle #1)
├── Supporting Deliverable 2 (removes obstacle #2)
├── ...
BONUSES (presented separately — see 04-bonuses.md)
├── Bonus 1 (high perceived value, low cost)
├── Bonus 2 (addresses remaining objection)
├── ...
GUARANTEE (see 05-guarantees.md)
└── Risk reversal mechanism
```

## Agent Execution: Offer Stack Builder

### Input Required from Client
1. Dream outcome (1 sentence)
2. Target avatar (who specifically)
3. Current delivery method (what they do now)
4. Known objections (what prospects say when they don't buy)

### Execution Steps

#### Step 1: Problem Brainstorm
Generate 15-25 problems the prospect faces. Use these prompt categories:
- "What would stop someone from starting?"
- "What would stop someone from continuing?"
- "What would stop someone from finishing?"
- "What would stop someone from believing it works?"
- "What external factors could derail them?"

#### Step 2: Solution Mapping
For each problem, generate 1-3 possible solutions. Rate each:
- **Value to client** (1-10)
- **Cost to deliver** (1-10, where 10 = expensive)
- **Uniqueness** (1-10)

Keep solutions where: Value ≥ 7 AND (Value - Cost) ≥ 4

#### Step 3: Delivery Optimization
For each kept solution, identify the optimal delivery format using the Delivery Cube. Optimize for:
- Highest perceived value at lowest actual cost
- Scalability without quality degradation
- "Done-for-you" > "Done-with-you" > "Do-it-yourself" (when cost-feasible)

#### Step 4: Stack Assembly
Arrange the final stack:
1. **Lead with the core promise** — the main deliverable
2. **Layer supporting elements** — each one removes a specific objection
3. **Separate out bonuses** — pull the most impressive low-cost items into the bonus category
4. **Assign values** — give each item a standalone dollar value
5. **Sum total value** — should be 10x-100x the asking price

#### Step 5: Value Equation Cross-Check
Run the assembled stack through the Value Equation audit:
- Does the stack increase Dream Outcome? ✅
- Does the stack increase Perceived Likelihood? ✅
- Does the stack decrease Time Delay? ✅
- Does the stack decrease Effort & Sacrifice? ✅

If any variable isn't addressed, add a solution that targets it.

## Pricing the Stack

> **Hormozi Law**: "Price is not based on cost. Price is based on value."

### Pricing Decision Framework
- **Floor**: Cost to deliver × 3 (minimum viable margin)
- **Target**: Where the price-to-value gap is so large the prospect feels stupid saying no
- **Ceiling**: The point where Perceived Likelihood starts dropping ("this seems too expensive to be real")

### The 10x Rule
> "The value of the offer should be perceived as AT LEAST 10x the price."

If your offer is $1,000, the prospect should feel they're getting $10,000+ in value.
