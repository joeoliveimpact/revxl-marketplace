# Scarcity, Urgency & Bonuses — Agent Execution Reference

> Source: $100M Offers, Ch. 11-14 — "Enhancing the Offer"

## Core Principle

> "The products remained unchanged, yet within this setting, an item that wouldn't sell at a different venue for $10,000 sold for $100,000. That's how powerful scarcity, urgency, and bonuses are."

These enhancers do NOT change your offer. They change the **psychology of how it's perceived**.

### Supply-Demand Psychology
- People want what they can't have
- People want what other people want
- People want things only a select few have access to
- Fear of loss is stronger than desire for gain

> **Hormozi Law**: "The longer you delay the ask, the bigger the ask you can make. The longer the runway, the bigger the plane that can take off."

## SCARCITY (Function of Quantity)

Scarcity = limiting HOW MANY can buy.

### Three Types of Scarcity

#### 1. Limited Supply of Seats/Slots
- "Only accepting X clients at this level of service"
- Can be per period or per total

**Sub-types**:
| Type | Example | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| **Total Business Cap** | "We only service 25 clients. Period." | Highest tier services |
| **Growth Rate Cap** | "We only accept 5 new clients per week" | Ongoing enrollment |
| **Cohort Cap** | "We take 100 clients 4x per year" | Batch-start programs |

#### 2. Limited Supply of Bonuses
- "First 10 buyers get X bonus"
- Creates urgency WITHIN the scarcity

#### 3. Never Available Again
- "This specific version will never be offered again"
- Limited edition releases

### Scarcity Rules

1. **Always sell out** — better to under-supply than over-supply
2. **Announce the sellout** — "We sold out" is social proof
3. **Compound over time** — each sellout makes the next launch more powerful
4. **Be honest** — "Honest scarcity" = define your actual capacity and advertise it
5. **Once you're out, you can never come back** — powerful for small groups

### Scarcity for Services (Agent Decision Matrix)

```
IF high-ticket 1-on-1 → Total Business Cap
IF ongoing enrollment → Growth Rate Cap  
IF batch/cohort model → Cohort Cap
IF digital product → Limited Release / Limited Bonuses
IF physical product → Limited Edition / Limited Quantity
```

### Honest Scarcity Script
> "We're at [X]% capacity this [period]. We can handle [Y] more [clients/projects] before we're full. Once we hit capacity, the next opening won't be until [date]."

## URGENCY (Function of Time)

Urgency = limiting WHEN they can buy.

### Four Types of Urgency

#### 1. Cohort-Based Rolling Urgency
> "If you sign up today, I can get you in with our next group that kicks off Monday. Otherwise you'll have to wait until the next kickoff date."

- Works for any recurring start dates
- Less frequent starts = more powerful urgency
- **Key stat**: Last 3% of campaign time generates 50-60% of sales

#### 2. Rolling Seasonal Urgency
- Rename the same promotion by season/event
- Each gets a start date and end date
- Example cycle:
  - Jan: "New Year Transformation"
  - Feb: "Valentine's Lovers Promo"
  - Mar: "Spring Into Shape"
  - Apr: "Summer Body Countdown"

> **Pro Tip for local businesses**: Vary marketing wrappers more frequently than national advertisers. Same core offer, new seasonal wrapper with dates.

#### 3. Pricing or Bonus-Based Urgency
> "Let's get you started today so you can take advantage of this promotion. We change them every 4 weeks or so."

- Urgency on the PROMOTION, not the service itself
- Interchange: price discount, added bonuses, free install, extra workshops
- Maintains integrity (service is always available; the deal isn't)

**Pipeline Cleaning**: Always notify existing pipeline before a price increase.

#### 4. Exploding Opportunity
- The opportunity itself decays with time
- Market arbitrage, platform early-mover advantage
- "Every day you wait, the competitive advantage shrinks"

### Urgency Application Matrix

```
IF cohort model → Cohort-Based Rolling Urgency
IF ongoing service → Pricing/Bonus-Based Urgency  
IF seasonal business → Rolling Seasonal Urgency
IF market-timing opportunity → Exploding Opportunity
```

## BONUSES (Demand Amplifier)

> "A single offer is less valuable than the same offer broken into its component parts and stacked as bonuses."

### Core Bonus Principles

1. **Present bonuses AFTER asking for the sale** (1-on-1 selling)
   - If they say YES → bonuses create wow/reinforcement
   - If they say NO → bonuses address their specific objection, then re-ask
2. **Never discount — add bonuses instead**
   > "Adding bonuses to increase value to close the deal is far superior to cutting prices."
3. **Bonus value should ECLIPSE core offer value** — subconsciously signals core must be even more valuable

### The 11 Bonus Rules

1. Always offer them
2. Give each a special name with a benefit in the title
3. Explain: how it relates, what it is, how you created it, how it improves their life
4. Provide proof (stat, past client, personal experience)
5. Paint a vivid mental picture of life AFTER using it
6. Assign a specific price tag and justify it
7. **Tools & checklists > additional trainings** (lower effort = higher value per Value Equation)
8. Each bonus should address a specific objection
9. Solve the NEXT problem they'll encounter
10. Total bonus value should exceed core offer price
11. Add scarcity/urgency to bonuses themselves for compound effect

### Bonus Types

| Type | Example | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| **Tools & Templates** | Checklists, swipe files, scripts | High perceived value, zero marginal cost |
| **Recorded Content** | Past workshops, webinars, interviews | Asset vault building |
| **Partner Products** | Adjacent business offerings | Free to you, high value to client |
| **Time-Limited Access** | VIP service for X days | Upsell pathway |
| **Community Access** | Private group, mastermind | Retention + social proof |

### Advanced: Partner Bonus Arbitrage

1. Identify adjacent businesses that serve the same customer
2. Negotiate free products/services for your clients (= free marketing for them)
3. Optionally negotiate referral commissions
4. Result: Your bonuses cost you $0 AND generate revenue

**Example (Pain Clinic)**:
| Partner | Bonus | Value | Your Commission |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chiropractor | 2 free adjustments | $100 | $100/referral |
| Food Company | Discount code | $50 savings | Free product |
| Orthotics | Discount | $150 savings | $100/referral |
| Health Club | Free PT session + month | $100 | $50/signup |
| **Total** | | **$400+ value** | **$250+ per client** |

### Bonus Scarcity & Urgency Combos

**Bonus + Scarcity**:
> "Only people who sign up for XYZ program will have access to Bonus #1, 2, 3 — never available anywhere else."

**Bonus + Urgency**:
> "If you buy today, I'll add XYZ bonus (normally $1,000) for free. I want to reward action takers."

## Agent Execution: Enhancement Checklist

For every offer, before launch, verify:

### Scarcity Check
- [ ] Is there a defined quantity limit? (slots, seats, units)
- [ ] Is the limit honest and defensible?
- [ ] Is there a plan to announce the sellout?
- [ ] Does it compound with previous sellouts?

### Urgency Check
- [ ] Is there a defined deadline?
- [ ] Is the deadline real (not fake countdown timers)?
- [ ] Does the urgency match the business model? (cohort/seasonal/pricing/exploding)
- [ ] Is there a "reason why" for the deadline?

### Bonus Check
- [ ] Are there at least 3 bonuses?
- [ ] Does each bonus have a name, value, and justification?
- [ ] Do bonuses address the top 3 prospect objections?
- [ ] Is total bonus value > core offer price?
- [ ] Are tools/checklists prioritized over additional training?
- [ ] Are any partner bonus opportunities being exploited?
