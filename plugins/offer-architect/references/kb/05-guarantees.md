# Guarantees — Agent Execution Reference

> Source: $100M Offers, Ch. 15 — "Enhancing the Offer: Guarantees"

## Core Principle

> "The single greatest objection for any product or service being sold is... risk. Risk that it doesn't do what it's supposed to do for them."

Guarantees reverse risk. A guarantee can **2-4x conversion** without changing anything else about the offer.

### The Guarantee Math
> "Don't be emotional, just do the math."

```
Without guarantee: 100 sales × 5% refund = 95 net sales
With guarantee:    130 sales × 10% refund = 117 net sales
Net improvement:   117/95 = 1.23x (23% more revenue)
```

For a guarantee to HURT you, the increase in refunds would have to 100% offset the increase in sales. This almost never happens.

## The Four Guarantee Types

### 1. Unconditional Guarantee
**Definition**: No questions asked refund. The strongest form.
- "If you're not happy for any reason, we refund everything."
- Essentially a risk-free trial where they pay first

**Best for**: Lower-ticket B2C offers (many people won't bother requesting)

**Warning**: People who buy ONLY because of the guarantee tend to be poor customers.

**Power Script** (via Jason Fladlien):
> "I'm not asking you to decide yes or no today. I'm asking you to make a fully informed decision, that is all. The only way you can make a fully informed decision is on the inside, not the outside. Whether it's 29 minutes or 29 days from now — if you ain't happy, I ain't happy."

**Best-Case/Worst-Case Close**:
> "Best case: you get [dream outcome] and we give you all your money towards [long-term goal]. Worst case: you tell me I suck, I write you a check, and you get [duration] of free [service]. Both options are risk-free. But the only thing guaranteed NOT to help you is walking out of here today."

---

### 2. Conditional Guarantee
**Definition**: Refund or compensation IF the client meets certain conditions.

**Key Design Rule**: Conditions should be the **key actions linked with success**. This way:
- 100% who qualify have likely achieved their result (so they won't want the refund)
- It forces clients to do the work (better outcomes)
- It protects you from freeloaders

**Conditional Guarantee Types**:

| Type | What Client Gets | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| **Outsized Refund** | 2x-3x money back IF conditions met | High-margin info products |
| **Service Guarantee** | Free continued service until result achieved | Service businesses (Hormozi's favorite) |
| **Modified Service** | Additional Y period of free service | Same, but with defined time cap |
| **Credit-Based** | Store credit toward any service | Upsell situations |
| **Personal Service** | Owner works with them 1-on-1 until success | Premium positioning |
| **Hotel + Airfare** | Reimburse travel costs if no value | Workshops, events |
| **Wage Payment** | Pay their hourly rate for wasted time | Consultations, calls |
| **Release of Service** | Let them out of contract free | Contract-based businesses |
| **Delayed Second Payment** | No billing until first result achieved | Activation-focused businesses |
| **First Outcome** | Cover ancillary costs until first result | Performance businesses |

**Hormozi's #1 Favorite — The Service Guarantee**:
> "You keep working for them free of charge until X is achieved."

Why it works:
- You never refund money
- The guarantee is around the OUTCOME
- Add conditions: must respond in 24hrs, must use your products, must attend calls
- In practice: "Since I've been advising businesses to use this, not a single person has said a client took them up on it."

---

### 3. Anti-Guarantee
**Definition**: Explicitly state "ALL SALES ARE FINAL."

**When to use**:
- Products that are consumable or lose value once seen (code, scripts, formulas)
- High-ticket services with high fulfillment cost
- When you want to ATTRACT motivated, serious buyers

**Power Position**:
> "We have an 'all sales are final' policy because our product is so exclusive and so powerful that once used, it cannot be unused. We're exposing the inner workings of our business."

**Personality Version**:
> "If you're the type of customer who needs a guarantee before taking a jump, then you are not the type of person we want to work with. We want motivated self-starters."

**Key**: Must provide a compelling "reason why" — not arbitrary, but logical.

---

### 4. Implied Guarantee (Performance-Based)
**Definition**: You only get paid if you deliver results.

**Models**:
| Model | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Performance** | Pay per result | $X per sale, $X per lb lost |
| **Revshare** | % of top-line revenue | 10% of revenue, 25% of growth |
| **Profit-Share** | % of profit | X% of gross profit |
| **Ratchets** | Escalating % by milestone | 10% if >$X, 20% if >$Y |
| **Triggers/Bonuses** | Bonus upon hitting milestone | $X when Y occurs |

**Advantages**:
- Perfect alignment between client and provider
- Weeds out low performers
- Maximum trust signal

**Drawbacks**: 
- Tracking and collection challenges
- Requires transparency from client

**Hybrid**: Pair with a minimum. 
> "We get the greater of $1,000/mo or 10% of revenue generated."

> "We get $1,000/mo for the first 3 months, then switch to 100% performance."

## Guarantee Stacking

You can combine guarantees for maximum power:

**Stack Example 1**: Unconditional + Conditional
> "30-day no questions asked guarantee PLUS a conditional triple-your-money-back 90-day guarantee."

**Stack Example 2**: Sequential Conditional
> "You'll make $10,000 by 60 days, $30,000 by 90 days, as long as you do steps 1, 2, and 3."

Stacking future-paces the prospect into believing the outcome is inevitable.

## Agent Decision Tree: Guarantee Selection

```
START
├── Is fulfillment cost high?
│   ├── YES → Anti-Guarantee or Conditional
│   └── NO ↓
├── Is it a lower-ticket B2C offer?
│   ├── YES → Unconditional (most won't bother refunding)
│   └── NO ↓
├── Is it a service with measurable outcomes?
│   ├── YES → Service Guarantee (Hormozi's #1) or Performance Model
│   └── NO ↓
├── Is it a high-ticket B2B offer?
│   ├── YES → Conditional with specific conditions + consider stacking
│   └── NO ↓
├── Is the product easily copied once seen?
│   ├── YES → Anti-Guarantee with strong "reason why"
│   └── NO → Outsized Conditional or Credit-Based
```

## Guarantee Naming

> **Pro Tip**: Name your guarantee something memorable.

**Bad**: "30 Day Money Back Satisfaction Guarantee"
**Good**: "In 30 days, if you wouldn't jump into shark-infested waters to get our product back, we return every dollar."
**Great**: "Club a Baby Seal Guarantee — After 30 days, if you wouldn't club a baby seal to stay on as a customer, you don't pay a penny."

## Create Your Own Guarantee

### Process:
1. **Identify the client's #1 fear** — "What are they most afraid of if they pay you?"
2. **Reverse that fear** — turn it into a promise
3. **Add teeth** — specify the "or what" (refund, free service, pay them, etc.)
4. **Add conditions if needed** — tie to actions that drive success
5. **Name it memorably**
6. **Do the math** — will the increased conversion outweigh increased refunds?

### The Guarantee Formula
```
IF you do not get [RESULT] in [TIMEFRAME], we will [CONSEQUENCE]
```
