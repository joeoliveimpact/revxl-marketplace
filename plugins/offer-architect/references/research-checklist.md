# Market Research Checklist — what every research-market pass must cover

Used by `offer-architect:research-market`. Every cell must be either filled with a sourced answer OR explicitly noted as "data unavailable, recommend follow-up."

---

## 1. Niche definition

- [ ] Niche stated in <15 words
- [ ] ICA hypothesis (age, income, gender, family, profession, where they hang out)
- [ ] Why this ICA NOW (cultural / regulatory / economic tailwind)

## 2. Market size & trajectory

- [ ] TAM (broad industry) — $ figure + source
- [ ] SAM (specific niche) — $ figure + source (or top-down estimate with reasoning)
- [ ] CAGR / growth trajectory — % + source
- [ ] 3+ demand signals (Reddit communities, podcast volume, news coverage, search trend, etc.)

## 3. Pricing benchmarks (full ladder)

- [ ] Monthly retainer: Low / Mid / High
- [ ] 3-month container: Low / Mid / High
- [ ] 6-month container: Low / Mid / High
- [ ] 12-month container: Low / Mid / High
- [ ] VIP / DFY tier: Low / Mid / High
- [ ] Source for each row

## 4. Named competitors (3-5 minimum)

For each:
- [ ] Name + IG/YouTube/web presence
- [ ] Positioning (their tagline / stated promise)
- [ ] Pricing (if public)
- [ ] What they do well
- [ ] What they don't have (gap the coach can exploit)
- [ ] Strategic vulnerability

## 5. Regulatory landscape

- [ ] Federal regulations affecting this niche (FDA, FTC, DEA, etc.)
- [ ] State-specific exposure (especially CA, NY, TX, FL for telehealth / coaching)
- [ ] Scope-of-practice issues (when does this become practicing medicine / law / therapy?)
- [ ] Recent regulatory changes (last 12-24 months)
- [ ] Healthcare-attorney consultation recommended? Yes/No

## 6. Premium / concierge anchors

- [ ] At least 2 high-end reference brands at the upper price ceiling
- [ ] Annual / per-package price for each
- [ ] What's included (so the coach can show their offer matches inclusions at 1/3 the price)

## 7. Add-on tests / services

For each relevant add-on:
- [ ] 2026 retail range
- [ ] Who pays (client direct vs. included)
- [ ] Coach's COGS if including

Common add-ons by niche:
- Longevity / performance: DEXA, VO2 max, RMR, bloodwork, CGM, HRV monitoring, hormone panels
- Mental health adjacent: psychometric assessments, neurofeedback, sleep studies
- Aesthetic: body composition, skin assessment, hair analysis
- Performance athlete: lactate testing, force plate, sleep tracking

## 8. ICA validation evidence

- [ ] Recent (last 12 months) article / podcast / Reddit thread showing ICA is actively buying
- [ ] Spending data (e.g., affluent men 40-55 spent $X on personal care in [year])
- [ ] Behavioral evidence (search volume trends, community growth, etc.)

## 9. Alternative positionings (for feasibility scoring)

- [ ] At least 2 alternative bets (different positioning angles inside the same niche)
- [ ] Each with: TAM, defensibility, pricing power, risk

## 10. Sources

- [ ] Full URL list with titles
- [ ] Date of each source noted (recency matters in fast-moving regulatory landscapes)
- [ ] Conflicting sources flagged (where two reputable sources disagree)

---

## Quality bar

A research pass that misses 3+ items above is incomplete. Stop and re-run targeted searches before moving to find-gaps.
