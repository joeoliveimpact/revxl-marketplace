### Objectives
- Understand how creating Grand Slam Offers can save a business from bankruptcy
- Learn to launch multiple offerings simultaneously with limited resources
- Discover how to use credit cards strategically when cash is depleted
- Build a portfolio of companies across diverse industries and business types
- Develop the core skill of making irresistible offers to customers 

### Instructions
1. Develop a Grand Slam Offer using a proven methodology that combines value, urgency, and risk reversal.
2. Go all-in on your business idea by committing fully, even when resources are limited.
3. Collect cash upfront from customers rather than waiting for delayed payments.
4. Pay your team and employees on time, even when you have no money left.
5. Launch multiple offerings simultaneously to maximize revenue potential.
6. Use business credit cards strategically to fund operations when cash is depleted.
7. Track your finances daily to monitor cash flow and spending patterns.
8. Continue launching and iterating even when facing personal and financial challenges.
9. Build a portfolio of companies across different industries and business types.
10. Focus on making offers as a core skill that drives business success.
