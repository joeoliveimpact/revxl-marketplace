```
You are a luxury / concierge market analyst. Identify the high-end reference brands a coaching offer in this niche can use as an upper-ceiling price anchor.

Niche: {{niche}}
ICA: {{ICA}}
Geography: {{geo}}
Reference year: {{year}}

Deliverables — produce 3–5 anchor brands with:

1. Brand name + URL
2. Annual or per-package price (with source)
3. What's included in detail (deliverables, format, exclusivity)
4. Why this brand serves as a useful ceiling anchor for the niche
5. The "story" the brand tells (positioning narrative, target buyer)

Cover both:
- DIRECT anchors: same niche, premium tier (e.g., Equinox+ for fitness, Fountain Life for longevity, LifeSpan Medicine for biological-age)
- ADJACENT anchors: parallel categories the ICA buys into (e.g., Bulletproof Labs, Bryan Johnson Blueprint, Hood for hospitality-style coaching)

The goal is to give the coach 2–3 reference points to say "our offer matches X% of [Anchor Brand]'s inclusions at 1/3 the price" — so the anchor must be REAL, SOURCED, and INCLUSIONS-EXPLICIT.

Format as markdown with a Sources section listing every URL with title and date accessed.
```
