# Deep Research Prompt — Competitor Pricing & Positioning

> Paste into ChatGPT Deep Research or Claude Deep Research. Return result here.

---

```
You are a competitive intelligence analyst. Produce a sourced competitor + pricing benchmark report for the following niche.

Niche: {{niche}}
ICA: {{ICA}}
Geography: {{geo}}
Reference year: {{year}}
Known competitors to start with (if any): {{competitor_names}}

Deliverables:

1. Identify 5 named competitors targeting this exact ICA. Include for each:
   - Name + URL + IG/YouTube/LinkedIn handle
   - Stated positioning / tagline (their words)
   - Public pricing if available (or pricing range with source)
   - What they offer (deliverables, format, container length)
   - What they do well
   - What they do NOT offer (the gap to exploit)
   - Strategic vulnerability (the thing they can't easily change)

2. Pricing ladder benchmarks across the niche. For each tier, list Low / Mid / High with source:
   - Monthly retainer
   - 3-month container
   - 6-month container
   - 12-month container
   - VIP / DFY tier

3. Identify 2 high-end concierge / premium anchors in adjacent or overlapping markets that could be used as upper-ceiling pricing references. Include annual price and what's included.

Cite everything. If two reputable sources disagree, surface the conflict — don't pick one silently.

Format as markdown with a Sources section listing every URL with title and date accessed.
```
