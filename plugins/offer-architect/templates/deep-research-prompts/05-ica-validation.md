# Deep Research Prompt — ICA Validation (Buying Evidence)

> Paste into ChatGPT Deep Research or Claude Deep Research. Return result here.

---

```
You are a market validation analyst. Produce a sourced report demonstrating that the following Ideal Customer Avatar (ICA) is ACTIVELY BUYING in this niche right now.

Niche: {{niche}}
ICA: {{ICA}}
Geography: {{geo}}
Reference year: {{year}}

Deliverables:

1. Three or more pieces of recent (last 12 months) evidence the ICA is actively buying:
   - Articles, news features, or industry reports
   - Podcasts or YouTube creators with audiences fitting this ICA
   - Reddit threads, Discord servers, or forum discussions

2. Spending data — what does this ICA spend on this category? Provide:
   - Annual category spend per buyer (or estimate with reasoning)
   - Total category spend in the geography
   - Cite the source

3. Behavioral evidence:
   - Search volume trends for top-3 niche keywords (last 24 months)
   - Community growth rate (subreddit subscribers, FB group members, etc.)
   - Influencer/creator emergence in this niche (last 12 months)

4. Cultural / demographic tailwind — why is this ICA buying NOW (vs. 5 years ago)? Cite cultural shifts, demographic changes, or economic conditions.

5. Pain-point evidence — surface 3–5 specific, verbatim pain quotes from the ICA (Reddit posts, podcast clips, forum threads, reviews). These are the pains the offer must address.

Cite everything with URL + date accessed.

Format as markdown with a Sources section.
```
