## 5. 1-month tier policy

1-month is a filter mechanism, not a revenue mechanism. Standard policy:
- 1-month tier is deliberately excluded from the data layer (no expensive add-ons)
- After 30 days, client gets a one-time upgrade offer: 1-month price credits 100% toward 3- or 6-month commitment if upgraded within 14 days of trial end
- Converts 30-45% of trials into longer commitments
