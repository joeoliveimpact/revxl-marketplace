## Style notes
- Voice: {{Voice1}}, {{Voice2}}, {{Voice3}}
- Don't read — tell.
- Sentences short. Pause for emphasis.
- Two pauses minimum: after the dream outcome (let it land) and after "I review {{ReviewCount}} categories" (let the depth land).
- Avoid: jargon, hedging language ("might", "could potentially"), generic AI tone.
