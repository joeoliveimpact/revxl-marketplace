**Diagnose every weak hook with the 4 Hook Killers** (root cause -> fix):
- **DELAY** -- topic arrives too late. Fix: delete everything before the subject; lead with the first interesting noun.
- **CONFUSION** -- needs a re-read. Fix: fewer/simpler words, active voice, one idea per line.
- **IRRELEVANCE** -- viewer does not feel it is for them (usually "I"-framing). Fix: swap me/my -> you/your; name their pain.
- **DISINTEREST** -- clear but flat, no open question. Fix: state an A-vs-B contrast ("Most people X. Here is why that is wrong.").
- **Retention = curiosity sustained.** The #1 killer is closing the curiosity loop too early -- paying off the question before the viewer is invested. Keep at least one open loop running at all times; only resolve it once the next loop is already open.
- **Re-hook every ~20-30 seconds.** Attention decays even after a great open, so plant a fresh micro-open on a cadence -- a new question, a "but here is the thing", a pattern interrupt -- so the curve never flattens.

On a Reel the attention cliff is ~2 seconds (spoken + visual together) -- the hook must clear all four before then.

**Opener order (non-negotiable): HOOK -> PROMISE -> MICRO-INTRO.** Open on the hook, then promise the payoff ("by the end you will know exactly how to X"), and only THEN introduce yourself -- never lead with your name or a greeting. The self-intro is a <=6-second, credibility-fused line ("I am X, I have done Y N times") that earns the next 30 seconds; it comes AFTER the hook+promise have bought the attention, not before, when it just burns the opening on someone the viewer has no reason to care about yet.
